# Rosemary Salt Solution

## Ingredients
- 300 g [[Absolut vodka]]
- 15 g [[Rosemary]]
- 100 g [[Salt Solution]]

## Method
Combine the vodka and rosemary in a lidded container and shake gently. Let sit in a cool, dark place for 1 week. Strain through a fine-mesh sieve lined with several layers of cheesecloth. Transfer to a storage container, add the Salt Solution and stir or shake. Refrigerate.
