# Raisin-Infused Rye

## Ingredients
- 1 bottle (750 ml) [[Bulleit rye]]
- 150 g [[Golden Raisins]]

## Method
Fill a large basin with water and place an immersion circulator inside. Set the circulator to 140F. Put the rye and raisins in a bowl and stir to combine. Transfer to a sealable, heatproof plastic bag, seal almost completely, remove the air by dipping the bag in the water, and finish sealing. When the water reaches 140F, cook the bag for 2 hours. Transfer to an ice bath and cool to room temperature. Pass through a fine-mesh sieve lined with several layers of cheesecloth. Funnel back into the bottle and refrigerate.

## Notes
Sous vide.
