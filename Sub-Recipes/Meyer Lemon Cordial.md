# Meyer Lemon Cordial

## Ingredients
- 500 g [[Unbleached Cane Sugar]]
- 250 g strained fresh Meyer [[Lemon Juice]]
- 250 g filtered water
- 100 g [[Aylesbury Duck vodka]]
- 15 g [[Meyer Lemon]] zest
- 2 g citric acid powder
- 0.5 g kosher salt

## Method
Combine all the ingredients in a heatproof resealable bag and seal, removing the air. Cook sous vide in a water bath at 130F for 2 hours. Strain, then refrigerate.
