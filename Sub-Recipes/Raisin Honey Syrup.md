# Raisin Honey Syrup

## Ingredients
- 1 kg [[Honey Syrup]] (p45)
- 200 g [[Golden Raisins]]

## Method
Combine in a heatproof resealable bag and seal, removing the air. Cook sous vide at 145F for 2 hours. Strain, then refrigerate.
