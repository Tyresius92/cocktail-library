# Thai Chile-Infused Bourbon

## Ingredients
- 10 fresh [[Thai chiles]]
- 1 bottle (750 ml) [[Elijah Craig Small Batch bourbon]]

## Method
Halve the chiles lengthwise and scrape the seeds and membranes into a container. Add the flesh of 5 of the chiles (reserve the other 5 for another use). Add the bourbon and stir. Let stand at room temperature up to 5 minutes, tasting often to monitor the heat. Strain through a fine-mesh sieve lined with several layers of cheesecloth, funnel back into the bottle and refrigerate.

## Notes
Watch the heat; chiles vary.
