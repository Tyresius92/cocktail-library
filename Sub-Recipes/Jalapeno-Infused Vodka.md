# Jalapeno-Infused Vodka

## Ingredients
- 4 [[Jalapenos]]
- 1 (1-liter) bottle [[Grey Goose vodka]]

## Method
Halve the jalapenos lengthwise, scrape the seeds and membranes into a container, and add the flesh of 2 of them (reserve the other 2 for another use). Add the vodka and stir. Let stand at room temperature up to 20 minutes, tasting often to monitor the heat. Strain through a fine-mesh sieve lined with several layers of cheesecloth, funnel back into the bottle and refrigerate.

## Notes
Used in [[Bloody Mary Verde]]. Room-temperature infusion; watch the heat.
