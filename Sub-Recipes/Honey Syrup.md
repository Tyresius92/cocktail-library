# Honey Syrup

## Ingredients
- 540g [[Honey]] (acacia or wildflower)
- 100g warm [[Filtered Water]]

## Method
Hand mixed: whisk the honey and water in a bowl until thoroughly blended. Transfer to a storage container and refrigerate up to 2 weeks.

## Notes
Most grocery honey is clover and tends to dominate drinks; the book prefers lighter clover or, better, a light floral like acacia (favorite for general use), but good honey is getting costly and hard to source, so they've settled on generic wildflower honey. Honey is thinned to roughly 3 parts honey to 1 part water by volume so it mixes in cocktails.
