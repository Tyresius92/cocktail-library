# Vanilla Lactic Syrup

## Ingredients
- 500 g [[Simple Syrup]]
- 1 vanilla bean, split and scraped
- 0.5 g salt
- 2.5 g lactic acid powder

## Method
Combine all the ingredients in a heatproof resealable bag and seal, removing the air. Cook sous vide at 135F for 1 hour. Strain, then refrigerate.
