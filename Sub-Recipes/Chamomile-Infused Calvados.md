# Chamomile-Infused Calvados

## Ingredients
- 1 bottle (750 ml) Pierre Ferrand Ambre Calvados (as printed)
- 5 g dried chamomile

## Method
Combine in a container and steep 1 hour. Strain, funnel back into the bottle and refrigerate.

## Notes
The book prints Pierre Ferrand Ambre here; Pierre Ferrand Ambre is a Cognac, so this may be a misprint for a Calvados. Left as printed.
