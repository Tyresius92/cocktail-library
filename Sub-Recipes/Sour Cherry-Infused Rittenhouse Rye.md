# Sour Cherry-Infused Rittenhouse Rye

## Ingredients
- 1 bottle (750 ml) [[Rittenhouse rye]]
- 50 g [[Dried sour cherries]]
- [[Pectinex Ultra SP-L]]

## Method
Combine the rye and cherries in a blender and process until smooth. Strain and weigh the liquid (discard the solids). Multiply the weight by 0.002 to get X g; stir in X g Pectinex, cover and let sit 15 minutes. Divide the mixture evenly among centrifuge containers. Weigh the filled containers and adjust the liquid in each so the weights are exactly the same (important for keeping the machine in balance). Run the centrifuge at 4,500 rpm for 12 minutes. Strain through a paper coffee filter or Superbag, being careful not to disturb the solids on the container bottoms; strain again if particles remain. Funnel back into the original bottle and refrigerate.

## Notes
Centrifuge required.
