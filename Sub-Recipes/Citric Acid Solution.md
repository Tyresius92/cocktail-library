# Citric Acid Solution

## Ingredients
- 100 g [[Filtered Water]]
- 25 g [[Citric Acid]] powder

## Method
Combine in a glass bowl and stir until dissolved. Transfer to a glass dropper bottle or other glass container and refrigerate.
