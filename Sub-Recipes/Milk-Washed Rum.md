# Milk-Washed Rum

## Ingredients
- 1 (1-liter) bottle [[Flor de Cana 4-year white rum]]
- 250 ml whole milk
- 15 g [[Citric Acid Solution]] (p298)
- [[Pectinex Ultra SP-L]]

## Method
Combine the rum and milk in a container and weigh the liquid. Multiply the weight by 0.002 to get X g. Let the mixture rest 5 minutes, then stir in the citric acid solution and refrigerate at least 12 hours. Stir in X g Pectinex, cover and let sit 15 minutes. Divide the mixture evenly among centrifuge containers. Weigh the filled containers and adjust the liquid in each so the weights are exactly the same (important for keeping the machine in balance). Run the centrifuge at 4,500 rpm for 12 minutes. Strain through a paper coffee filter or Superbag, being careful not to disturb the solids on the container bottoms; strain again if particles remain. Funnel back into the original bottle and refrigerate.

## Notes
Used in [[Root Beer Float]]. Milk washing: the acid curdles the milk, which carries off tannins and color.
