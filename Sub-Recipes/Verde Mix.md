# Verde Mix

## Ingredients
- 1,200 g cucumber
- 1,000 g tomatillo juice
- 650 g green bell pepper juice
- 500 g fresh pineapple juice
- 250 g fresh [[Lime Juice]]
- 150 g [[Salt Solution]] (p298)
- 25 g serrano chile, seeds removed
- 15 g garlic

## Method
Peel, chop and puree the cucumber. In a blender, combine the puree with the juices, salt solution, serrano and garlic. Blend until smooth. Transfer to a storage container and refrigerate.

## Notes
Used in [[Bloody Mary Verde]].
