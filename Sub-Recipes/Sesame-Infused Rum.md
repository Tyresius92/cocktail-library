# Sesame-Infused Rum

## Ingredients
- 25 g [[White sesame seeds]]
- 1 bottle (750 ml) [[El Dorado 12-year rum]]

## Method
In a small skillet over medium-low heat, toast the sesame seeds, stirring often, until fragrant and slightly golden, about 4 minutes. Cool slightly. Combine with the rum in a bowl and let stand at room temperature for 5 minutes. Strain through a fine-mesh sieve lined with several layers of cheesecloth, funnel back into the rum bottle and refrigerate.
