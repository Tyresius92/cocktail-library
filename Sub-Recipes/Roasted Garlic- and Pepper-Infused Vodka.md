# Roasted Garlic- and Pepper-Infused Vodka

## Ingredients
- 1 bottle (750 ml) [[Absolut vodka]]
- 12 g [[Roasted garlic]], smashed into a paste
- 3 g [[Black peppercorns]], crushed

## Method
Combine all the ingredients in a bowl and stir. Cover and refrigerate at least 12 hours. Strain through a fine-mesh sieve lined with several layers of cheesecloth, funnel back into the vodka bottle and refrigerate.

## Notes
Used in [[Bloody Mary]] variations.
