# Toasted Almond-Infused Apricot Liqueur

## Ingredients
- 100 g [[Blanched slivered almonds]]
- 1 bottle (750 ml) [[Giffard Abricot du Roussillon]]

## Method
Fill a large basin with water and place an immersion circulator inside. Set the circulator to 145F. In a small skillet over medium heat, toast the almonds until slightly golden, about 5 minutes; cool slightly. Put the almonds and liqueur in a bowl and stir to combine. Transfer to a sealable, heatproof plastic bag, seal almost completely, remove the air by dipping the bag in the water, and finish sealing. When the water reaches 145F, cook the bag for 2 hours. Transfer to an ice bath and cool to room temperature. Pass through a fine-mesh sieve; if particles remain, strain again through a paper coffee filter or Superbag. Funnel back into the bottle and refrigerate.

## Notes
Sous vide.
