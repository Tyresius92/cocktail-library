# Grapefruit Cordial

## Ingredients
- 250g strained fresh [[Grapefruit Juice]]
- 250g [[Unbleached Cane Sugar]]
- 2.5g [[Citric Acid]]
- 10g [[Grapefruit]] zest

## Method
Immersion circulator at 135F. Whisk all ingredients in a bowl, seal in a heatproof bag, and cook 2 hours. Cool in an ice bath, pass through a fine-mesh sieve lined with several layers of cheesecloth (then a coffee filter or Superbag if particles remain). Refrigerate up to 2 weeks.

## Notes
Works for any citrus (try Meyer lemon, tangerine, mandarin); adjust the citric acid to the fruit, using less for high-acid fruits. Lower heat (135F) because the cordial loses its bright, fresh flavor if cooked hotter. The zest and juice particles break down into a syrup with multiple layers of grapefruit flavor.
