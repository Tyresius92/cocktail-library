# Phosphoric Acid Solution

## Ingredients
Ready-to-use option named in the book: Extinct Acid Phosphate by Darcy O'Neil (Art of Drink; see Resources, p299). Bulk phosphoric acid is concentrated and must be diluted: research safe methods.

## Method
Use even diluted in small quantities: a couple of drops to enhance acidity of other ingredients, or 1/2 to 1 tsp as the solo acidic backbone (more than 1 tsp adds a metallic flavor). Odorless and flavorless: adds tongue-tingling tartness (p194).
