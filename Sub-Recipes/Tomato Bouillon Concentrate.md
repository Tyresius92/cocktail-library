# Tomato Bouillon Concentrate

## Ingredients
- 75 g Knorr Tomato with Chicken Granulated Bouillon
- 25 g boiling water

## Method
Combine in a storage container and stir vigorously until the bouillon has dissolved. Refrigerate.

## Notes
Used in [[Normandie Club Bloody Mary Mix]].
