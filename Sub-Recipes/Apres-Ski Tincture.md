# Apres-Ski Tincture

## Ingredients
- 250 g vodka
- 20 g 100 percent [[Cedar incense scrapings]]
- 10 g crushed [[Cinnamon Stick]]s

## Method
Put all the ingredients in a jar, cover and shake. Let sit in a cool, dark place for 1 week. Strain through a fine-mesh sieve lined with several layers of cheesecloth, pour into a storage container and refrigerate.

## Notes
Cedar incense scrapings: the tincture is about a woody aroma on top of the drink, not for drinking in quantity. Find an incense that lists cedar wood as its only ingredient (no added chemicals), then scrape the cedar off the stick. See also [[Cedar Tincture]].
