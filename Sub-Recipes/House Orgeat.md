# House Orgeat

## Ingredients
- 800 g [[Almond Milk]] (p255)
- 1.2 kg superfine sugar
- 14 g [[Pierre Ferrand Ambre Cognac]]
- 18 g [[Lazzaroni amaretto]]
- 3 g rose water

## Method
Combine the almond milk and sugar in a saucepan over medium-low heat, stirring occasionally, until the sugar dissolves. Remove from heat and stir in the Cognac, amaretto and rose water. Cool to room temperature, then refrigerate.

## Notes
Keeps up to 2 weeks. Used in [[Peach Boy]], [[Professor Hinkle]], [[Long-Distance Lover]].
