# Cardamom-Infused St-Germain

## Ingredients
- 1 bottle (750 ml) [[St-Germain]]
- 10 g green cardamom pods

## Method
Combine in a container, cover and steep 12 hours. Strain, funnel back into the bottle and refrigerate.
