# Strawberry Cream Syrup

## Ingredients
- 500 g [[Clarified Strawberry Syrup]] (p57)
- 500 g [[Vanilla Lactic Syrup]]
- 130 g [[Citric Acid Solution]] (p298)

## Method
Combine all the ingredients and stir to blend. Refrigerate.
