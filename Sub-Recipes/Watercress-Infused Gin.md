# Watercress-Infused Gin

## Ingredients
- 1 (1-liter) bottle [[Fords gin]]
- 75 g [[Watercress]] leaves
- [[Pectinex Ultra SP-L]]

## Method
Combine all the ingredients in a blender and process until smooth. Strain and weigh the liquid (discard the solids). Multiply the weight by 0.002 to get X g; stir in X g Pectinex, cover and let sit 15 minutes. Divide the mixture evenly among centrifuge containers. Weigh the filled containers and adjust the liquid in each so the weights are exactly the same. Run the centrifuge at 4,500 rpm for 12 minutes. Strain through a paper coffee filter or Superbag without disturbing the solids; strain again if particles remain. Funnel back into the gin bottle and refrigerate.

## Notes
Centrifuge required.
