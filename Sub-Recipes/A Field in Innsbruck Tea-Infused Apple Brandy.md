# A Field in Innsbruck Tea-Infused Apple Brandy

## Ingredients
- 750 ml [[Clear Creek 2-year apple brandy]]
- 20 g [[August Uncommon Tea A Field in Innsbruck]]

## Method
Combine the brandy and tea in a container and steep for 10 minutes. Strain through a paper coffee filter or fine-mesh sieve lined with cheesecloth, funnel back into the bottle and refrigerate.

## Notes
Room-temperature infusion.
