# Donn's Mix No. 1

## Ingredients
- 400 g strained fresh grapefruit juice
- 200 g [[Cinnamon Syrup]] (p52)

## Method
Combine the grapefruit juice and Cinnamon Syrup in a bowl and whisk until blended. Transfer to a storage container and refrigerate.
