# Madras Curry-Infused Gin

## Ingredients
- 1 bottle (750 ml) [[Dorothy Parker gin]]
- 5 g [[Madras curry powder]]

## Method
Combine in a bowl and stir. Let stand at room temperature for 15 minutes, stirring occasionally. Strain through a fine-mesh sieve lined with several layers of cheesecloth, funnel back into the gin bottle and refrigerate.

## Notes
Used in [[Rendezvous in Chennai]].
