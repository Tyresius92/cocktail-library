# Brown Butter-Infused Apple Brandy

## Ingredients
- 225 g unsalted butter
- 750 ml [[Clear Creek 2-year apple brandy]]
- 750 ml [[Clear Creek 8-year apple brandy]]

## Method
Brown the butter in a saucepan until golden with a nutty aroma, then cool slightly. Combine with both brandies in a wide container, stir, cover and freeze until the butter solidifies on top (fat washing). Poke a hole, drain the liquid and strain through a fine-mesh sieve lined with cheesecloth or a Superbag. Refrigerate.

## Notes
Fat-washing technique.
