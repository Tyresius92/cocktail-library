# Birch-Infused Cocchi Vermouth di Torino

## Ingredients
- 1 bottle (750 ml) [[Cocchi Vermouth di Torino]]
- 4.75 g [[Terra Spice birch extract]]

## Method
Add the birch extract to the bottle, cap and shake to combine. Let stand 3 weeks before using.
