# Hickory Smoke-Infused Cognac

## Ingredients
- 1 bottle [[Pierre Ferrand 1840 Cognac]]
- 1 pinch finely ground [[Hickory chips]] (enough to fill smoker)

## Method
In a wide container with a lid, smoke the Cognac with the hickory chips using a PolyScience Smoking Gun (see [[_Notes - Smoky Flavors]]). Cover immediately to capture as much smoke as possible. Let stand until the smoke has dissipated, about 10 minutes. Transfer to a container and refrigerate.

## Notes
Use within 3 hours.
