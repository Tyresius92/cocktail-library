# Root Beer-Infused Cocchi Americano

## Ingredients
- 1 bottle (750 ml) [[Cocchi Americano Bianco]]
- 2.75 g [[Terra Spice root beer extract]]

## Method
Add the root beer extract directly to the bottle. Seal, then gently tip the bottle upside down a few times to combine. Refrigerate.
