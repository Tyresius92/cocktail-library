# House Lemon Cordial

## Ingredients
- about 3 quarts [[Lemon]] wheels or wedges, or the rinds of 10 lemons
- 600 g white sugar
- 1,400 g fresh [[Lemon Juice]]
- Pectinex Ultra SP-L (0.2% of the liquid weight)

## Method
Combine the lemon pieces and sugar in a large container, cover and refrigerate 2 days, stirring occasionally (oils extract into the sugar and it liquefies). Strain, weigh the liquid (discard the solids), add the lemon juice and stir until dissolved. Multiply the total weight by 0.002 to get X grams; stir in X g Pectinex, cover and let sit 15 minutes. Divide evenly among centrifuge containers, balancing their weights exactly, and run at 4,500 rpm for 12 minutes. Carefully strain the cordial through a paper coffee filter or Superbag without disturbing the solids; re-strain if particles remain. Refrigerate.

## Notes
Used in [[Rye Pie]]. Keeps up to 1 month. Needs a centrifuge.
