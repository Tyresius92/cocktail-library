# House Orange Bitters

## Ingredients
- 100 g [[Fee Brothers West Indian Orange Bitters]]
- 100 g [[Angostura Orange Bitters]]
- 100 g [[Regans' Orange Bitters]]

## Method
Place all the ingredients in a bowl and stir to combine. Transfer to a storage container and store at room temperature.

## Notes
The earlier version of this note had 10 g Regans' and plain Angostura; the book (p295) lists 100 g each of the three orange bitters.
