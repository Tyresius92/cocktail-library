# Raspberry Syrup

## Ingredients
- 500g [[Simple Syrup]]
- 150g fresh [[Raspberry]]
- 2.5g [[Citric Acid]]

## Method
Immersion circulator at 135F. Stir all ingredients together in a bowl, seal in a heatproof bag, and cook 2 hours. Cool in an ice bath, pass through a fine-mesh sieve (then a paper coffee filter or Superbag if particles remain). Refrigerate up to 2 weeks.

## Notes
Gentle heat extracts the berries' flavor below the temperature where they turn jammy. Unlike blended syrups (bitter seed flavors, cloudy) or cooked syrups (jam-like, deep purple), this gives a vibrant, transparent pink syrup with no free-floating particles, which matters for carbonation (Collins or fully carbonated cocktails).
