# Coconut-Infused Bourbon

## Ingredients
- 50 g unsweetened coconut flakes
- 1 (1-liter) bottle [[Old Forester 86 bourbon]]

## Method
Fill a large basin with water and place an immersion circulator inside. Set the circulator to 145F. In a small skillet over medium heat, toast the coconut, stirring often, until slightly golden, about 4 minutes; cool slightly. Put the coconut and bourbon in a bowl and stir to combine. Transfer to a sealable, heatproof plastic bag. Seal almost completely, then remove as much air as possible by dipping the bag (other than the unsealed portion) in the water; the counterpressure pushes out the rest of the air. Finish sealing, remove from the water, and when the water has reached 145F cook the bag for 2 hours. Transfer to an ice bath and cool to room temperature. Pass through a fine-mesh sieve lined with several layers of cheesecloth; if particles remain, strain again through a paper coffee filter or Superbag. Transfer to a container, cover and freeze for 24 hours (this solidifies the fatty coconut oils, allowing for a transparent infusion). Strain again through a fine-mesh sieve lined with cheesecloth, funnel back into the bottle and refrigerate.

## Notes
Sous vide with a freeze step to remove the coconut fat.
