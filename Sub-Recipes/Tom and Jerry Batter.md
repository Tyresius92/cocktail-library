# Tom and Jerry Batter

## Ingredients
- 450 g [[Unbleached Cane Sugar]]
- 1 tbsp ground cinnamon
- 1 tbsp ground mace
- 1 tbsp ground allspice
- 1/2 tbsp ground cloves
- 12 eggs, separated
- 1/2 tsp cream of tartar

## Method
Whisk the sugar and spices together. Break up the yolks with an immersion blender and, while blending, slowly add the sugar mixture; cover and set aside in a cool place. In a separate clean, dry bowl, beat the whites with the cream of tartar to medium peaks. Gently fold the whites into the yolk mixture. Transfer to a storage container and refrigerate.
