# House Grenadine

## Ingredients
- 250g [[POM Wonderful pomegranate juice]]
- 250g [[Unbleached Cane Sugar]]
- 1.85g powdered [[Malic Acid]]
- 1.25g powdered [[Citric Acid]]
- 0.15g [[Terra Spice orange extract]]

## Method
Blender: combine all the ingredients and process until the sugar has dissolved. Refrigerate up to 3 weeks.

## Notes
Many bars reduce pomegranate juice for grenadine, which gives a murky, dense syrup that tastes nothing like the childhood version (made with high-fructose corn syrup). This is a clean, simple version using quality ingredients; malic acid adds tartness.
