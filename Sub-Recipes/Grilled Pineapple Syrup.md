# Grilled Pineapple Syrup

## Ingredients
- 1 pineapple
- 500 g [[Unbleached Cane Sugar]]
- 2.5 g citric acid powder

## Method
Peel and cut the pineapple into 3/4-inch-thick wheels, then grill over a wood fire until slightly smoky but not burned. Cool, then run through a juice extractor. Weigh out 500 g of the juice (reserve the rest). Blend the juice, sugar and citric acid until smooth. Strain through a fine-mesh sieve lined with several layers of cheesecloth, then refrigerate.

## Notes
Used in [[Smoky Colada]]. Keeps up to 4 weeks.
