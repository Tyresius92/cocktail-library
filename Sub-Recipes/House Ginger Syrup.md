# House Ginger Syrup

## Ingredients
- 250g fresh [[Ginger]], washed and coarsely chopped
- about 300g [[Unbleached Cane Sugar]] (1.5 x the weight of the juice)

## Method
Blender: juice the ginger (no need to peel) and pass the juice through a fine-mesh sieve. Weigh the juice, multiply by 1.5 and weigh out that much sugar. Blend the juice and sugar until dissolved. Refrigerate up to 2 weeks.

## Notes
1 part fresh ginger juice to 1 1/2 parts cane sugar. Fiery and intense, but balanced by other ingredients. Used where ginger beer would be, e.g. Moscow Mule, Dark and Stormy, Penicillin. If you can't juice ginger, buy plain ginger juice with no added sweeteners or flavorings.
