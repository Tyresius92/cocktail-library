# Pineapple Gum Syrup

## Ingredients
- 250g [[Unbleached Cane Sugar]]
- 15g [[Gum Arabic]]
- 1.5g [[Citric Acid]]
- 250g fresh [[Pineapple Juice]]

## Method
Immersion circulator at 145F. Blend the sugar, gum arabic and citric acid for 30 seconds; with the blender running, slowly add the pineapple juice and process until the dry ingredients dissolve, about 2 minutes. Seal in a heatproof bag and cook 2 hours. Cool in an ice bath, strain through a Superbag, and refrigerate up to 1 week.

## Notes
Pineapple's high pectin gives shaken drinks a frothy head like egg white, but the juice separates quickly and spoils fast. This syrup keeps it in solution; citric acid adds sharpness and brightens the flavor, and gum arabic (as in Demerara Gum Syrup) keeps sugar and juice together and allows use in stirred drinks too.
