# Toasted Pecan-Infused Plantation Rum

## Ingredients
- 150 g [[Pecans]]
- 1 bottle (750 ml) [[Plantation Barbados 5-year rum]]

## Method
Fill a large basin with water and place an immersion circulator inside. Set the circulator to 145F. In a small skillet over medium heat, toast the pecans until slightly golden, about 5 minutes; cool slightly. Put the pecans and rum in a bowl and stir to combine. Transfer to a sealable, heatproof plastic bag, seal almost completely, remove the air by dipping the bag in the water, and finish sealing. When the water reaches 145F, cook the bag for 2 hours. Transfer to an ice bath and cool to room temperature. Pass through a fine-mesh sieve; if particles remain, strain again through a paper coffee filter or Superbag. Funnel back into the bottle and refrigerate.

## Notes
Sous vide.
