# Malic Acid Solution

## Ingredients
- 90 g [[Filtered Water]]
- 10 g lactic acid powder (as printed)

## Method
Combine in a glass bowl and stir until dissolved. Transfer to a glass dropper bottle or other glass container and refrigerate.

## Notes
The book prints lactic acid powder here, apparently a copy of the Lactic Acid Solution; presumably malic acid is meant. Left as printed.
