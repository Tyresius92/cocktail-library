# Normandie Club Bloody Mary Mix

## Ingredients
- 6 oz fresh tomato juice
- 2 oz bottled tomato juice
- 2 oz fresh celery juice
- 1 1/2 oz fresh red bell pepper juice
- 1 oz [[Tomato Bouillon Concentrate]]
- 1/2 oz fresh lemon juice
- 1/2 oz fresh lime juice
- 1/2 oz dill pickle juice (preferably Bubbie's)
- 4 tsp Worcestershire sauce
- 2 tsp Bragg Liquid Aminos
- 2 tsp Sriracha
- 2 tsp prepared horseradish (preferably Bubbie's)

## Method
Place all the ingredients in a bowl and stir to combine. Transfer to a storage container and refrigerate.
