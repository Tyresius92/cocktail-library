# Chamomile-Infused Rye Whiskey

## Ingredients
- 1 bottle (750 ml) [[Rittenhouse rye]]
- 5 g dried chamomile flowers

## Method
Combine in a bowl and stir. Let stand at room temperature for 1 hour, stirring occasionally. Strain through a fine-mesh sieve lined with several layers of cheesecloth, funnel back into the original bottle and refrigerate.
