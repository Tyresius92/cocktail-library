# Demerara Gum Syrup

## Ingredients
- 300g [[Demerara Sugar]]
- 18g [[Gum Arabic]]
- 150g [[Filtered Water]]

## Method
Immersion circulator at 145F: blend the sugar and gum arabic for 30 seconds; with the blender running, slowly add the water and process until dissolved, about 2 minutes. Seal in a heatproof bag and cook 2 hours. Cool in an ice bath, transfer to a storage container and refrigerate up to 2 weeks.

## Notes
Demerara's molasses gives a rich toffee flavor. Used to round the edges of intense spirits (as in Old-Fashioneds) and add body to stirred drinks without too much sweetness. Not for clean, sharp, bright drinks (e.g. a Daiquiri), where it would muddy or dull the fresh flavors.
