# Oolong-Infused Vodka

## Ingredients
- 1 (1-liter) bottle [[Absolut Elyx vodka]]
- 20 g [[Oolong tea leaves]]

## Method
Combine in a bowl and stir. Let stand at room temperature for 20 minutes, stirring occasionally. Strain through a fine-mesh sieve lined with several layers of cheesecloth, funnel back into the vodka bottle and refrigerate.
