# Cedar Tincture

## Ingredients
- 1 bottle (750 ml) [[Absolut vodka]]
- 20 g 100 percent [[Cedar incense scrapings]] (see [[Apres-Ski Tincture]], p297)

## Method
Combine the vodka and cedar shavings in an iSi whipper and follow the pressurized-infusion method: charge with N2O, shake, recharge, stand 15 minutes shaking every 30 seconds, vent quickly, strain through a paper coffee filter or Superbag, funnel back into the bottle and refrigerate.
