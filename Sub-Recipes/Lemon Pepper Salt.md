# Lemon Pepper Salt

## Ingredients
- 20 g dehydrated [[Lemon]] slices
- 10 g freshly ground black pepper
- 50 g kosher salt

## Method
Process the lemon slices, pepper and half the salt in a spice grinder or blender until powdery. Transfer to a storage container, add the remaining salt, seal and shake to combine. Store in a dry place.
