# Cherry Wood-Smoked Almond Milk

## Ingredients
- 1 liter [[Almond Milk]] (p255)
- 1 pinch dried cherry wood chips (enough to fill smoker)

## Method
In a wide container with a lid, smoke the almond milk with cherry wood chips using a PolyScience Smoking Gun (see [[_Notes - Smoky Flavors]]). Cover immediately to capture as much smoke as possible. Let stand until the smoke has dissipated, about 10 minutes. Transfer to a container and refrigerate.

## Notes
Use within 3 hours; refrigerate until ready to use.
