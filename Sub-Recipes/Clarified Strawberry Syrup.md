# Clarified Strawberry Syrup

## Ingredients
- 250g hulled [[Strawberry]]
- 250g [[Unbleached Cane Sugar]]
- 0.5g [[Pectinex Ultra SP-L]]

## Method
Centrifuge: blend the strawberries and sugar until very smooth. Once the sugar has dissolved, add the Pectinex and process 10 seconds. Transfer to a centrifuge container, weigh it and balance the other containers with equal weights of water. Spin at 4,500 rpm for 12 minutes, then carefully strain through a Superbag or paper coffee filter without disturbing the solids on the bottom. Refrigerate up to 1 week.

## Notes
Clarifying blended strawberry syrup matters most for stirred or carbonated cocktails. Without a centrifuge, see the overnight cheesecloth option in the technique note.
