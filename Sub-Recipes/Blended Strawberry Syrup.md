# Blended Strawberry Syrup

## Ingredients
- 250g hulled [[Strawberry]]
- 250g [[White Sugar]]

## Method
Blender: process the strawberries and sugar until very smooth. Pass through a fine-mesh sieve, pressing the solids to extract as much liquid as possible. Refrigerate up to 2 weeks.

## Notes
Heat gives berries an artificial taste, so equal weights of strawberries and sugar are blended cold to lock in fresh strawberry flavor. See [[Clarified Strawberry Syrup]] for the clarified version.
