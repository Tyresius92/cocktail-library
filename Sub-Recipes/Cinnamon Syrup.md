# Cinnamon Syrup

## Ingredients
- 500g [[Simple Syrup]]
- 10g crushed [[Cinnamon Stick]]s
- 0.1g [[Kosher Salt]]

## Method
Immersion circulator at 145F. Stir all ingredients together, seal in a heatproof bag, and cook 2 hours. Cool in an ice bath, pass through a fine-mesh sieve (then a coffee filter or Superbag if particles remain). Refrigerate up to 2 weeks.

## Notes
Overnight steeping gives a mild, somewhat bitter syrup; boiling gives pungent flavor but skews sweet as water boils off. The circulator gives a full spectrum of cinnamon flavor, with woody undertones and the bright aroma of freshly grated cinnamon, not just Red Hots candy.
