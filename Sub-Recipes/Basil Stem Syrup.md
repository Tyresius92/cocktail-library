# Basil Stem Syrup

## Ingredients
- 500 g water
- 50 g basil stems
- 500 g [[Unbleached Cane Sugar]]

## Method
Bring the water to a boil in a saucepan. Remove from heat, add the basil stems and steep for 30 minutes. Strain through a fine-mesh sieve lined with several layers of cheesecloth, then add the sugar and whisk until dissolved. Transfer to a storage container and refrigerate.

## Notes
Keeps up to 2 weeks.
