# Chamomile-Infused Blanco Tequila

## Ingredients
- 1 bottle (750 ml) [[Pueblo Viejo blanco tequila]]
- 5 g dried chamomile

## Method
Combine in a container and steep 1 hour. Strain, funnel back into the bottle and refrigerate.
