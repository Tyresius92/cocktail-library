# Graham Cracker-Infused Elijah Craig Single-Barrel Bourbon

## Ingredients
- 2 bottles (750 ml) [[Elijah Craig Small Batch bourbon]]
- 408 g graham crackers
- Pectinex Ultra SP-L

## Method
Combine the bourbon and graham crackers in a blender and process until smooth. Strain and weigh the liquid (discard the solids). Multiply the weight by 0.004 to get X g; stir in X g Pectinex, cover and let sit 15 minutes. Divide among centrifuge containers, weigh and adjust so the weights are exactly equal, and run at 4,500 rpm for 12 minutes. Strain through a paper coffee filter or Superbag without disturbing the solids; strain again if particles remain. Funnel back into the bourbon bottle and refrigerate.

## Notes
The book titles this "Graham Cracker-Infused Bourbon" and lists Elijah Craig Small Batch; the note name was kept from the Campfire recipe, which names Single-Barrel. Centrifuge required (see [[_Notes - Making Infusions]]).
