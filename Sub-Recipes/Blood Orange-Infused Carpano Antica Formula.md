# Blood Orange-Infused Carpano Antica Formula

## Ingredients
- 1 bottle (750 ml) [[Carpano Antica Formula vermouth]]
- 55 g blood orange zest

## Method
Combine the bottle contents and the ingredient in an iSi whipper, seal, and charge with N2O. Shake about 5 times, change the cartridge, charge again and shake. Let stand 15 minutes, shaking every 30 seconds. Vent the gas quickly at a 45-degree angle into a container. Strain through a paper coffee filter or Superbag, funnel back into the bottle and refrigerate. (Technique: pressurized infusion.)

## Notes
Best within a week or so (citrus zest).
