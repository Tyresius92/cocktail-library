# Basic Bloody Mary Mix

## Ingredients
- 1,100 g organic tomato juice (bottled)
- 180 g Worcestershire sauce
- 30 g Magi seasoning
- 72 g strained fresh lemon juice
- 72 g strained fresh lime juice
- 30 g Tapatio hot sauce

## Method
Place all the ingredients in a bowl and stir to combine. Transfer to a storage container and refrigerate.
