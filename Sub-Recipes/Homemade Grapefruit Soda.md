# Homemade Grapefruit Soda

## Ingredients
- 5 oz seltzer
- 1 3/4 oz [[Grapefruit Cordial]] (p53)
- 1 tsp [[Citric Acid Solution]] (p298)
- 1 tsp [[Phosphoric Acid Solution]] (p194)
- 1 drop Terra Spice grapefruit extract

## Method
Chill all the ingredients. Combine them in a carbonation bottle, charge with CO2 and gently shake to help dissolve the CO2 (see [[_Notes - Carbonating Cocktails]]). Refrigerate the bottle at least 20 minutes, preferably 12 hours, before opening.
