# Spiced Almond Demerara Gum Syrup

## Ingredients
- 500g [[Demerara Gum Syrup]]
- 30g [[Sliced Almonds]]
- 6g crushed [[Cinnamon Stick]]s
- 0.25g [[Whole Cloves]], preferably Penang
- 1 [[Green Cardamom Pod]]

## Method
Direct heat: put the gum syrup in a small saucepan over low heat, add the remaining ingredients and stir to combine. Bring to a simmer, then cook, stirring constantly, for 10 minutes, keeping it at a simmer (never a rolling boil). Let cool to room temperature, strain through a fine-mesh sieve, and refrigerate up to 1 month.

## Notes
Created to add balance and seasoning to the [[Normandie Club Old-Fashioned]]: cinnamon, clove and cardamom give bright spice notes, almonds a fatty roundness that complements the coconut-infused bourbon. In small quantities spiced syrups act like bitters; for larger amounts in a citrus drink, replace part of it with a neutral syrup like simple syrup so the spice doesn't overwhelm.
