# Almond Milk

## Ingredients
- 600 g [[Sliced Blanched Almonds]]
- 7 1/2 cups filtered water

## Method
Combine the almonds and water in a blender. Blend until very smooth, then strain through a fine-mesh sieve lined with several layers of cheesecloth, letting the liquid drain for 1 to 2 hours. Don't press on the solids until the very end, when the pulp is mostly dry; the pulp that builds up at the bottom of the cheesecloth further filters the milk.

## Notes
Yield and shelf life not stated on the page. The authors' preferred dairy alternative (see [[_Notes - Eggs and Dairy]]): mild flavor that works with a wide range of spirits and liqueurs. Nut milks are thinner than cream, so use more of them and add a little more sweetener.
