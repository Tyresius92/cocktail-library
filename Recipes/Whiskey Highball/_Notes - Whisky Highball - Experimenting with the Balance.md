# Whisky Highball: Experimenting with the Balance (pp. 212-213)

The mixer is a large part of a Highball's volume and the choices are nearly unlimited, so swapping it is a vast canvas: another commercial mixer (tonic, cola) or radical choices like beer, sparkling wine or juice, including vegetable juices for savory drinks like the Bloody Mary.

## Cocktails
- [[Paloma]]: tequila + grapefruit soda (and liqueur)
- [[Cuba Libre]]: rum + cola + lime
- [[Negroni Sbagliato]]: Americano with prosecco for seltzer
- [[Screwdriver]]: vodka + fresh orange juice (+ citric acid)
