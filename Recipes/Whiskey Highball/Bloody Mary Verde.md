# Bloody Mary Verde
## Root
[[Whisky Highball]]

## Ingredients
- Rim: [[Kosher Salt]] and chile powder, equal parts #seasoning
- 1 [[Lime]] wedge (for rimming) #seasoning
- 5 oz [[Verde Mix]] (p296) #balance
- 1 1/2 oz [[Jalapeno-Infused Vodka]] (p291) #core
- 1 oz fresh [[Lime Juice]] #balance
- Garnish: 1 [[Cherry Tomato]] and 1 [[Lemon]] wedge on a skewer #seasoning

## Method
Mix equal parts kosher salt and chile powder on a small plate. Rub the lime wedge along the upper 1/4 inch of a Collins glass, halfway around, then roll the wet portion in the mixture. Fill the glass with ice cubes. Add the remaining ingredients and stir a few times. Garnish with the cherry tomato and lemon wedge.

## Notes
Devon Tarby, 2013. The one exception to the book's tomato-based mixes: a tomatillo-based Verde Mix. Cucumber and tomatillo are less sweet than tomatoes, so honey is added to the mix. The intro says the mix pairs with a spicy infused spirit (a jalapeno infusion fits that); the recipe uses [[Jalapeno-Infused Vodka]].
