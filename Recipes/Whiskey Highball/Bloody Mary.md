# Bloody Mary
## Root
[[Whisky Highball]]

## Ingredients
- Rim: kosher salt and pepper, equal parts #seasoning
- 1 lemon wedge (for rimming) #seasoning
- 2 oz [[Aylesbury Duck vodka]] #core
- 5 oz [[Basic Bloody Mary Mix]] (p295) #balance
- 1/4 oz fresh [[Lemon]] juice #balance
- Garnish: 1 lemon wedge and 1 [[Celery Stalk]] #seasoning

## Method
Mix equal parts salt and pepper on a small plate. Rub the lemon wedge along the upper 1/4 inch of a pint glass, halfway around, then roll the wet portion in the salt and pepper. Fill the glass with ice cubes. Add the remaining ingredients and stir a few times. Garnish with the lemon wedge and celery stalk.

## Notes
Classic. The queen of savory Highballs; a simple baseline and template to adjust. Ideas from the book: a splash of mezcal for smoke, or swap manzanilla sherry for the vodka to boost savoriness and lower the ABV, or use [[Roasted Garlic- and Pepper-Infused Vodka]] (p292). See [[_Notes - Rimming]].
