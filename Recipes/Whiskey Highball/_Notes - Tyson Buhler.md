# Friends and Family: Tyson Buhler (p220)

Bar director at Death & Co and 2015 World Class bartending competition winner.

Gist of his essay (paraphrased):
- The Highball is likely the first drink any bartender learns; his introduction was golf-course regulars ordering whisky sodas.
- Japan has a whole high-end Highball culture now. The *mizuwari* ("cut with water") ritual: chill the glass with ice, pour off the melt, add whisky and stir a precise number of times (13 1/2), then add sparkling water and stir 3 1/2 times. Some bars use one big piece of ice, others re-ice at each step.
- Guests see a two-ingredient drink that is mostly water, so the care behind it is hard to convey, but that's fine if they enjoy it.
- The Highball's beauty is simplicity: a spirit, a glass, ice and carbonated water; no shaker, syrups or bitters. That also leaves lots of room to branch out past G&Ts, rum and Cokes and Palomas, which is where [[Calvados and Tonic]] comes from.
