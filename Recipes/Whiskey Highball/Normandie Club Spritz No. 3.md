# Normandie Club Spritz No. 3
## Root
[[Whisky Highball]]

## Ingredients
- 1 oz [[Boissiere dry vermouth]] #core
- 1 oz [[St-Germain]] #core
- 1/4 oz [[Pueblo Viejo Blanco tequila]] #seasoning
- 1/4 oz [[Campo de Encanto Grand and Noble pisco]] #seasoning
- 1/4 oz [[Giffard Creme de Pamplemousse]] #seasoning
- 1 oz clarified [[Grapefruit Juice]] (see [[_Notes - Clarifying Juice]], p146) #balance
- 1/2 oz clarified [[Lemon Juice]] (see [[_Notes - Clarifying Juice]], p146) #balance
- 2 1/2 oz cold [[Seltzer]] #balance
- Garnish: 1 [[Grapefruit]] half wheel #seasoning

## Your Bar
```dataviewjs
await dv.view("Views/recipe-status");
```

## Method
Chill all the ingredients. Combine in a carbonating bottle, charge with CO2 and gently shake to dissolve it (see [[_Notes - Carbonating Cocktails]]). Refrigerate at least 20 minutes, preferably 12 hours, before opening. Pour into an ice-filled Highball glass and garnish with the grapefruit half wheel.

## Notes
Devon Tarby, 2015. Expands on the [[Improved Wine Spritz]]: a split base of dry vermouth and St-Germain, with clarified juices for balance without changing the Champagne-like texture. Fully carbonated rather than topped with seltzer, for uniform effervescence. (Printed as "Normandie Club Spritz #3".)
