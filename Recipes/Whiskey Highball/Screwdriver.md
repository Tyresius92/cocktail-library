# Screwdriver
## Root
[[Whisky Highball]]

## Ingredients
- 4 oz fresh [[Orange Juice]] #balance
- [[Citric Acid Solution]] (p298), if needed #seasoning
- 2 oz [[Vodka]] #core

## Method
Taste the juice before mixing. If the flavor seems a little flat, add a bit of Citric Acid Solution, a drop at a time, until the flavor is sweet with a tangy bite. Combine the orange juice and vodka in a Highball glass, then add 3 ice cubes. Stir for 3 seconds. No garnish.

## Notes
Classic. Highballs with citrus juice as the mixer disappoint when made with processed commercial juice; they're redeemed by freshly pressed, in-season orange juice, whose bright acidity and sweetness are balanced. Even fresh juice can fall flat, hence the drops of citric acid. See [[_Notes - Alternative Acids]].
