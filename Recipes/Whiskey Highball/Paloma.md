# Paloma
## Root
[[Whisky Highball]]

## Ingredients
- 1 [[Lime]] wedge #seasoning
- Rim: [[Kosher Salt]] #seasoning
- 1 3/4 oz blanco [[Tequila]] #core
- 1/4 oz [[Giffard Creme de Pamplemousse]] #balance
- 1/4 oz fresh [[Lime Juice]] #balance
- 4 oz cold [[Homemade Grapefruit Soda]] (p295) #balance
- Garnish: 1 [[Grapefruit]] wedge #seasoning

## Method
Rub the lime wedge along the upper 1/2 inch of a Highball glass, halfway around the circumference, then roll the wet portion in the salt (see [[_Notes - Rimming]]). Pour in the tequila, grapefruit liqueur and lime juice, then add 3 ice cubes. Stir for 3 seconds. Add the soda and stir once. Garnish with the grapefruit wedge.

## Notes
Classic. Mexicans are far more likely to mix a Paloma than a Margarita: simply grapefruit soda and tequila with a squeeze of lime. Also works with mezcal. The authors amplify it with homemade grapefruit soda from fresh grapefruit plus a touch of grapefruit liqueur.
