# Cuba Libre
## Root
[[Whisky Highball]]

## Ingredients
- 2 oz white rum ([[Rum]]) #core
- 1/4 oz fresh [[Lime Juice]] #balance
- 4 oz cold [[Coca-Cola]] #balance
- Garnish: 1 [[Lime]] wedge #seasoning

## Method
Pour the rum into a Highball glass, then add 3 ice cubes. Stir for 3 seconds. Add the lime juice and cola and stir once. Garnish with the lime wedge.

## Notes
Classic. A humble Highball (rum and Coke) elevated by lime juice: lime peel is a key ingredient in most colas, so the juice draws that flavor out while cutting through the sweetness. The core spirit matters: rum's sugarcane backbone complements cola's round spice, and whisky's spice works with cola's bitter and citrus flavors, but vodka's subtlety would fall flat and gin's botanicals would clash with the mixer.
