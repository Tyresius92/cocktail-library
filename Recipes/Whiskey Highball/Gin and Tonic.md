# Gin and Tonic
## Root
[[Whisky Highball]]

## Ingredients
- 2 oz London dry gin ([[Gin]]) #core
- 4 oz cold [[Tonic Water]] #balance
- Garnish: 1 [[Lime]] wedge #seasoning

## Your Bar
```dataviewjs
await dv.view("Views/recipe-status");
```

## Method
Pour the gin into a Highball glass, then add 3 ice cubes. Stir for 3 seconds. Add the tonic water and stir once. Garnish with the lime wedge.

## Notes
Classic. Perhaps even more popular than the Whisky Highball because of how its two simple ingredients interact. Tonic isn't just a bitter seltzer: it has a fair amount of sugar, giving a sweetness that highlights the botanical flavors of high-proof English gin while curbing its alcohol heat.
