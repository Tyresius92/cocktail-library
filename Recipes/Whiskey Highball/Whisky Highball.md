# Whisky Highball
## Root
[[Ideal Whisky Highball]]

## Ingredients
- 2 oz [[Scotch]] #core
- 6 oz cold [[Seltzer]] #balance
- Garnish: 1 [[Lemon]] wedge #seasoning

## Method
Pour the scotch into a Highball glass, then add 3 ice cubes. Stir for 3 seconds. Add the seltzer and stir once. Garnish with the lemon wedge.

## Notes
Classic (p199). Nobody knows who first mixed scotch with sparkling water, but the cocktail dates to around the turn of the twentieth century, as did its name. Possible origins: steam locomotives 'highballing' (the pressure ball at the top of the gauge), a raised-globe train signal meaning the track was clear, or simply a nineteenth-century word for a glass (a ball), meaning a tall glass of scotch.
