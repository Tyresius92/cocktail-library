# Wine Spritzes (pp224-226)

Highballs with wine as the core. Still wine is lower proof and milder than a spirit, so more wine and less seltzer than a spirit Highball. Related to the earlier sparkling-wine Highballs ([[Mimosa]], [[Kir Royale]], [[St-Germain Cocktail]], [[Aperol Spritz]]).

## Ladder of complexity
- [[Wine Spritz]]: wine + seltzer
- [[Improved Wine Spritz]]: adds lemon juice and simple syrup (sweet-sour balance, from the Daiquiri chapter)
- [[King's Landing]]: infused vermouth, pear liqueur and lemon topped with cava
- [[Normandie Club Spritz No. 3]]: split dry vermouth / St-Germain base, clarified juices, fully carbonated
- [[Daisy Chain]]: sherry/St-Germain/Suze/verjus built to mimic dry sparkling wine, topped with cider
- [[Normandie Club Spritz No. 1]]: clarified cucumber water and lime, fir eau de vie, carbonated
