# Mimosa
## Root
[[Whisky Highball]]

## Ingredients
- 1 oz fresh [[Orange Juice]] #balance
- 3 drops [[Citric Acid Solution]] (p298) #balance
- 5 oz cold dry sparkling wine #core

## Your Bar
```dataviewjs
await dv.view("Views/recipe-status");
```

## Method
Combine the orange juice and Citric Acid Solution in a chilled flute. Pour in the sparkling wine and quickly dip the barspoon into the glass to gently mix. No garnish.

## Notes
Classic. Sparkling wines vary widely in acidity, sugar and flavor, so they aren't interchangeable. Fresh juice makes a big difference, and since oranges vary, a little citric acid keeps it bright and zippy. See [[Dry Champagne]] for the Champagne version of the template.
