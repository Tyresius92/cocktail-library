# Negroni Sbagliato
## Root
[[Whisky Highball]]

## Ingredients
- 1 oz [[Carpano Antica Formula vermouth]] #core
- 1 oz [[Campari]] #core
- 1 oz cold [[Prosecco]] #balance
- Garnish: 1 orange wedge #seasoning

```dataviewjs
await dv.view("Views/recipe-status");
```

## Method
Pour the vermouth and Campari into a Highball glass and stir to combine. Fill the glass with ice cubes, then pour in the prosecco and quickly dip the barspoon into the glass to gently mix the wine with the cocktail. Garnish with the orange wedge.

## Notes
Classic. The [[Americano]] with the seltzer replaced by sparkling wine. Because sparkling wine has more flavor than seltzer, less is used, resulting in a drink that dances between bitter, sweet and refreshing.
