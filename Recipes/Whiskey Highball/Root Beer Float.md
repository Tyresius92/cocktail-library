# Root Beer Float
## Root
[[Whisky Highball]]

## Ingredients
- 2 1/2 oz water #balance
- 1 1/2 oz [[Milk-Washed Rum]] (p291) #core
- 1/2 oz [[Giffard Vanille de Madagascar]] #seasoning
- 1/2 oz [[Simple Syrup]] (p45) #balance
- 1/8 tsp [[Phosphoric Acid Solution]] (p194) #balance
- 2 drops [[Terra Spice root beer extract]] #seasoning

## Your Bar
```dataviewjs
await dv.view("Views/recipe-status");
```

## Method
Chill all the ingredients. Combine them in a carbonation bottle, charge with CO2 and gently shake to help dissolve it (see [[_Notes - Carbonating Cocktails]]). Refrigerate at least 20 minutes before opening. To serve, carefully pour into a Highball glass with ice.
