# Calvados and Tonic
## Root
[[Whisky Highball]]

## Ingredients
- 2 oz [[Domaine de Montreuil Pays d'Auge Reserve Calvados]] #core
- 4 oz cold [[Fever-Tree tonic water]] #balance

## Your Bar
```dataviewjs
await dv.view("Views/recipe-status");
```

## Method
Pour the Calvados into a Highball glass, then add 3 ice cubes. Stir for 3 seconds. Add the tonic and stir once. No garnish.
