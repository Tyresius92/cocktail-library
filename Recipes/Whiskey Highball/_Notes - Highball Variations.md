# Highball Variations and the Extended Family (pp. 216-219, condensed)

Because of the Highball's simplicity, elements from the earlier chapters' root cocktails can generate balanced Highballs: the Daiquiri's acid-and-sugar interplay in small quantities (not so much that it becomes a sour), the Old-Fashioned's bitters to accent a spirit, the Martini's spirit married to fortified wine. Some drinks later look like sours, Old-Fashioneds or Martinis stretched with a mixer.

The extended family (p218) plays with the one-part-base-to-two-parts-mixer template: savory Highballs like the Bloody Mary increase the mixer; Champagne drinks like the Mimosa flip it and use a small amount of orange juice as flavoring. At heart all are alcohol plus mixer.
