# Ideal Whisky Highball
## Root
[[Whisky Highball]]

## Ingredients
- 2 oz [[Hakushu 12-year Japanese whisky]] #core
- 4 oz cold [[Seltzer]] #balance

## Your Bar

## Method
Pour the whisky into a Highball glass and add 1 ice cube. Let sit for 3 seconds. Add the seltzer and stir once. No garnish.

## Notes
The book's root recipe (p200). The base spirit is a personal choice (smoky Islay scotch, fruity blended Irish whiskey), but since a Highball has only whiskey and sparkling water, the authors lean toward a spirit that doesn't overpower the seltzer's light tingle. They omit the usual lemon wedge as unnecessary, keeping the harmony of great whisky and bubbly water intact. With stronger-flavored whiskies (Islay single malt, American rye or bourbon) adjust the ratio: they prefer 5 oz seltzer to 2 oz whisky, or keep a glass of chilled seltzer at hand to top off.
