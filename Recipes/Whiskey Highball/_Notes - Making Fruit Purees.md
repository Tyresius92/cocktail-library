# Making Fruit Purees (p222 sidebar)

Needs a blender and a fine-mesh sieve.
1. Wash the fruit well; peel it if the skin is bitter or off-putting; remove pits and seeds.
2. For fruits that brown (peaches, pears, apples), add [[Ascorbic Acid]] powder to the blender: 1/2 tsp per 2 cups of fruit.
3. Blend until smooth, then pass through the fine-mesh sieve.
4. Store covered in the refrigerator up to 1 week, or in the freezer up to 1 month.
