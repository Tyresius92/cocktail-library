# Bellini
## Root
[[Whisky Highball]]

## Ingredients
- 1 oz fresh [[Peach]] puree (see [[_Notes - Making Fruit Purees]]) #balance
- 5 oz cold [[Prosecco]] #core

## Your Bar
```dataviewjs
await dv.view("Views/recipe-status");
```

## Method
Put the peach puree in a flute. Pour in the prosecco and quickly dip the barspoon into the glass to gently mix. No garnish.

## Notes
Classic. Best with peaches at peak ripeness; even the best commercial puree tastes dull next to one made from prime peaches. Works with almost any ripe fruit, so it opens the door to many variations.
