# Crushed Ice and Whipping (p134, condensed from the Mojito intro)

Crushed ice means thinking about how the cocktail evolves after it's served. Key: pack the ice into the glass, using a greater volume of ice than cocktail. This keeps the drink cold and keeps the ice from moving around; if it moves, dilution speeds up and the drink becomes a watery mess.

Precautions for the Mojito and its variations:
1. Use a glass that holds a full cocktail plus ice: a Collins or double Old-Fashioned of at least 16 oz.
2. Freeze the glass so it's extremely cold when the drink is poured.
3. Instead of shaking, whip: shake with just a few pieces of crushed ice, enough to mix without diluting much. There's no need to chill as much as for a drink served neat, since the crushed ice will chill and dilute it.
4. After pouring, add crushed ice to about four-fifths full, stir or swizzle for a few seconds, then pack with ice, mounding it above the rim like a snow cone. The result: a glass full of ice to the bottom, with even distribution of liquid and a cone of ice above the wash line.
