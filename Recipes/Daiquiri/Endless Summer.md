# Endless Summer
## Root
[[Daiquiri]]

## Ingredients
- 1 oz [[Cimarron blanco tequila]] #core
- 1 oz [[Dolin Blanc]] #core
- 1/4 oz [[Giffard Creme de Pamplemousse]] #seasoning
- 1/4 oz [[Giffard blue curacao]] #seasoning
- 3/4 oz fresh [[Pineapple Juice]] #balance
- 3/4 oz fresh [[Lime Juice]] #balance
- 1/4 oz [[Simple Syrup]] (p45) #balance
- Garnish: 1 [[Grapefruit]] wedge #seasoning

## Your Bar
```dataviewjs
await dv.view("Views/recipe-status");
```

## Method
Shake all the ingredients with ice, then strain into a Collins glass filled with ice cubes. Garnish with the grapefruit wedge.
