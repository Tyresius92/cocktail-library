# Fizzes (pp. 140-141)

Fizzes are basically Collins-style drinks served without ice. Most commonly gin, but any spirit works.

- Egg white: when shaken, its tightly packed proteins unfold and trap air, forming bubbles. Topping with seltzer adds frothiness as the proteins wrap around the CO2 bubbles for a light, fluffy foam.
- Method: dry shake everything except the seltzer, shake again with ice, double strain into a fizz glass, then slowly add seltzer, tapping the bottom of the glass on a flat surface to settle the foam, until a white puck of foam forms on top.

## Cocktails
- [[Silver Fizz]]: simple fizz plus egg white
- [[Ramos Gin Fizz]]: Silver Fizz plus heavy cream and orange flower water; shaken 5 minutes (tradition says 10)
