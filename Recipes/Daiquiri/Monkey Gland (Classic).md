# Monkey Gland (Classic)
## Root
[[Daiquiri]]

## Ingredients
- 2 oz [[Plymouth gin]] #core
- 1 oz fresh [[Orange Juice]] #balance
- 1 tsp [[House Grenadine]] #balance
- 2 dashes [[Pernod absinthe|Pernod Absinthe]] #seasoning

## Method
Shake all the ingredients with ice, then strain into a chilled coupe. No garnish.

## Notes
Classic recipe, shown as the "before" for the clarified juice experiment. The authors call it "a big glass of BS" because it lacks balance: orange juice, high in sugar and low in acid, mutes the gin and dulls the grenadine, and has a homogenizing effect that tends to make drinks boring. Not irreparable; see the clarified version.
