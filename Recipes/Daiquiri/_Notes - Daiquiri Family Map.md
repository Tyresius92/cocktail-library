# The Daiquiri Family Map (p135)

Daiquiri root: 1 3/4 oz Cana Brava rum, 1/4 oz La Favorite rhum agricole blanc, 1 oz fresh lime juice, 3/4 oz simple syrup, lime wedge garnish ([[Ideal Daiquiri]]). Spokes: rum (core), lime juice (acid), sweetener.

- Experimenting with the core: [[Fresh Gimlet]], [[Amaretto Sour]] ([[_Notes - Daiquiri - Experimenting with the Core]])
- Experimenting with the balance: [[Pisco Sour]], [[Hemingway Daiquiri]] (offshoots [[High Five]]), [[Cat Video]] (offshoot of Pisco Sour) ([[_Notes - Daiquiri - Experimenting with the Balance]])
- Variations ([[_Notes - Daiquiri Variations]]): [[Southside]] (offshoots [[Smoke and Mirrors]], [[Smokescreen]], [[Kentucky Maid]]), [[Pompadour]], [[Whiskey Sour]], [[Bee's Knees]], [[Pink Lady]], [[Jack Rose]], [[Brown Derby]], [[Boukman Daiquiri]]
