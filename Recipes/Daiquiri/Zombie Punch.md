# Zombie Punch
## Root
[[Daiquiri]]

## Ingredients
- 1 1/4 oz [[Appleton Signature Blend rum]] #core
- 1 1/4 oz [[Ron del Barrilito Three Stars|Ron del Barrilito 3-Star rum]] #core
- 3/4 oz [[Hamilton Demerara 151 Overproof rum]] #core
- 1/2 oz [[Donn's Mix No. 1]] #balance
- 3/4 oz fresh [[Lime]] juice #balance
- 1/2 oz [[Tailor Velvet Falernum]] #balance
- 1 tsp [[House Grenadine]] #balance
- 2 dashes [[Pernod absinthe|Pernod Absinthe]] #seasoning
- Garnish: [[Mint]] sprig, 1 cherry, 1 orange slice, 1 parasol, 1 pineapple wedge #seasoning

## Your Bar
```dataviewjs
await dv.view("Views/recipe-status");
```

## Method
Shake all the ingredients with ice, then strain into a zombie-size tiki mug filled with crushed ice. Garnish with the mint sprig, cherry, orange slice, parasol and pineapple wedge.

## Notes
Don the Beachcomber, 1934. A famous Daiquiri variation, introduced in the glassware sidebar (p127). Based on former Death & Co head bartender Brian Miller's reworked recipe (full version in the authors' first book, *Death & Co: Modern Classic Cocktails*); this homage backs off the booze slightly and tweaks the rum mix. The key is a mix of a Jamaican rum, an aged Spanish-style rum, and aged 151 rum ("firewater"). Highly potent, drink with caution.
