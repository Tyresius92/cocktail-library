# Dark and Stormy
## Root
[[Daiquiri]]

## Ingredients
- 2 oz cold [[Seltzer]] #balance
- 2 oz [[Gosling's Black Seal]] #core
- 3/4 oz fresh [[Lime Juice]] #balance
- 1 oz [[House Ginger Syrup]] #balance
- Garnish: 1 [[Lime]] wheel and 1 piece of [[Candied Ginger]] on a skewer #seasoning

## Method
Pour the seltzer into a Collins glass. Short shake the remaining ingredients with ice for about 5 seconds, then strain into the glass. Fill the glass with ice cubes and garnish with the lime wheel and candied ginger.

## Notes
Classic. In the [[Moscow Mule]] the vodka core keeps the drink focused on ginger, lime and fizz. With a more flavorful spirit the proportions may need adjusting: to balance the rich molasses flavor of Gosling's Black Seal, this version ups both the ginger syrup and the lime juice. Like the Mule, traditionally made with ginger beer.
