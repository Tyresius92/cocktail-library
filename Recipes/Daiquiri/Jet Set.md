# Jet Set
## Root
[[Daiquiri]]

## Ingredients
- 1 oz [[Clear Creek pear brandy]] #core
- 1/2 oz [[Clear Creek 2-year apple brandy]] #core
- 1 tsp [[Giffard Menthe-Pastille]] #seasoning
- 1 dash [[Absinthe]] #seasoning
- 3/4 oz fresh [[Lime]] juice #balance
- 3/4 oz [[Simple Syrup]] (p45) #balance
- Garnish: 1 mint sprig #seasoning

## Your Bar
```dataviewjs
await dv.view("Views/recipe-status");
```

## Method
Shake all the ingredients with ice, then strain into a double Old-Fashioned glass over 1 large ice cube. Garnish with the mint sprig.
