# Whiskey Sour
## Root
[[Daiquiri]]

## Ingredients
- 2 oz [[Elijah Craig Small Batch Bourbon]] #core
- 3/4 oz fresh [[Lemon Juice]] #balance
- 3/4 oz [[Simple Syrup]] #balance
- Garnish: 1 [[Lemon]] wedge #seasoning


## Method
Shake all the ingredients with ice, then strain into a double Old-Fashioned glass over 1 large ice cube. Garnish with the lemon wedge.

## Notes
Classic. Aged spirits like bourbon paired with lime juice often give unpleasant results: try a [[Basic Sour]] with whiskey and lime and you'll see. The vanilla, spice and tannins that make bourbon tasty don't play well with the high acidity, astringency and flavor of lime, which is why classic recipes like this call for lemon.
