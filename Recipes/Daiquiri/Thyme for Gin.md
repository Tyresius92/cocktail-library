# Thyme for Gin
## Root
[[Daiquiri]]

## Ingredients
- 2 oz [[Citadelle Jardin d'Ete gin]] #core
- 3/4 oz [[Homemade thyme liqueur]] #balance
- 3/4 oz fresh [[Lemon Juice]] #balance
- 3/4 oz [[Agave Syrup]] #balance

## Your Bar
```dataviewjs
await dv.view("Views/recipe-status");
```

## Method
Shake all the ingredients with ice, then strain into a rocks glass over fresh ice.

## Notes
Personal recipe (not from the book). Same skeleton as the [[Bee's Knees]]: gin, lemon and a sweetener, with the thyme liqueur adding herbal sweetness on top of the agave.

⚠️ Reconstructed from memory about two months after the fact. It definitely had all of these ingredients, but the ratios could very easily be wrong. Note that the thyme liqueur and agave are both sweet, so 3/4 oz of each may be too much sweetener; try pulling one back first.
