# Touch and Go
## Root
[[Daiquiri]]

## Ingredients
- 1/2 [[Mandarin Orange]], cut into 4 pieces #balance
- 3/4 oz [[Basil Stem Syrup]] #balance
- 1 1/2 oz [[Espolon blanco tequila]] #core
- 1 tsp [[Del Maguey Vida mezcal]] #core
- 3/4 oz fresh [[Lemon]] juice #balance
- Garnish: 1 [[Basil]] leaf #seasoning

## Your Bar
```dataviewjs
await dv.view("Views/recipe-status");
```

## Method
In a shaker, gently muddle the orange with the basil syrup. Add the remaining ingredients and shake with ice, then dump (no straining) into a double Old-Fashioned glass. Garnish with the basil leaf.

## Notes
Alex Day, 2015. Muddling draws out flavor from less acidic citrus like mandarin oranges, which have intensely aromatic peels and sweet juice. A cross between a tequila sour and a [[Caipirinha]] that's balanced even without muddled citrus; the mandarin adds a layer of vibrant flavor.
