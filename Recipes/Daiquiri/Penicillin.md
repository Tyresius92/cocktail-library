# Penicillin
## Root
[[Daiquiri]]

## Ingredients
- 2 oz [[Famous Grouse Scotch]] #core
- 3/4 oz fresh [[Lemon]] juice #balance
- 1/3 oz [[Honey Syrup]] (p45) #balance
- 1/3 oz [[House Ginger Syrup]] (p48) #balance
- Garnish: 3 sprays of Islay scotch ([[Laphroaig 10-year scotch]]) and 1 piece of [[Candied Ginger]] on a skewer #seasoning

## Your Bar
```dataviewjs
await dv.view("Views/recipe-status");
```

## Method
Shake all the ingredients with ice, then strain into a double Old-Fashioned glass over 1 large ice cube. Mist the top of the drink with the scotch, then garnish with the candied ginger.
