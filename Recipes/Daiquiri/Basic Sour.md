# Basic Sour
## Root
[[Daiquiri]]

## Ingredients
- 2 oz spirit #core
- 3/4 oz fresh [[Lemon]] or [[Lime]] juice #balance
- 3/4 oz [[Simple Syrup]] #balance

## Your Bar
```dataviewjs
await dv.view("Views/recipe-status");
```

## Method
Shake all the ingredients with ice, then strain into a chilled glass. No garnish.

## Notes
Classic formula, a starting place for any cocktail with lemon or lime juice. Alcohol strength is matched by a sweet-sour balance; for most people 2 : 3/4 : 3/4 strikes the right note. Tweak to taste: more juice for tarter, more sugar for sweeter, but don't increase both (it would overshadow the spirit). **Sours vs. daisies:** sours rely on a solitary base spirit; daisies include liqueur, which also contributes to the sugar content (e.g. Margarita and Cosmopolitan with Cointreau, covered in the Sidecar chapter, p149). Small amounts of liqueur in this chapter function as seasoning, not as part of the core.
