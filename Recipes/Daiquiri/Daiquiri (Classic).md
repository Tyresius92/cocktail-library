# Daiquiri (Classic)
## Root
[[Daiquiri]]

## Ingredients
- 2 oz [[Cana Brava white rum]] #core
- 3/4 oz fresh [[Lime Juice]] #balance
- 3/4 oz [[Simple Syrup]] #balance
