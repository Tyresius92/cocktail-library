# Daiquiri Variations (p128)

Variations can take many forms so long as there's a core assemblage of spirit, acidity and sweetness. The book explores classic variations and original takes: swapping base spirits (or using more than one in a cocktail), adding flavorful syrups, or seasoning with herbs and bitters.

## Cocktails
- [[Southside]]: gin and mint, a minty cousin of the [[Fresh Gimlet]]
- [[Boukman Daiquiri]]: rum plus a little Cognac, cinnamon syrup
- [[Jack Rose]]: apple brandy and grenadine
- [[Zombie Punch]]: tiki classic with three rums (see [[_Notes - Tiki Mugs]])
- [[Pisco Sour]] and [[Hemingway Daiquiri]]: citrus variations, see [[_Notes - Citrus]]
- [[Tarby Party]]: split-base rum
- [[Bee's Knees]] and [[Pink Lady]]: gin sours (honey syrup; grenadine plus apple brandy and egg white)
- [[Cat Video]]: pisco, singani and Creme Yvette, a Pisco Sour riff
- [[Pompadour]]: split base with Pineau des Charentes
- [[Smoke and Mirrors]], [[Smokescreen]] and [[Kentucky Maid]]: aged spirits bridged to lime with mint, absinthe/Chartreuse or cucumber
- [[Whiskey Sour]]: why aged spirits want lemon
- [[High Five]] and [[Brown Derby]]: grapefruit variations
- Extended family: [[_Notes - Daiquiri Extended Family]] ([[Mojito]])
