# Sours with Sparkling Wine and Beer (pp. 140-143)

Part of the bubbly branch of the extended family ([[_Notes - Collins-Style Drinks]]).

- Sparkling wine changes the balance by adding proof and flavor, so the sour base is reduced: the [[French 75]] uses half the gin and two-thirds of the lemon and simple syrup of a Collins.
- Technique: shake everything except the sparkling wine, strain, pour in the wine, then quickly dip a barspoon in to mix gently.
- Beer and cider vary widely in alcohol, flavor, sweetness and acidity, so base spirit, citrus and sweetener need adjusting ([[Accidental Guru]]).

## Cocktails
- [[French 75]] (root of this branch)
- [[Old Cuban]]: Mojito with bubbles
- [[Ice Queen]]: French 75 meets Old Cuban plus cucumber
- [[The Eliza]]: French 75 with Aviation gin, pear brandy, cassis, lavender bitters
- [[Accidental Guru]]: whiskey, peach liqueur, ginger, wheat beer
