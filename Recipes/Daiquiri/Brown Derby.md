# Brown Derby
## Root
[[Daiquiri]]

## Ingredients
- 2 oz [[Elijah Craig Small Batch Bourbon]] #core
- 1 oz fresh [[Grapefruit]] juice #balance
- 1 tsp fresh [[Lemon]] juice #balance
- 1/2 oz [[Honey Syrup]] #balance

## Method
Shake all the ingredients with ice, then strain into a chilled coupe. No garnish.

## Notes
Classic. With robust aged spirits grapefruit juice acts differently than with lighter liquors: rum and gin enhance its refreshing flavor, while aged spirits draw out its denser flavors and sweetness. The classic recipe has no citrus beyond grapefruit; the authors add a bit of lemon to cut the richness of the syrup, which might otherwise overshadow the brightness of the grapefruit.
