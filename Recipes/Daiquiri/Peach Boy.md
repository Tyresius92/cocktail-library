# Peach Boy
## Root
[[Daiquiri]]

## Ingredients
- 1 1/2 oz [[Krogstad aquavit]] #core
- 3/4 oz [[Mathilde peach liqueur]] #balance
- 3/4 oz fresh [[Lemon]] juice #balance
- 1/2 oz [[House Orgeat]] (p285) #balance
- Garnish: 1 [[Mint Bouquet]] #seasoning

## Your Bar
```dataviewjs
await dv.view("Views/recipe-status");
```

## Method
Short shake all the ingredients with ice for about 5 seconds, then strain into a double Old-Fashioned glass. Top with crushed ice. Garnish with the mint bouquet and insert a straw.
