# Hemingway Daiquiri
## Root
[[Daiquiri]]

## Ingredients
- 1 1/2 oz [[Flor de Cana 4-Year White]] #core
- 1/2 oz [[Luxardo maraschino liqueur]] #balance
- 1 oz fresh [[Grapefruit Juice]] #balance
- 1/2 oz fresh [[Lime Juice]] #balance
- 1 tsp [[Simple Syrup]] #balance
- Garnish: 1 [[Lime]] wedge #seasoning

## Method
Shake all the ingredients with ice, then strain into a chilled coupe. Garnish with the lime wedge.

## Notes
Classic. The book uses it to show how grapefruit juice affects balance. Grapefruit is bitter-tart but less acidic than lime, so simply swapping 1 oz in for the lime of the root Daiquiri would be thin; hence the fairly generous lime. Because grapefruit juice and maraschino both add sweetness, the simple syrup is dialed way back. The drink is about rum, grapefruit and maraschino, but gets its vibrancy from the lime and syrup.

Variability: lemons and limes are fairly consistent, but grapefruits and oranges vary a lot in sweetness and acidity, which matters when grapefruit is a large share of the drink. Taste the juice first. If it's particularly acidic, raise the simple syrup to 1/4 oz instead of 1 tsp; if particularly sweet, leave the syrup out.
