# Pisco Sour
## Root
[[Daiquiri]]

## Ingredients
- 2 oz [[Campo de Encanto Grand and Noble pisco]] #core
- 1/2 oz fresh [[Lemon Juice]] #balance
- 1/2 oz fresh [[Lime Juice]] #balance
- 3/4 oz [[Simple Syrup]] #balance
- 1 [[Egg White]] #balance
- Garnish: 3 drops [[Angostura Bitters]] #seasoning

## Your Bar
```dataviewjs
await dv.view("Views/recipe-status");
```

## Method
Dry shake all the ingredients (no ice), then shake again with ice. Double strain into a chilled coupe. Carefully garnish the top of the foam with the bitters.

## Notes
Classic. A sour that benefits from lemon and lime together: lemon for its mild flavor, lime for brightness. The book finds it isn't nearly as delicious with just one or the other. The pungent lime plays well with the gritty texture of the pisco, and the relative neutrality of lemon keeps the lime from taking over. Photo on p121 (three drops of bitters dotted on the foam).
