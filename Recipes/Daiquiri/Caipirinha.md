# Caipirinha
## Root
[[Daiquiri]]

## Ingredients
- 1 [[Lime]] (6 of 8 pieces, see method) #balance
- 3/4 oz [[Simple Syrup]] #balance
- 1 [[Sugar cube]] #balance
- 2 oz [[Cachaca]] #core

## Method
Cut the lime in half crosswise, then cut each half into quarters. Put 6 of the pieces in a shaker, reserving the other 2 for another use. Add the simple syrup and sugar cube and gently muddle the lime, carefully pressing each piece. Add the cachaca and shake with ice, then dump (ice and lime included) into a double Old-Fashioned glass. No garnish.

## Notes
Classic. The Brazilian Caipirinha uses almost an entire lime, so it's the showcase for [[_Notes - Muddling Citrus]]. An exception to the book's rule never to pour the shaking ice into the serving glass: the ice and muddled lime stay in so the lime keeps flavoring the drink. It can get bitter, but the authors find a pleasant limy flavor builds toward the end.
