# Grapefruit Collins
## Root
[[Daiquiri]]

## Ingredients
- 2 oz cold [[Seltzer]] #balance
- 2 oz [[Beefeater London Dry gin]] #core
- 1 1/2 oz fresh [[Grapefruit Juice]] #balance
- 1/2 oz fresh [[Lemon Juice]] #balance
- 1/2 oz [[Simple Syrup]] #balance
- 4 dashes [[Peychaud's bitters]] #seasoning
- Garnish: 1 [[Grapefruit]] half wheel #seasoning

## Method
Pour the seltzer into a Collins glass. Short shake the remaining ingredients with ice for about 5 seconds, then strain into the glass. Fill the glass with ice cubes and garnish with the grapefruit half wheel.

## Notes
Sam Ross, 2005. Seltzer contains a bit of carbonic acid, which increases the perception of acidity, so the authors often use a 1-to-1 ratio of citrus juice to sweetener in Collins-style cocktails unless they want an extra-tart drink (as in the basic [[Tom Collins]]; when adding other flavors they back off the acid). Grapefruit juice has a balanced sweet-tart flavor, so it is removed from the equation and equal amounts of lemon juice and simple syrup are used.
