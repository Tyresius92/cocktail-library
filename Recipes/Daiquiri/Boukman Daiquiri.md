# Boukman Daiquiri
## Root
[[Daiquiri]]

## Ingredients
- 1 1/2 oz [[Flor de Cana 4-Year White]] #core
- 1/2 oz [[Pierre Ferrand 1840 Cognac]] #core
- 3/4 oz fresh [[Lime Juice]] #balance
- 3/4 oz [[Cinnamon Syrup]] #balance

## Your Bar
```dataviewjs
await dv.view("Views/recipe-status");
```

## Method
Shake all the ingredients with ice, then strain into a chilled coupe. No garnish.

## Notes
Alex Day, 2008. One favorite way to riff on the Daiquiri is to bring another rum or spirit into the core. Here a bit of the rum is swapped for rich, smooth Cognac, and the Cognac's aged character is bolstered with a spiced syrup. Lemon would complement the Cognac and cinnamon and make a decent drink, but lime cuts through them and adds an unexpected astringency that keeps it bright and refreshing. Pictured on p109.
