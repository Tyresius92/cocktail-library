# Moscow Mule
## Root
[[Daiquiri]]

## Ingredients
- 2 oz cold [[Seltzer]] #balance
- 2 oz [[Vodka]] #core
- 1/2 oz fresh [[Lime Juice]] #balance
- 3/4 oz [[House Ginger Syrup]] #balance
- Garnish: 1 [[Lime]] wheel and 1 piece of [[Candied Ginger]] on a skewer #seasoning

## Method
Pour the seltzer into a Collins glass. Short shake the remaining ingredients with ice for about 5 seconds, then strain into the glass. Fill the glass with ice cubes and garnish with the lime wheel and candied ginger.

## Notes
Classic. Famous for good reason: strong ginger bite, unquestionably refreshing. Uses slightly less lime than a typical sour to honor the original (just vodka and ginger beer with a squeeze of lime, more of a Highball than a sour). Ginger's powerful flavor has a zip somewhat like citrus, so the authors often back down on lemon or lime when using it. The map on p135 shows the Dark and Stormy (p140) and Presbyterian (p141) as its offshoots.
