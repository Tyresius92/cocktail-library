# Daiquiri
## Root
[[Daiquiri]]

## Ingredients
- 2 oz [[Rum]] #core
- 3/4 oz fresh [[Lime Juice]] #balance
- 3/4 oz [[Simple Syrup]] #balance
- Garnish: 1 [[Lime]] wedge #seasoning

## Your Bar
```dataviewjs
await dv.view("Views/recipe-status");
```

## Method
Shake all the ingredients with ice, then strain into a chilled coupe. Garnish with the lime wedge.

## Notes
The classic recipe. The Daiquiri is one of many sours (spirit, citrus and sweetener whose sweet and sour elements temper the spirit's strength and flavor). The book chose it as the chapter's focus for its universal appeal and the wide variety of rums to explore. A modest combination of rum, lime juice and sugar, shaken and served up; not the blender or slushie kind.
