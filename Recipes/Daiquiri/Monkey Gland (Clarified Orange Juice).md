# Monkey Gland (Clarified Orange Juice)
## Root
[[Daiquiri]]

## Ingredients
- 2 oz [[Plymouth gin]] #core
- 1 oz clarified [[Orange]] juice (see [[_Notes - Clarifying Juice]]) #balance
- 1 tsp [[House Grenadine]] #balance
- 2 dashes [[Pernod absinthe|Pernod Absinthe]] #seasoning

## Your Bar
```dataviewjs
await dv.view("Views/recipe-status");
```

## Method
Stir all the ingredients over ice, then strain into a chilled coupe. No garnish.

## Notes
Swapping in clarified orange juice and stirring (not shaking) steers the drink toward a more pleasurable balance, with qualities somewhat akin to a Martini. Another possible improvement the book suggests: a few drops of Citric Acid Solution (p298, appendix, not digitized yet). The authors encourage trying the experiment with agar-clarified orange juice.
