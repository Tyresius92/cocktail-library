# Rye Pie
## Root
[[Daiquiri]]

## Ingredients
- 1 1/2 oz [[Rittenhouse rye]] #core
- 1/2 oz [[St. George raspberry brandy]] #core
- 1/4 oz [[Clear Creek Cherry Brandy]] #core
- 1 oz [[House Lemon Cordial]] (p285) #balance
- 1/2 oz [[Simple Syrup]] (p45) #balance
- Garnish: 1 lemon wheel and 1 [[Brandied Cherry]] on a skewer #seasoning

## Your Bar
```dataviewjs
await dv.view("Views/recipe-status");
```

## Method
Shake all the ingredients with ice, then strain into a double Old-Fashioned glass filled with ice cubes. Garnish with the lemon wheel and cherry.
