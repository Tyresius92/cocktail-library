# Malibu
## Root
[[Daiquiri]]

## Ingredients
- 1 oz [[Cabeza blanco tequila]] #core
- 1/2 oz [[Campo de Encanto Grand and Noble pisco]] #core
- 1/2 oz [[Lillet blanc]] #core
- 1/2 oz [[Giffard Creme de Pamplemousse]] #seasoning
- 1 tsp [[Campari]] #seasoning
- 3/4 oz fresh [[Lime Juice]] #balance
- 1/2 oz fresh [[Grapefruit Juice]] #balance
- 1/2 oz [[Grapefruit Cordial]] (p53) #balance
- 3 drops [[Salt Solution]] (p298) #seasoning
- Garnish: 10 [[Toasted Coconut Chips]] #seasoning

## Your Bar
```dataviewjs
await dv.view("Views/recipe-status");
```

## Method
Shake all the ingredients with ice, then strain into a chilled coupe. Serve with the toasted coconut chips alongside.
