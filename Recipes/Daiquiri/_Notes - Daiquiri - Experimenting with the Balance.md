# Daiquiri: Experimenting with the Balance

Condensed from p. 118. The balance is the collaboration between citrus and sugar, with flexibility depending on preference. A Goldilocks experiment (shaker, jigger, strainer, three chilled coupes, 2 1/2 oz fresh lime juice, 2 oz simple syrup, rum; no garnish) shows where your sweet-sour preference falls and how small tweaks turn a cocktail from tasty to delicious. Once you understand the basic sour template you can swap in any spirit (balanced, though not guaranteed delicious), lemon for lime, or a different sweetener; this flexibility is why sours became a huge family. Other citrus is a great way to develop recipes (e.g. the 1920s Hemingway Daiquiri).
