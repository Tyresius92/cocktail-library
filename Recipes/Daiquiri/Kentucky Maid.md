# Kentucky Maid
## Root
[[Daiquiri]]

## Ingredients
- 5 [[Mint]] leaves #seasoning
- 3 slices [[Cucumber]] #seasoning
- 3/4 oz [[Simple Syrup]] #balance
- 2 oz [[Elijah Craig Small Batch Bourbon]] #core
- 1 oz fresh [[Lime]] juice #balance
- Garnish: 1 mint sprig skewered through 1 cucumber wheel #seasoning

## Method
Put the mint in a shaker and top with the cucumbers. Add the simple syrup and muddle, making sure to break up the cucumber skins. Add the bourbon and lime juice and shake with ice. Strain into a double Old-Fashioned glass over 1 large ice cube. Garnish with the mint sprig skewered through a cucumber wheel.

## Notes
Samuel Ross, 2005. Despite the conventional wisdom that lemon is a better partner for aged spirits than lime, well-chosen ingredients can bridge those conflicting elements. Bourbon and lime are bridged by muddled cucumber (adds juiciness like lemon would) and mint (herbaceous seasoning). The authors' go-to "welcome to bourbon" cocktail; they haven't met anyone who doesn't like it.
