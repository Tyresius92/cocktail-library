# Mojito
## Root
[[Daiquiri]]

## Ingredients
- 10 [[Mint]] leaves #seasoning
- 3/4 oz [[Simple Syrup]] #balance
- 1 white [[Sugar cube]] #balance
- 2 oz [[Cana Brava white rum]] #core
- 1 oz fresh [[Lime Juice]] #balance
- Garnish: 1 [[Mint Bouquet]] #seasoning

## Method
In a shaker, gently muddle the mint leaves, simple syrup and sugar cube until the sugar cube breaks apart. Add the remaining ingredients and whip (shake with a few pieces of crushed ice, just until incorporated). Dump into a Collins glass and add crushed ice until about four-fifths full. Swizzle for a few seconds, then pack the glass with ice, mounding it above the rim. Garnish with the mint bouquet in the center of the ice and serve with a straw.

## Notes
Classic. Same proportions of rum, lime and sweetener as the Daiquiri, but served over crushed ice, which makes it a distant cousin. See [[_Notes - Crushed Ice and Whipping]] for the technique.
