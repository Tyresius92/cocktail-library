# The Balance: Citrus Juice

Condensed from pp. 115-117.

## Principles
- Fresh, locally grown citrus tastes better: a lime in Chicago isn't a lime in Thailand. Lemons and limes are fairly consistent, but taste the juice (grapefruit and oranges vary more). Many varieties exist within each type (oranges: bitter vs. sweet; common, navel, blood).
- Structure: outer skin (flavedo) with oil pockets, bitter white pith (albedo), juice segments. Expressing the peel mists oil that floats on top and perfumes the drink.
- Use a zester for garnish or infusions and avoid pith (bitter); any pith on a twist dropped into a drink makes it increasingly bitter over time. With a conical juicer, too much pressure extracts pith bitterness.
- Muddling citrus segments (oranges/mandarins, lemons, limes) gets juice, aromatic peel and pith at once (Caipirinha, p137). Rarely muddle grapefruit (its oils dominate) and remove seeds first.
- Rule of thumb: pair **lime juice with unaged spirits** (light rum, gin, blanco tequila, vodka) and **lemon juice with aged spirits** (bourbon, scotch, Cognac), with many exceptions.

## Grapefruit
Grapefruit juice is fairly balanced but noticeably bitter, so combine it with lemon or lime juice plus a sweet syrup. Taste it first; if acidic add simple syrup, if sweet reduce other sweeteners. Twists add intense perfume but numb the palate: don't rub on the rim; express well above the drink and drop it in, and remove after a few minutes.

## Cheat sheet (p117)
- **Limes:** Persian/Tahitian/Bearss: large, juicy, seedless, best for juice and muddling. Mexican/Key: smaller, more acidic juice, aromatic peels good for syrups. Kaffir/Makrut: astringent juice, but leaves are great for muddling and infusions.
- **Lemons:** Eureka: sweet, tart classic lemon flavor, thick skin good for twists. Lisbon: similar juice, more per fruit, thinner skin. Meyer: very juicy, highly aromatic zest great for infusions and syrups, juice more acidic than Eureka/Lisbon, so good to bolster tartness.
- **Grapefruit:** Ruby Red: juicy, bright acidity and high sweetness, thick red skin for twists. Star Ruby: sweet, slightly tart, darker, thinner skin (better for half-wheel garnish). Duncan: similar sweetness with crisper clarity, white-fleshed.
- **Oranges:** Valencia: sweet juice that works well, thin uniform skin (include some pith in twists for structure). Navel: less vibrant juice, thick skin great for twists. Blood: dark red juice lacking acidity or flavor, aromatic dark orange skin.
- **Other:** Tangerines: complex juice, can sub for orange juice, thin aromatic skin. Clementines: supersweet juice (pair with lemon juice or Citric Acid Solution), thick skin good for infusions, seedless, good for muddling. Mandarins: supersweet; flimsy peel unsuitable for twists but great for syrups and infusions; may have seeds. Satsumas: like clementines, loose skin, great for muddling. Kumquats: very tart juice, edible sweet skin, muddle segments or use whole fruit for syrups. Yuzu: sweet-tart, between lime and mandarin; intensely aromatic skin, used for cordials. Pomelo: like a larger, slightly sweeter grapefruit; potent skin for dramatic twists.
