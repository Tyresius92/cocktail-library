# Pompadour
## Root
[[Daiquiri]]

## Ingredients
- 1 1/2 oz [[Rhum JM VSOP]] #core
- 1 1/2 oz [[Pasquet Pineau des Charentes]] #core
- 3/4 oz fresh [[Lemon]] juice #balance
- 1/2 oz [[Vanilla Lactic Syrup]] #balance

## Your Bar
```dataviewjs
await dv.view("Views/recipe-status");
```

## Method
Shake all the ingredients with ice, then strain into a chilled coupe. No garnish.

## Notes
Tyson Buhler, 2015. Another way to riff on a [[Basic Sour]]: incorporate a low-ABV ingredient as part of a split base, here the French aperitif Pineau des Charentes. The volume of Pineau is higher than if a boozy spirit were swapped into the split base. A balance between the power of rhum agricole, the juiciness of the Pineau and the sweet roundness of vanilla.
