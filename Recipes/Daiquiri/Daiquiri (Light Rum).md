# Daiquiri (Light Rum)
## Root
[[Daiquiri]]

## Ingredients
- 2 oz [[Cana Brava white rum]] #core
- 1 oz fresh [[Lime Juice]] #balance
- 3/4 oz [[Simple Syrup]] #balance

## Your Bar
```dataviewjs
await dv.view("Views/recipe-status");
```

## Method
Shake all the ingredients with ice, then strain into a chilled coupe.

## Notes
Part of the book's three-rum experiment (same recipe, three rums; shake them at the same time). Most similar to the root recipe, and identical to Daiquiri (More Lime Juice) in the p118 experiment. Undeniably refreshing; the star is bright, unmistakable fresh lime.
