# Daiquiri (Less Sweetener)
## Root
[[Daiquiri]]

## Ingredients
- 2 oz [[Cana Brava white rum]] #core
- 3/4 oz fresh [[Lime]] juice #balance
- 1/2 oz [[Simple Syrup]] #balance

## Your Bar
```dataviewjs
await dv.view("Views/recipe-status");
```

## Method
Shake all the ingredients with ice, then strain into a chilled coupe.

## Notes
Isn't very pleasant: even a small decrease in simple syrup makes the cocktail both too boozy and too tart.
