# Muddling Citrus (p137, condensed from the Caipirinha)

Citrus skins are loaded with flavorful oils; they can be expressed from twists or incorporated by muddling. For wedges to garnish, cut top to bottom so they're easy to squeeze. For muddling, cut into pieces that make it easy to extract both juice and skin oils with one even pressing: halve the lime across the middle, set cut side down, slice each half into quarters.

Muddle with sugar to help extract flavor from the skin. A sugar cube is preferred because it cuts into the skin and brings out more oil. Put the sugar cube in a small shaker tin, add the lime and simple syrup, and muddle each piece by pressing down and twisting slightly to flatten the lime and break down the sugar. Stop as soon as all pieces are crushed.
