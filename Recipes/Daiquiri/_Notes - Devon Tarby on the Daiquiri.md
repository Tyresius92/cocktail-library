# Devon Tarby on the Daiquiri (Friends and Family, p123)

Devon Tarby is a partner in Proprietors LLC and has worked behind the company's bars and at The Varnish in Los Angeles. Her drink: [[Tarby Party]]. Condensed:

- The Daiquiri is her favorite drink; always the right one for the moment. Eric Alperin taught her to make a proper one at The Varnish: limes hand-squeezed right before service, hand-carved block ice swapped out every twenty minutes, glassware chilled in a deep freezer.
- It's a merciless cocktail: it demonstrates all the tiny things a bartender must do and reveals any flaws. The hardest part is the shake. It's the drink used to teach shaking technique, and a "Daiquiri shake" is shorthand for the longest shake (see [[_Notes - Shaking to Dilution]]).
- Her personal rules for variations:
  1. It still has to taste and feel like a Daiquiri: brightly flavored, just enough sugar to curb the citrus, but more acidic than other sours.
  2. Serve it in a coupe; poured over ice it ceases to be a Daiquiri.
  3. No more than 1/2 oz of aged spirits, and no overproof rum.
  4. You have to be able to drink it in a few sips.
- Drinking a Daiquiri is a break from complicated, layered cocktails, "like sinking into the couch and watching cartoons".
