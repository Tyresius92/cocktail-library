# Class Act
## Root
[[Daiquiri]]

## Ingredients
- 1 oz [[Pineau des Charentes]] (Pierre Ferrand) #core
- 1/2 oz [[Singani 63]] #core
- 1/2 oz [[St. George spiced pear liqueur]] #balance
- 3/4 oz fresh [[Pineapple Juice]] #balance
- 1/2 oz fresh [[Lemon Juice]] #balance
- 1/2 oz [[Simple Syrup]] (p45) #balance
- Garnish: 1 [[Pineapple]] wedge #seasoning

## Your Bar
```dataviewjs
await dv.view("Views/recipe-status");
```

## Method
Shake all the ingredients with ice, then double strain into a chilled coupe. Garnish with the pineapple wedge.
