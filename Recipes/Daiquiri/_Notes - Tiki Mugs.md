# Glassware: Tiki Mugs (p127)

Introduced via the [[Zombie Punch]], a famous Daiquiri variation.
- Mai Tai-style glass: squat and round, about 16 oz. Good for any sour-size cocktail (4 oz before dilution) served over crushed ice.
- Cooler-style glass: also about 16 oz but taller and narrower, for coolers and swizzles.
- Zombie glass: the biggest and most iconic, up to 20 oz.
- The authors' own all-purpose tiki mug holds 14 oz and is tall like a zombie glass, with a flintlock-toting pirate atop a pile of skulls instead of anything Polynesian.
