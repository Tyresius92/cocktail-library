# Normandie Club Martini No. 2
## Root
[[Gin Martini]]

## Ingredients
- 1 3/4 oz [[Absolut Elyx vodka]] #core
- 1/4 oz [[Linie aquavit]] #core
- 1 oz [[Lustau Jarana fino sherry]] #balance
- 1 tsp [[Giffard Abricot du Roussillon]] #seasoning
- 1 tsp [[White Honey Syrup]] (p286) #balance
- Garnish: 2 to 3 sprays of [[Rosemary Salt Solution]] (p298) #seasoning

## Your Bar
```dataviewjs
await dv.view("Views/recipe-status");
```

## Method
Stir all the ingredients over ice, then strain into a chilled Nick & Nora glass. Mist the top of the drink with the salt solution.
