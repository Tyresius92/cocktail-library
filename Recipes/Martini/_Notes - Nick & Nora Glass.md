# Glassware: The Nick & Nora Glass

Condensed from p. 83. The traditional V-shaped Martini glass is flawed: the deep cone makes the drink slosh out, it's clumsy to hold by the stem (so a hand warms the bowl), and many are far too large (10-12 oz) for a drink with a standard 3 oz measure of booze. Aim for a vessel holding 4-6 oz. The book prefers a **Nick & Nora** glass (named for the sleuths in Hammett's *The Thin Man*): deep curved bowl, often tapering toward the top, less top-heavy, with a thin, delicate lip. Chill the glass first (freezer, or fill with ice water while you mix and dump it before straining); a chilled glass keeps the drink colder. Avoid a warm glass.
