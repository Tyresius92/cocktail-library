# Ideal Gin Martini
## Root
[[Gin Martini]]

## Ingredients
- 2 oz [[Plymouth gin]] #core
- 1 oz [[Dolin dry vermouth]] #balance
- 1 dash [[House Orange Bitters]] #seasoning
- Garnish: 1 [[Lemon]] twist #seasoning


## Method
Stir all the ingredients over ice, then strain into a chilled Nick & Nora glass. Express the lemon twist over the drink, then set it on the edge of the glass.

## Notes
The book's root recipe: the classic pushed in a vermouth-heavy direction (2 parts gin to 1 part vermouth, vermouth collaborating equally with the gin). Plymouth is a crowd-pleasing citrusy core; Dolin dry's alpine herbs match it; the vermouth is boosted to a full ounce because the bars always use fresh vermouth (an open bottle deteriorates like wine). A baseline for customizing: drier with less vermouth, or more herbaceous with more. Orange bitters amplify both gin and vermouth without fundamentally changing the drink. The lemon twist is expressed and rested on the rim, not rubbed around it, because the powerful oils would linger on the tongue and overwhelm the other flavors.
