# Nurse Hazel
## Root
[[Gin Martini]]

## Ingredients
- 2 oz [[Oolong-Infused Vodka]] (p291) #core
- 3/4 oz [[Lustau Jarana fino sherry]] #balance
- 1/2 oz [[La Quintinye Vermouth Royal blanc]] #balance
- 1/4 oz [[Cointreau]] #seasoning
- 1 dash [[House Orange Bitters]] (p295) #seasoning
