# European Union
## Root
[[Martinez]]

## Ingredients
- 1 1/2 oz [[Hayman's Old Tom gin]] #core
- 1/2 oz [[Busnel Pays d'Auge VSOP Calvados]] #core
- 3/4 oz [[Carpano Antica Formula vermouth]] #balance
- 1 tsp [[Strega]] #seasoning
- 1 dash [[Bitter Truth Aromatic Bitters]] #seasoning
- Garnish: 1 [[Brandied Cherry]] #seasoning

## Your Bar
```dataviewjs
await dv.view("Views/recipe-status");
```

## Method
Stir all the ingredients over ice, then strain into a chilled coupe. Garnish with the cherry.

## Notes
Alex Day, 2009. A Martinez variation: a small amount of Calvados softens the Old Tom and adds fruity depth, while a touch of Strega adds complexity, acting much like bitters. All the (pre-Brexit) European friends in one glass.
