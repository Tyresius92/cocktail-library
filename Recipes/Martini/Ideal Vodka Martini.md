# Ideal Vodka Martini
## Root
[[Gin Martini]]

## Ingredients
- 2 1/2 oz [[Absolut Elyx vodka]] #core
- 1/2 oz [[Dolin dry vermouth]] #balance
- Garnish: 1 [[Cocktail Olive]] #seasoning

## Method
Stir all the ingredients over ice, then strain into a chilled coupe. Garnish with the olive.

## Notes
The book's root Vodka Martini. Personal taste matters even more here. It prefers more complexity than ice-cold vodka alone but errs dry and clean: less vermouth so it exerts only a small modifying influence and the vodka stays front and center. Because vodka is such a large part of the drink, it uses one that brings more than proof: Absolut Elyx has a smooth, slightly sweet flavor and softness. The olive adds a hint of salt, which helps blend the flavors.
