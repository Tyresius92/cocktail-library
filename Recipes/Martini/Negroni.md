# Negroni
## Root
[[Martinez]]

## Ingredients
- 1 oz [[Tanqueray gin]] #core
- 1 oz [[Carpano Antica Formula vermouth]] #balance
- 1 oz [[Campari]] #seasoning
- Garnish: 1 [[Orange]] half wheel #seasoning

## Method
Stir all the ingredients over ice, then strain into an Old-Fashioned glass over 1 large ice cube. Garnish with the orange half wheel.

## Notes
Classic. The exception to the rule that spirit-and-vermouth cocktails fail when unbalanced: a deeply bitter drink with a full ounce of Campari. Campari brings lots of proof, so there is less gin, and it finds balance in an equal-parts core where the amaro is a unified part of the core, the gin provides clean structure, and the bitterness is balanced by the richness of sweet vermouth. Variations substitute other high-alcohol liqueurs or amari for the Campari and adjust the balance so they harmonize.
