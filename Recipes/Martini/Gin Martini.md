# Gin Martini
## Root
[[Gin Martini]]

## Ingredients
- 2 oz [[Gin]] #core
- 3/4 oz [[Dry Vermouth]] #balance
- Garnish: 1 [[Lemon]] twist or olive #seasoning

## Your Bar
```dataviewjs
await dv.view("Views/recipe-status");
```

## Method
Stir all the ingredients over ice, then strain into a Nick & Nora glass. Garnish with the lemon twist (expressed over the drink and set on the edge of the glass) or olive.

## Notes
The standard recipe found in most cocktail books: gin paired with dry vermouth, served up, garnished with a lemon twist or an olive. The book starts its Martini exploration here before moving to its own root recipes.
