# Beth's Going to Town
## Root
[[Martinez]]

## Ingredients
- 1 oz [[Highland Park 12-year scotch]] #core
- 1 oz [[Aviation gin]] #core
- 1/2 oz [[Carpano Antica Formula vermouth]] #balance
- 1/4 oz [[Giffard Abricot du Roussillon]] #seasoning
- 1/4 oz [[Ramazzotti]] #seasoning
- Garnish: 1 [[Mint]] leaf #seasoning

## Your Bar
```dataviewjs
await dv.view("Views/recipe-status");
```

## Method
Stir all the ingredients over ice, then strain into a chilled Old-Fashioned glass. Garnish with the mint leaf.

## Notes
Daniel Zacharczuk, 2015. A complex, Martinez-style drink using a soft scotch and a rich, spicy contemporary gin in place of the traditional Old Tom. The sarsaparilla flavors of Aviation marry with the cola notes in both the sweet Spanish vermouth and the Ramazzotti; substituting another brand of any of these gives markedly different results.
