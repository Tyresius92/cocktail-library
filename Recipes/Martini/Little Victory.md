# Little Victory
## Root
[[Gin Martini]]

## Ingredients
- 1 1/2 oz [[Beefeater London Dry gin]] #core
- 1/2 oz [[Absolut Elyx vodka]] #core
- 1 oz [[Root Beer-Infused Cocchi Americano]] (p292) #balance
- 1 tsp [[Orange Marmalade]] #balance
- Garnish: 1 [[Orange]] wedge #seasoning

## Your Bar
```dataviewjs
await dv.view("Views/recipe-status");
```

## Method
Stir all the ingredients over ice, then double strain into a double Old-Fashioned glass over 1 large ice cube. Garnish with the orange wedge.
