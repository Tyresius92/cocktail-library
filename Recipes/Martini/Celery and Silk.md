# Celery and Silk
## Root
[[Gin Martini]]

## Ingredients
- 1 1/2 oz [[Beefeater London Dry gin]] #core
- 1/2 oz [[Linie aquavit]] #core
- 1 oz [[Celery Root-Infused Dolin Blanc]] (p288) #balance
- 2 dashes [[Miracle Mile Celery Bitters]] #seasoning
