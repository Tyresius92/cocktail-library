# Martini: Experimenting with the Balance

The Martini's core is a base spirit plus an aromatized (or fortified) wine, and its proportions are flexible. Changing the vermouth or swapping in another wine changes proof, sweetness and flavor, so adjust the rest to accommodate.

## Cocktails
- [[Martini (Very Dry)]]: 2 1/2 : 1/4, mostly gin
- [[Martini (Our Root Recipe)]]: 2 : 1, gin and vermouth married
- [[Martini (Wet)]]: 1 1/2 : 1 1/2, herbal and light
- [[Normandie Club Martini No. 1]]: fino sherry in place of vermouth, honey for body
- [[Little Victory]]: root beer-infused Cocchi Americano with orange marmalade
