# Negroni Family

Equal-parts spirit, sweet vermouth and a bitter amaro (Campari) as a unified core. Variations substitute other high-alcohol liqueurs or amari for Campari, swap the base spirit, or split the vermouths, adjusting so the whole stays balanced.

## Cocktails
- [[Negroni]]: the classic
- [[Strawberry Negroni]]: clarified strawberry syrup and cacao nib tincture
- [[White Negroni]]: Suze and Dolin blanc in place of Campari and sweet vermouth
- [[La Rosita]]: tequila, split vermouths, Campari
- [[Boulevardier]]: bourbon Negroni
- [[Old Pal]]: rye, dry vermouth, Campari
- [[Abbot Kinney]]: low-ABV spritz-like gin, blanc vermouth, Suze, St-Germain, verjus
