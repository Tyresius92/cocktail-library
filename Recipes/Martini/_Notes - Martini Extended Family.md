# The Martini Extended Family

From p. 84. The Manhattan likely predates the Martini, but the book built the chapter on the Martini because a Manhattan's bolder ingredients make subtle changes harder to perceive. From the Manhattan the family branches out: the Martinez adds a small amount of highly flavorful liqueur; further flavors (Bigallet China-China Amer in the Brooklyn) lead to the Negroni, a bitter-and-sweet favorite with many variations; and the low-ABV Bamboo swaps the high-proof spirit for sherry at its core.
