# Normandie Club Martini No. 1
## Root
[[Vodka Martini]]

## Ingredients
- 2 oz [[Aylesbury Duck vodka]] #core
- 1 oz [[Alexander Jules fino sherry]] #balance
- 1 tsp [[White Honey Syrup]] #balance
- Garnish: 3 or 4 sprays of [[Sel Gris Solution]] #seasoning

## Method
Stir all the ingredients over ice, then strain into a Nick & Nora glass. Mist the top of the drink with the Sel Gris Solution.

## Notes
Devon Tarby and Alex Day, 2015. Fino sherry replaces the vermouth, an example of how a fairly simple change to the balance calls for further adjustments: because fino is so dry, more is used than the vermouth in the root [[Ideal Vodka Martini]], and a bit of honey syrup adds body. Sherry is a more delicate modifier, so this showcases the subtle texture of a wheat-based vodka that vermouth would overshadow. Finished with a spritz of mineral-rich sea salt solution for briny aroma.
