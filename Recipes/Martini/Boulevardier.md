# Boulevardier
## Root
[[Negroni]]

## Ingredients
- 1 1/2 oz [[Elijah Craig Small Batch Bourbon]] #core
- 3/4 oz [[Carpano Antica Formula vermouth]] #balance
- 3/4 oz [[Campari]] #seasoning
- Garnish: 1 [[Brandied Cherry]] #seasoning

## Your Bar
```dataviewjs
await dv.view("Views/recipe-status");
```

## Method
Stir all the ingredients over ice, then strain into a chilled coupe. Garnish with the cherry.

## Notes
Classic, 1920s (Harry MacElhone, Barflies and Cocktails; Harry's New York Bar, Paris). The original called for equal portions; over the years the ratio shifted in favor of whiskey. Following the Martini orthodoxy, using more bourbon and less Campari and vermouth lets the whiskey assert itself.
