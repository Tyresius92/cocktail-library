# Abbot Kinney
## Root
[[Negroni]]

## Ingredients
- 3/4 oz [[Fords gin]] #core
- 1 1/2 oz [[Dolin Blanc]] #balance
- 1/4 oz [[Suze]] #seasoning
- 1/4 oz [[St-Germain]] #seasoning
- 1/4 oz [[Fusion Napa Valley verjus blanc]] #seasoning
- 2 dashes [[Miracle Mile Celery Bitters]] #seasoning
- Garnish: 1 [[Celery Stalk|celery]] ribbon #seasoning

## Your Bar
```dataviewjs
await dv.view("Views/recipe-status");
```

## Method
Stir all the ingredients over ice, then strain into a chilled Nick & Nora glass. Twist the celery ribbon into a spiral and balance it on the rim of the glass.

## Notes
Devon Tarby, 2015. Although stirred, it works like a spritz: low ABV and enough acidity to be a refreshing palate cleanser with a meal.
