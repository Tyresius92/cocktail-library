# Six Ways to Season a Martini
## Root
[[Martini (Our Root Recipe)]]

## Ingredients
- 6 oz [[Beefeater London Dry gin]] #core
- 3 oz [[Dolin dry vermouth]] #balance
- Garnish: 1 [[Cocktail Olive]], 1 [[Pickled Onion]], 1 [[Lemon]] twist, 1 [[Orange]] twist and 1 [[Lime]] twist #seasoning

## Method
Stir all the ingredients over ice, then strain into six coupes. Leave the first glass without a garnish, add the olive to the second, the onion to the third, and express the citrus twists over each of the remaining glasses, then place the peels into the drinks.

## Notes
A garnish experiment (makes three Martinis' worth portioned into six glasses; don't drink six full-size Martinis). Beefeater is the perfect middle-road gin: assertive but not off-puttingly flavorful. Findings: the unadorned Martini has little aroma; olive adds a hint of brininess that grows as it sits; onion is similar but stronger; lemon twist is brightly perfumed; orange tastes sweeter; lime clashes and tastes off-putting. After 10 minutes, olive and onion get more savory and the citrus twists turn bitter as oils suffuse the drink; if dipping a lemon twist, remove it after a few minutes.
