# Martinez Family

A small amount of liqueur added to the Martini formula (Martinez: 1 tsp maraschino). The liqueur is the place to start for variations.

## Cocktails
- [[Martinez]]: Old Tom gin, sweet vermouth, maraschino
- [[Brooklyn]]: rye Manhattan riff with China-China Amer and maraschino
- [[Poet's Dream]]: an "improved" Martini with Benedictine
- [[European Union]]: Old Tom, Calvados, Strega
- [[Mea Culpa]]: vodka with two sherries, apricot and China-China Amer
- [[Beth's Going to Town]]: scotch and Aviation gin, apricot and Ramazzotti
- [[Watercress]]: watercress-infused gin and vodka with peach liqueur
