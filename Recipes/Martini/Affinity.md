# Affinity
## Root
[[Manhattan]]

## Ingredients
- 2 oz [[Compass Box Oak Cross]] scotch #core
- 1/2 oz [[Carpano Antica Formula vermouth]] #balance
- 1/2 oz [[Dolin dry vermouth]] #balance
- 2 dashes [[Angostura Bitters]] #seasoning
- Garnish: 1 [[Lemon]] twist #seasoning

## Your Bar
```dataviewjs
await dv.view("Views/recipe-status");
```

## Method
Stir all the ingredients over ice, then strain into a Nick & Nora glass. Express the lemon twist over the drink, then set it on the edge of the glass.
