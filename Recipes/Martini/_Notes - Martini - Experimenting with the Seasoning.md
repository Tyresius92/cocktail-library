# Martini: Experimenting with the Seasoning

The seasoning in a Martini is largely the garnish. The book takes a less-is-more ("Coco Chanel") approach: if a garnish doesn't add anything, simplify. Citrus twists: lemon and orange are best, grapefruit can work, lime rarely does (pungent). Rarely rub the glass rim (oils too potent; orange is the exception); expressing the oil over the top disperses aroma evenly; dropping the twist in further flavors the drink (good or bad, depending). For Martinis: express the twist over the top, then rest it on the rim.
