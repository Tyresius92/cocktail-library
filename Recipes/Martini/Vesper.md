# Vesper
## Root
[[Gin Martini]]

## Ingredients
- 1 1/2 oz [[Plymouth gin]] #core
- 3/4 oz [[Aylesbury Duck vodka]] #core
- 1/2 oz [[Lillet Blanc]] #balance
- Garnish: 1 [[Lemon]] twist #seasoning

## Method
Stir all the ingredients over ice, then strain into a Nick & Nora glass. Express the lemon twist over the drink, then set it on the edge of the glass.

## Notes
Classic. The true core of a Martini is spirit plus aromatized wine, and here two spirits are combined: this works because the balance shifts correspondingly, with Lillet blanc replacing the vermouth. Lillet has similar alcohol but a fruitier quality than vermouth's savoriness, and its flavors are delicate enough that gin alone (even soft Plymouth) would overpower it. Replacing some gin with vodka stretches out the gin's botanicals and lets the Lillet shine.
