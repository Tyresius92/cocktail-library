# Martinez
## Root
[[Manhattan]]

## Ingredients
- 1 1/2 oz [[Hayman's Old Tom gin]] #core
- 1 1/2 oz [[Carpano Antica Formula vermouth]] #balance
- 1 tsp [[Luxardo maraschino liqueur]] #seasoning
- 2 dashes [[House Orange Bitters]] #seasoning
- Garnish: 1 [[Lemon]] twist #seasoning

## Method
Stir all the ingredients over ice, then strain into a chilled Nick & Nora glass. Express the lemon twist over the drink, then set it on the edge of the glass.

## Notes
Classic, from the earliest days of American cocktails. A host of Martini-family cocktails include a small amount of liqueur: here just 1 tsp of maraschino brings fruitiness and astringency that pair beautifully with the vanilla in aged spirits and sweet vermouth. The liqueur is a great place to start with Martinez variations (Benedictine for honeyed sweetness and herbal undertones, or fruit liqueurs like peach, apricot or raspberry).
