# Brooklyn
## Root
[[Manhattan]]

## Ingredients
- 2 oz [[Rittenhouse Rye]] #core
- 3/4 oz [[Dolin dry vermouth]] #balance
- 1/4 oz [[Bigallet China-China Amer]] #seasoning
- 1 tsp [[Maraska maraschino liqueur]] #seasoning
- Garnish: 1 [[Brandied Cherry]] #seasoning

## Method
Stir all the ingredients over ice, then strain into a chilled Nick & Nora glass. Garnish with the cherry.

## Notes
Classic. Another balance strategy: substitute small amounts of amari or liqueurs for part of the vermouth. A riff on the Manhattan with a bit of amaro and a teaspoon of maraschino, but recognizable as a rye-based Martinez variation because of the liqueur. Historically made with Amer Picon (modern bottlings, if you can find them, are less bitter and flavorful), so the book uses the bittersweet China-China Amer.
