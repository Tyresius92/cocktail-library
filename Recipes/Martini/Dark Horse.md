# Dark Horse
## Root
[[Manhattan]]

## Ingredients
- 1 1/2 oz [[Appleton Estate 21-year rum]] #core
- 1/2 oz [[Bordelet Calvados]] #core
- 1/2 oz [[Amaro Nardini]] #seasoning
- 1/2 oz [[Grand Marnier]] #balance
- Garnish: 1 [[Lemon]] twist #seasoning

## Your Bar
```dataviewjs
await dv.view("Views/recipe-status");
```

## Method
Stir all the ingredients over ice, then strain into a chilled Nick & Nora glass. Express the lemon twist over the drink, then set it onto the edge of the glass.
