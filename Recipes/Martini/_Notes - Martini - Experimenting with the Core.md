# Martini: Experimenting with the Core

Swap or split the base spirit (the "Mr. Potato Head" approach), but keep the spirit-aromatized wine relationship intact: any change to the base will probably demand a change to the vermouth to rebalance. Don't push past the limits of balance (too sweet, bitter or powerful), where the substitution's personality distracts from the core.

## Cocktails
- [[Vesper]]: gin and vodka together with Lillet blanc replacing vermouth
- [[Dean Martin]]: some gin replaced with Douglas fir brandy, plus woodsy blanc vermouth
