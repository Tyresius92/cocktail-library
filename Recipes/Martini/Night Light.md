# Night Light
## Root
[[Gin Martini]]

## Ingredients
- 1 1/2 oz [[Campo de Encanto Grand and Noble pisco]] #core
- 1/2 oz [[Dolin Blanc]] #balance
- 1/4 oz [[St-Germain]] #seasoning
- 3/4 oz [[Fusion Napa Valley verjus blanc]] #balance
- Garnish: 1 [[Grapefruit]] half wheel #seasoning

## Your Bar
```dataviewjs
await dv.view("Views/recipe-status");
```

## Method
Stir all the ingredients over ice, then strain into a double Old-Fashioned glass over 1 large ice cube. Garnish with the grapefruit half wheel.
