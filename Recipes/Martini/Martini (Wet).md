# Martini (Wet)
## Root
[[Gin Martini]]

## Ingredients
- 1 1/2 oz [[Plymouth gin]] #core
- 1 1/2 oz [[Dolin dry vermouth]] #balance

## Your Bar
```dataviewjs
await dv.view("Views/recipe-status");
```

## Method
Stir all the ingredients over ice, then strain into a chilled Nick & Nora glass. No garnish.

## Notes
Citrusy and light, with a more herbal focus; the gin bows to the vermouth. The book likes all three, depending on mood.
