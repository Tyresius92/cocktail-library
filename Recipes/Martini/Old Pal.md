# Old Pal
## Root
[[Negroni]]

## Ingredients
- 1 1/2 oz [[Rittenhouse Rye]] #core
- 3/4 oz [[Dolin dry vermouth]] #balance
- 3/4 oz [[Campari]] #seasoning
- Garnish: 1 [[Lemon]] twist #seasoning

## Your Bar
```dataviewjs
await dv.view("Views/recipe-status");
```

## Method
Stir all the ingredients over ice, then strain into a chilled Nick & Nora glass. Express the lemon twist over the drink, then place it into the drink.

## Notes
Classic. Almost identical to the Boulevardier, with dry vermouth instead of sweet and rye instead of bourbon. Teeters toward sweet but is spicy from the rye, with a bright backbone from the French vermouth and a bitter finish via Campari.
