# Rob Roy
## Root
[[Manhattan]]

## Ingredients
- 2 oz [[Balvenie Doublewood 12-year scotch]] #core
- 3/4 oz [[Carpano Antica Formula vermouth]] #balance
- 1 dash [[Angostura Bitters]] #seasoning
- Garnish: 1 [[Brandied Cherry]] on a skewer #seasoning

## Method
