# Brandy Flip
## Root
[[Flip]]

## Ingredients
- 1 1/2 oz [[Cognac]] #core
- 3/4 oz [[Demerara Gum Syrup]] (p54) #balance
- 1 whole egg ([[Whole Egg]]) #balance
- Garnish: [[Nutmeg]] #seasoning

## Your Bar
```dataviewjs
await dv.view("Views/recipe-status");
```

## Method
Dry shake all the ingredients, then shake again with ice. Double strain into a chilled coupe. Garnish with a few grates of nutmeg.

## Notes
Classic. Their favorite Flip is sherry-based, but the Brandy Flip is another popular one. For their version they chose woody, juicy Cognac (works with the egg's fat), cut the brandy from 2 oz to 1 1/2 oz because too much alcohol makes a Flip taste like creamy hooch, and raised the sweetener a bit to make up for the sherry's missing sweetness.
