# Experimenting with the Seasoning (pp262-263)

In Flips an amaro is a powerful seasoning that cuts through the density of egg or dairy. It acts almost like a fully formed cocktail itself (strong, sweet, sometimes sour, always bitter), so adding it to a Flip means readjusting every other proportion. See [[_Notes - Amaro]].

## Cocktails
- [[Barnaby Jones]]: Cynar in a nog; Scotch and maple syrup both reduced.
- [[Jump in the Line]]: nondairy Pina Colada riff, amontillado core with Amaro di Angostura.
