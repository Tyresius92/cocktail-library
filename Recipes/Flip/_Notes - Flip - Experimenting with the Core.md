# Experimenting with the Core (p252-)

Of the Flip's three components (spirit, sugar, egg), the spirit offers the most room to play. Classic books say a Flip can be made with nearly any fortified wine or spirit; in practice there are challenges (see [[_Notes - Flip - Understanding the Template]]).
