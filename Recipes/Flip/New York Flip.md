# New York Flip
## Root
[[Flip]]

## Ingredients
- 1 oz [[Elijah Craig Small Batch Bourbon]] #core
- 3/4 oz [[Tawny Port]] #core
- 1/4 oz [[Simple Syrup]] (p45) #balance
- 3/4 oz [[Heavy Cream]] #balance
- 1 [[Egg Yolk]] #balance
- Garnish: [[Nutmeg]] #seasoning

## Your Bar
```dataviewjs
await dv.view("Views/recipe-status");
```

## Method
Dry shake all the ingredients, then shake again with ice. Double strain into a chilled coupe. Garnish with a few grates of nutmeg.

## Notes
Classic. A more decadent version of the New York Sour (bourbon, lemon juice, simple syrup, red wine float): the lemon juice is replaced by heavy cream and an egg yolk, with bourbon and port as the connecting ingredients. An egg yolk (not the whole egg) is the lecithin-rich emulsifier here; see [[_Notes - Eggs and Dairy]].
