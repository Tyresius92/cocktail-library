# Grasshopper
## Root
[[Flip]]

## Ingredients
- 1 oz [[Tempus Fugit Creme de Menthe]] #core
- 1 oz [[Giffard white creme de cacao]] #core
- 1 oz [[Heavy Cream]] #balance
- 8 [[Mint]] leaves #seasoning
- Garnish: 1 [[Mint]] leaf #seasoning

## Your Bar
```dataviewjs
await dv.view("Views/recipe-status");
```

## Method
Shake all the ingredients with ice. Double strain into a chilled coupe. Garnish with the mint leaf, placing it on top of the drink.

## Notes
Classic. Rich but oddly refreshing, liqueur-based. Dive-bar fare for decades, but quality liqueurs made with real mint and cacao make it worth revisiting. Fresh mint reinforces the minty character.
