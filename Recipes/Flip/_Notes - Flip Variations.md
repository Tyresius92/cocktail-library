# Flip Variations (p266-)

Variations go down a few paths: swap base spirits or fortified wines, remove eggs or dairy, or manipulate the sweetener. Consistent across them is richness and a creamy texture.

## Cocktails so far
- Hot: [[Irish Coffee]] (whipped cream float), [[Tom and Jerry]], [[Almond Orchard]]
- Liqueur and cream: [[Grasshopper]], [[Golden Cadillac]]
- Tropical: [[Pina Colada]] (basically a Flip, not a sour)
- Frozen: [[Frozen Mudslide]] (blended with ice cream)
- Extended family: [[Hans Gruber]], [[Indulge]], [[Saturday Morning Cartoons]]
- Egg and dairy: [[Eggnog]], [[New York Flip]]
- Dairy, no egg: [[Brandy Alexander]], [[White Russian]]
- Nondairy: [[Almond Orchard]], [[Jump in the Line]]
- Technique: [[N2O Ramos Gin Fizz]] (pressurized shaking)
- Seasoned with amaro: [[Barnaby Jones]], [[Bean Me Up Biscotti]]
