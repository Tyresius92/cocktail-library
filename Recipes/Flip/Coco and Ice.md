# Coco and Ice
## Root
[[Flip]]

## Ingredients
- 1 oz [[El Dorado 12-Year rum]] #core
- 1/2 oz [[Olmeca Altos reposado tequila]] #core
- 1/2 oz [[Giffard Banane du Bresil]] #balance
- 1/4 oz [[Caffe Lolita Licor de Cafe]] #seasoning
- 1 1/2 oz [[Unsweetened Coconut Milk]] #balance
- Garnish: 1 [[Banana Chip]] #seasoning

## Your Bar
```dataviewjs
await dv.view("Views/recipe-status");
```

## Method
Shake all the ingredients with ice, then strain into a double Old-Fashioned glass over 1 large cube. Garnish with the banana chip.

## Notes
Mary Bartlett, 2014. Rich, coconut-milk based; filed under Flip by me (no root stated).
