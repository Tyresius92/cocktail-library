# Bean Me Up Biscotti
## Root
[[Flip]]

## Ingredients
- 1 1/2 oz aged rum #core
- 1/2 oz [[Coffee Liqueur]] #balance
- 1/4 oz [[Faretti Biscotti Famosi liqueur]] #seasoning
- 1 oz [[Heavy Cream]] #balance
- 1 whole egg ([[Whole Egg]]) #balance
- Garnish: [[Nutmeg]] #seasoning

## Your Bar
```dataviewjs
await dv.view("Views/recipe-status");
```

## Method
Dry shake all the ingredients, then shake again with ice. Double strain into a small white wine glass. Garnish with a few grates of nutmeg.

## Notes
Trevor Easter (see [[_Notes - Trevor Easter]]). Dessert-style Flip: aged rum, coffee liqueur and biscotti liqueur with cream and egg.
