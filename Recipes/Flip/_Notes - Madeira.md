# Madeira (p251, condensed)

Categorized by age, from rainwater (minimum 3 years in oak) up to older expressions. For cocktails the authors prefer lighter styles, especially rainwater: sweetness between the driest and second-driest styles, a whisper of residual sugar that boosts other ingredients and adds slight fruit. It lightens a cocktail without making it seem diluted.

**Recommended bottles:** [[Broadbent Rainwater Medium-Dry Madeira]] and [[Sandeman Rainwater Madeira]]. Also in your vault: [[Barbeito 5-year rainwater madeira]].
