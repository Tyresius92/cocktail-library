# The Core: Fortified Wine (p244, condensed)

Chapter 2 covered vermouth (fortified and flavored with botanicals). This chapter focuses on unflavored fortified wines: sherry, port, madeira and others. They can act as the core spirit, or be substituted for vermouth anywhere it's used as a modifier. Even a tiny bit adds subtle complexity, such as the briny minerality of fino or the stewed fruit of ruby port.
