# Glassware: Dealer's Choice (p266, condensed)

No single glass is best for the Flip family (hot or cold, modest or very frothy).
- **Cold egg or dairy drinks**: a glass big enough to hold the froth without much headroom, about 7-8 oz; stemmed (hands don't warm the drink); wide rim for spice garnishes. Often a coupe or small wineglass (white or rose); their favorite is the Schott Zwiesel rose glass, which flares slightly at the top.
- **Hot drinks** need a handle: for Irish Coffee and Tom and Jerry (both p268) a clear toddy glass lets guests see the drink. For others, like [[Almond Orchard]], a wooden bowl, coffee mug or teacup adds character, as tiki mugs do for tropical drinks.
