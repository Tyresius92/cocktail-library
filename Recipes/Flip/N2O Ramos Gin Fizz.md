# N2O Ramos Gin Fizz
## Root
[[Flip]]

## Ingredients
- 2 oz [[Plymouth gin]] #core
- 1/4 oz fresh [[Lime Juice]] #balance
- 1/4 oz fresh [[Lemon Juice]] #balance
- 1 oz [[Simple Syrup]] (p45) #balance
- 1 oz [[Heavy Cream]] #balance
- 1 egg white ([[Egg White]]) #balance
- 3 drops [[Orange Flower Water]] #seasoning
- Cold [[Seltzer]] #balance

## Method
Combine all the ingredients except the seltzer in an iSi whipper; add no ice. Seal and shake for 20 seconds. Open, add five 1-inch ice cubes, and seal tightly again. Charge with one N2O cartridge, then shake for 10 seconds. Release the first burst of pressure into a shaker or other vessel, then turn the canister fully upside down and dispense into a large glass. Let settle about 1 minute. Carefully add cold seltzer, pouring it down a spoon so it goes to the bottom of the glass and lifts the foam above the rim. No garnish.

## Notes
A pressurized-shaking take on the [[Ramos Gin Fizz]] (traditionally shaken for ten minutes to beat cream and egg into froth). The N2O whipper gives the fluffiest cocktail the authors have made, so a large glass is needed. Safety: the technique is not endorsed by iSi; the authors always use large 1-liter canisters and never fill more than half full (including ice). Works for any cocktail whose proteins bond around air (egg, cream).
