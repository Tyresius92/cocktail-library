# Flip
## Root
[[Flip]]

## Ingredients
- 2 oz spirit or fortified wine #core
- 2 tsp [[Demerara Sugar]] #balance
- 1 whole egg ([[Whole Egg]]) #balance
- Garnish: [[Nutmeg]] #seasoning

## Method
Dry shake all the ingredients, then shake again with ice. Double strain into a chilled coupe. Garnish with a few grates of nutmeg.

## Notes
The classic template: booze, sugar and whole egg, served cold. The Flip traces to 17th-century England (beer, rum and sugar heated for a warming winter drink); colonial America plunged a red-hot iron poker into it so it frothed, or "flipped". Eventually the poker and the beer dropped out, the drink went cold, and egg was added.
