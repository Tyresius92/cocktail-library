# The Balance: Eggs and Dairy (pp253-255, condensed)

Eggs and dairy add flavor, texture and sweetness to a drink, but are delicate and need careful handling to avoid spoilage or curdling (see p264).

## Egg (p253)
- The egg gives a Flip body and texture, balancing the alcohol. Without it, a Flip is just sweetened spirit shaken with ice.
- **Safety**: the main risk is *Salmonella enterica*, via an infected hen or cracked shells. Alcohol and citrus acid do not reliably kill bacteria. Buy the freshest, clean, uncracked eggs (organic, ideally local), refrigerate, and use them soon. Fresh whites make stiff, stable foam and fresh yolks give deep flavor.
- **Whites**: silky texture and foam in fizzes and sours (Ramos Gin Fizz p141, Pisco Sour p120); stretches flavors and tempers bitterness (Campari) or tannins (tea infusions). Egg white can smell like wet dog from oxidation, which is why they're topped with bitters, nutmeg or cinnamon.
- **Yolks**: less water, more protein, fat and vitamins, plus lecithin (an emulsifier) that bonds spirits and cream into a smooth thick texture, as in the New York Flip (p257). Usually recipes use the whole egg instead.
- **Whole egg**: the classic Flip approach: foamy and flavorful. Also used in the Royal Fizz and nogs.
- Trick: infuse whole eggs in their shell by sealing them with an aromatic like lavender; the white absorbs it.
- Egg drinks need a longer shake and a double strain. See [[Whole Egg]] and [[Egg White]].

## Dairy (p254)
- Adding cream to Flips made them nogs. For cocktails the authors use cow's dairy only, mainly **heavy cream** (35-40% fat): richer, perceived as sweet, less likely to curdle with acid, and carries flavor better. **Half-and-half** (10-15% fat) is useful when a drink uses a lot of dairy, like the White Russian (p267), so it doesn't overwhelm or weigh the drink down. Whole milk is 3.5% fat. Butter is used occasionally.
- Temperature matters: serve cold drinks frosty and hot drinks steaming in a chilled or heated glass, in a small volume so the temperature doesn't drift.
- Use fresh, organic, ideally local dairy. Shake a little longer than usual and double strain.

## Dairy alternatives (p254)
Nut milks best mimic cream's texture and flavor delivery because nuts contain more oils. They're thin, so use more and add a little more sweetener; they taste like their source. Almond milk is most common (mild, versatile): [[Almond Milk]].
