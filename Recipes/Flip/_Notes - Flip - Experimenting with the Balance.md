# Experimenting with the Balance (pp256-257)

Two branches of Flip variations: those without dairy and those with. The dairy branch splits again into drinks with egg and drinks without.

## With egg and dairy
- [[Eggnog]] (whole egg + cream; single-serving version)
- [[New York Flip]] (egg yolk + cream; bourbon and tawny port)

## Dairy without egg
- [[Brandy Alexander]] (cream and creme de cacao)

## No dairy or egg
- [[Almond Orchard]] (hot, almond milk)
