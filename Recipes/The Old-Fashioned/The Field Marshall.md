# The Field Marshall
## Root
[[Champagne Cocktail]]

## Ingredients
- 1 oz [[Tariquet Classique VS Bas-Armagnac]] #core
- 1/2 oz [[Royal Combier]] #balance
- 2 dashes [[Angostura Bitters]] #seasoning
- 2 dashes [[Peychaud's bitters]] #seasoning
- [[Dry Champagne]] #core
- Garnish: 1 [[Lemon]] twist #seasoning

## Method
Stir all the ingredients (except the Champagne) over ice, then strain into a chilled flute. Pour in the Champagne and quickly dip the barspoon into the glass to gently mix the Champagne with the cocktail. Express the lemon twist over the drink, then place it in the drink.

## Notes
Alex Day, 2013. Riffing on a Champagne Cocktail is as easy as riffing on any Old-Fashioned: here the sugar is swapped for a sweet liqueur. Combier is an orange curacao and the "royal" bottling has a Cognac base for a rich backbone. Somewhere between a classic Champagne Cocktail and an Old-Fashioned, made with Armagnac and Champagne instead of whiskey.
