The defining traits of the Old-Fashioned and its extended family of cocktails:
- An Old-Fashioned is spirit driven
- An Old-Fashioned is balanced by a small amount of sweetness
- An Old-Fashioned is seasoned with bitters and a garnish

Root recipes: [[Old Fashioned]] (classic, p3) and [[Ideal Old-Fashioned]] (the book's version, p4). Guest bartender's take: [[Dave Fernie's Old-Fashioned]].

Technique: build in a mixing glass and stir with 1-inch ice cubes until just shy of full dilution (cold and integrated, but the whiskey's bite still noticeable), then strain over one large cube. Underdiluted on purpose, since the drink keeps diluting in the glass. If building in the glass, use ice slightly too large for the glass, stir about 2 minutes, and never let the ice float.

Glass: a 12-14 oz rocks glass with a thick, weighted base, tapering slightly toward the bottom.
