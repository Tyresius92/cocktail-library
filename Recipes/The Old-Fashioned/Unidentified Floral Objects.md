# Unidentified Floral Objects
## Root
[[Old Fashioned]]

## Ingredients
- 1 oz [[Dupont Pays d'Auge Original Calvados]] #core
- 1/2 oz [[Wyoming whiskey]] #core
- 1/4 oz [[Laird's 100-proof straight apple brandy]] #core
- 1/4 oz [[Blume Marillen apricot eau-de-vie]] #seasoning
- 1 tsp [[Demerara Gum Syrup]] (p54) #balance
- 1 dash [[House Orange Bitters]] (p295) #seasoning
- Garnish: 1 orange twist #seasoning

## Your Bar
```dataviewjs
await dv.view("Views/recipe-status");
```

## Method
Stir all the ingredients over ice, then strain into a double Old-Fashioned glass over 1 large ice cube. Express the orange twist over the drink, then gently rub it around the rim of the glass and place it into the drink.
