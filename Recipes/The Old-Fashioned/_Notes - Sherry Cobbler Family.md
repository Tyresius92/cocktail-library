# Sherry Cobbler Family

Cobblers swap the high-proof spirit for a larger pour of low-proof amontillado sherry and replace bitters with muddled citrus, served over crushed ice.

## Cocktails
- [[Sherry Cobbler]]: the classic
- [[Traction]]: lemon and strawberry, sherry with rum
- [[Peeping Tomboy]]: stone fruit and almond, sherry with Cognac
