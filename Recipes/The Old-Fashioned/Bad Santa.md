# Bad Santa
## Root
[[Old Fashioned]]

## Ingredients
- 2 oz [[Cocoa Butter-Infused Absolut Elyx Vodka]] #core
- 1/4 oz [[Giffard white creme de cacao]] #balance
- 1/4 oz [[Giffard Vanille de Madagascar]] #balance
- 1 tsp [[Giffard Menthe-Pastille]] #seasoning
- 1 drop [[Salt Solution]] #seasoning
- 1 small [[Candy Cane]] #seasoning

## Your Bar
```dataviewjs
await dv.view("Views/recipe-status");
```

## Method
Stir all the ingredients over ice, then strain into an Old-Fashioned glass over 1 large ice cube. Garnish with the candy cane.

## Notes
Devon Tarby, 2015. Crystal clear but tastes like minty dark chocolate. Vodka is a nontraditional base, but infusing it with cocoa butter and adding chocolate liqueur gives it the richness and complexity of an aged spirit; salt solution brightens the flavors.
