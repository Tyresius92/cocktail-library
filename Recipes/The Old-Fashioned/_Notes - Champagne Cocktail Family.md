# Champagne Cocktail Family

Swap the whiskey for Champagne and you have a Champagne Cocktail: built in the flute (no ice, no stirring), starting with cold Champagne. The riffs keep it light, with a small amount of spirit, sweetener and seasoning.

**Sparkling wines (p. 28, condensed):** in cocktails sparkling wine adds effervescence, proof, flavor, acidity and sweetness. Champagne is the king but pricey; choose drier (no sweeter than brut). Cremant (esp. Bourgogne and Alsace) is the go-to: similar to Champagne and far more affordable. Cava (Spain; traditional method; apple, pear, lime zest) is another option, with quality levels cava / reserva / gran reserva. Prosecco (Charmat method, fresh green apple and melon) is better as a soft supporting mixer in Italian aperitif drinks like the Aperol Spritz. Lambrusco (strawberry, raspberry, rhubarb) is rare in cocktails but adds juicy complexity and slight fizz.
