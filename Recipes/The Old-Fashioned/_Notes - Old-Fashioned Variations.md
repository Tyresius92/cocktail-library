# Old-Fashioned Variations

In practice the three tactics (core, balance, seasoning) are combined. These drinks play with several elements at once.

## Cocktails
- [[Snowbird]]: split base of rye and apple brandy, St-Germain, celery bitters
- [[Cold Girl Fever]]: a Golden Boy-inspired riff with Islay scotch and raisin honey syrup
- [[Ned Ryerson]]: modest changes: apple brandy and Castilian bitters
- [[Deadpan]]: Cognac and sesame rum, sherry and vanilla liqueur for sweetness
- [[Autumn Old-Fashioned]]: four-spirit core with maple syrup
- [[Bad Santa]]: cocoa butter-infused vodka, minty dark chocolate
- [[Beach Bonfire]]: bourbon and cachaca, pineapple syrup, smoke, served with a pilsner
