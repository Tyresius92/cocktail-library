# Sazerac Family

Sazerac-style drinks are Old-Fashioneds served neat in a glass rinsed with absinthe: the aromatic rinse (or a few dashes of absinthe) is what defines them.

## Cocktails
- [[Sazerac]]: the classic: rye, Cognac, Peychaud's
- [[Cut and Paste]]: apple brandy + Irish whiskey, honey syrup
- [[Save Tonight]]: cherry-infused rye with apple brandy
- [[Bananarac]]: Cognac + rye with banana liqueur
