# Julep Family

Juleps are Old-Fashioneds served over crushed ice in a Julep tin, with a mint bouquet rubbed around the tin for aroma. These riffs keep the format and play with the core spirit, the amaro/liqueur balance, and the herb.

## Cocktails
- [[Mint Julep]]: the classic
- [[Last One Standing]]: Cognac + Jamaican rum, amaro and peach liqueur
- [[Heritage Julep]]: Calvados and pear, amaro, cinnamon syrup
- [[Camellia Julep]]: unaged pear brandy infused with cacao nibs, amontillado standing in for oak
