# Black Forest
## Root
[[Old Fashioned]]

## Ingredients
- 3/4 oz [[Famous Grouse Scotch]] #core
- 3/4 oz [[Brown Butter-Infused Apple Brandy]] (p287) #core
- 1/4 oz [[Clear Creek Douglas fir brandy]] #seasoning
- 1/4 oz [[Cacao Nib Tincture]] (p297) #seasoning
- 1 tsp [[Luxardo Amaro Abano]] #seasoning
- 1 tsp [[Simple Syrup]] (p45) #balance
- 1 drop [[Salt Solution]] (p298) #seasoning
- Garnish: 1 spray of [[Cedar Tincture]] (p298) #seasoning

## Your Bar
```dataviewjs
await dv.view("Views/recipe-status");
```

## Method
Stir all the ingredients over ice, then strain into a double Old-Fashioned glass over 1 large ice cube. Mist the top of the drink with the tincture.

## Notes
Bryan Bruce and Devon Tarby, 2016. Uses a tiny amount of dense amaro beneath the core flavor (see [[_Notes - Amaro]]). Appendix cocktail; root assigned by me.
