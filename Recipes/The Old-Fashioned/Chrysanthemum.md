# Chrysanthemum
## Root
[[Old Fashioned]]

## Ingredients
- 2 1/2 oz [[Dolin dry vermouth]] #core
- 1/2 oz [[Benedictine]] #balance
- 1 tsp [[Pernod absinthe]] #seasoning
- 1 [[Orange]] twist #seasoning

```dataviewjs
await dv.view("Views/recipe-status");
```

## Method
Stir all the ingredients over ice, then strain into a chilled coupe. Express the orange twist over the drink, then gently rub it around the rim of the glass and place it into the drink.

## Notes
Classic. A low-proof Old-Fashioned with no base spirit, sugar-based sweetener, or traditional bitters, served up. It looks like it hails from the Manhattan or Martini family, but the proportions give away its lineage: small amounts of Benedictine and absinthe sweeten and season the herbaceous vermouth core.
