# Golden Boy
## Root
[[Old Fashioned]]

## Ingredients
- 1 1/2 oz [[Raisin-Infused Scotch]] #core
- 1/2 oz [[Barbeito 5-year rainwater madeira]] #core
- 1/4 oz [[Busnel Pays d'Auge VSOP Calvados]] #core
- 1/4 oz [[Benedictine]] #balance
- 2 dashes [[Peychaud's bitters]] #seasoning
- 1 [[Lemon]] twist #seasoning

## Your Bar
```dataviewjs
await dv.view("Views/recipe-status");
```

## Method
Stir all the ingredients over ice, then strain into an Old-Fashioned glass over 1 large ice cube. Express the lemon twist over the drink, then place it into the drink.

## Notes
Alex Day and Devon Tarby, 2013. Scotch and raisins, with the madeira and Benedictine supplying enough sweetness that no extra sweetener is needed.
