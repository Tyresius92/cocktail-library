# Camellia Julep
## Root
[[Mint Julep]]

## Ingredients
- 1 [[Mint]] bouquet #seasoning
- 1 1/2 oz [[Cacao Nib-Infused Pear Brandy]] #core
- 1/2 oz [[Lustau Los Arcos amontillado sherry]] #balance
- 1 tsp [[Demerara Gum Syrup]] #balance

## Method
Build as for a [[Mint Julep]]: rub the interior of a Julep tin with the mint bouquet and set it aside, add the remaining ingredients, fill the tin halfway with crushed ice and stir, churning the ice, for about 10 seconds. Add more crushed ice to two-thirds full and stir until the tin is frosted, then cone more ice above the rim. Garnish with the mint bouquet and serve with a straw.

## Notes
Devon Tarby, 2013. When an unaged spirit is the core, add other ingredients to mimic the flavors oak and time normally contribute. Here a clean pear distillate is infused with bitter cacao nibs and enhanced with the nutty, spiced amontillado sherry.
