# Hot Toddy Family

A toddy is an Old-Fashioned with hot water in place of cold: a bit more sweetener and seasoning, about 4 oz water to 2 oz spirit.

## Cocktails
- [[Hot Toddy]]: the classic
- [[In Hot Water]]: Cognac, espresso liqueur, maple, cardamom bitters
- [[Gun Club Toddy]]: chamomile Calvados, Pineau des Charentes, honey
- [[Heat Miser]]: chile bourbon heated with apple juice and sherry
