# Thick as Thieves
## Root
[[Old Fashioned]]

## Ingredients
- 1 oz [[Hine H Cognac]] #core
- 1 oz [[Zacapa 23 rum]] #core
- 1 tsp [[Coffee-Infused Kalani Coconut Liqueur]] (p290) #seasoning
- 1 tsp [[Marie Brizard white creme de cacao]] #seasoning
- 1 tsp [[Demerara Gum Syrup]] (p54) #balance
