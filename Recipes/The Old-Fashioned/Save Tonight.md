# Save Tonight
## Root
[[Sazerac]]

## Ingredients
- [[Pernod absinthe]] (rinse) #seasoning
- 1 1/2 oz [[Sour Cherry-Infused Rittenhouse Rye]] #core
- 1/2 oz [[Clear Creek 2-year apple brandy]] #core
- 1 tsp [[Demerara Gum Syrup]] #balance
- 2 dashes [[Peychaud's bitters]] #seasoning
- 2 dashes [[Pernod absinthe]] #seasoning
- Garnish: 1 [[Brandied Cherry]] on a skewer #seasoning

```dataviewjs
await dv.view("Views/recipe-status");
```

## Method
Rinse a chilled Old-Fashioned glass with absinthe and dump. Stir the remaining ingredients over ice, then strain into the glass. Garnish with the cherry.

## Notes
Devon Tarby, 2017. The tart bite of cherry-infused rye is complemented by the juiciness of apple brandy, but alone these would be sweet and bland. Just a couple of dashes of absinthe add an anise pop and complexity, a demonstration of the power of seasoning.
