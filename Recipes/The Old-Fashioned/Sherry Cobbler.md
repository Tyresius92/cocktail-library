# Sherry Cobbler
## Root
[[Old Fashioned]]

## Ingredients
- 3 [[Orange]] slices #seasoning
- 1 tsp [[Cane Sugar Syrup]] #balance
- 3 1/2 oz [[Amontillado Sherry]] #core
- Garnish: 1/2 [[Orange]] wheel and 1 [[Mint]] bouquet #seasoning

## Method
In a Collins glass, muddle the orange slices and syrup. Add the sherry and stir briefly. Top with crushed ice and stir a few times to chill, then top with more crushed ice, packing the glass fully. Garnish with the orange half wheel and mint bouquet and serve with a straw.

## Notes
Classic. Replace the high-proof whiskey in an Old-Fashioned with a larger quantity of low-proof amontillado sherry (body and acidity make a strong backbone) and swap the bitters for muddled orange slices, which add sweetness from the flesh and seasoning from the bitter pith and oils, countered by fresh mint. Garnish is open: use whatever seasonal fruit or herbs you have (the bartenders' running joke is the most over-garnished cobbler possible).
