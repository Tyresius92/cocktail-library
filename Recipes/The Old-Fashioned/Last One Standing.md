# Last One Standing
## Root
[[Mint Julep]]

## Ingredients
- 1 [[Mint]] bouquet #seasoning
- 1 oz [[Pierre Ferrand 1840 Cognac]] #core
- 1/2 oz [[Hamilton Jamaican pot still gold rum]] #core
- 3/4 oz [[Amaro CioCiaro]] #balance
- 1 tsp [[Giffard Creme de Peche]] #seasoning
- Garnish: 1 [[Peach]] slice and [[Confectioners' Sugar]] #seasoning

## Method
Build as for a [[Mint Julep]]: rub the interior of a Julep tin with the mint bouquet and set it aside, add the remaining ingredients, fill the tin halfway with crushed ice and stir, churning the ice, for about 10 seconds. Add more crushed ice to two-thirds full and stir until the tin is frosted, then cone more ice above the rim. Garnish with the mint bouquet and peach slice, lightly dust the top of the mint with confectioners' sugar, and serve with a straw.

## Notes
Natasha David, 2014. Cognac and Jamaican rum together make a big, funky core that plays off the bright fruit of the peach liqueur. The amaro acts as a bridge between the two camps, working much like bitters to balance disparate flavors.
