# Hot Toddy
## Root
[[Old Fashioned]]

## Ingredients
- 2 [[Lemon]] wedges #seasoning
- 1 1/2 oz [[Elijah Craig Small Batch Bourbon]] #core
- 3/4 oz [[Honey Syrup]] #balance
- 1 dash [[Angostura Bitters]] #seasoning
- 4 oz boiling water
- Garnish: 2 [[Lemon]] wedges and [[Nutmeg]] #seasoning

## Method
Squeeze the lemon wedges into a toddy mug, then add the remaining ingredients (except the water). Pour in the boiling water, grate nutmeg over the top and garnish with the lemon wedges.

## Notes
Classic. A toddy is an Old-Fashioned with hot water swapped in for cold. Heat raises the perception of alcohol, so the drink is lengthened (about 4 oz water to 2 oz spirit is a good baseline); heat also drives off volatile aromas, which can be off-putting if the proof is too high but lets you play with aromatic spirits (Calvados turns ethereal when heated). Because toddies are heated and more diluted they need a bit more sweetener and seasoning than an Old-Fashioned; the small amount of lemon juice seasons rather than acidifies.
