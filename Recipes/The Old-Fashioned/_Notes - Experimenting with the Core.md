# Experimenting with the Core

Nearly any spirit can be the core as long as the other ingredients support and accent it. The easiest way to improvise is the Mr. Potato Head approach (Phil Ward): take out one thing and replace it with something similar. Three ways to alter the core: swap the spirit (Eagle Rare 10-Year for spicier, Old Grand-Dad 114 for boozier, Old Weller Antique 107 for softer, Elijah Craig Small Batch for balanced); infuse it; or use a fortified wine or amaro instead of a strong spirit. A vermouth core needs less sweetener and more bitters; an amaro core needs less of both, or one eliminated entirely.
