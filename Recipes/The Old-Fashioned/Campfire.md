# Campfire
## Root
[[Old Fashioned]]

## Ingredients
- 1 3/4 oz [[Graham Cracker-Infused Elijah Craig Single-Barrel Bourbon]] (p290) #core
- 1/2 oz [[Cacao Nib Tincture]] (p297) #seasoning
- 1 tsp [[Giffard white creme de cacao]] #balance
- 1 pinch [[Cherry Wood Chips]] (enough to fill the smoker) #seasoning

## Your Bar
```dataviewjs
await dv.view("Views/recipe-status");
```

## Method
Stir all the ingredients over ice, then strain into a double Old-Fashioned glass over 1 large ice cube. Using a PolyScience Smoking Gun, smoke the top of the drink with cherry wood smoke. No garnish.
