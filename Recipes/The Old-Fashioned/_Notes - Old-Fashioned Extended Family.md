# The Old-Fashioned Extended Family

Manipulating the Old-Fashioned template creates whole new categories of drinks. What connects the root recipe and the extended family is that they all focus on a core flavor and contain only small amounts of sweetener and seasoning. The book highlights its versions of some classics, each followed by original cocktails inspired by them (pp. 26-41).

## Families
- [[_Notes - Champagne Cocktail Family]]: [[Champagne Cocktail]] (Champagne as core, built in a flute)
- [[_Notes - Julep Family]]: [[Mint Julep]] (mint in place of bitters, crushed ice)
- [[_Notes - Sherry Cobbler Family]]: [[Sherry Cobbler]] (amontillado sherry as core, muddled citrus)
- [[_Notes - Sazerac Family]]: [[Sazerac]] (absinthe-rinsed glass, served neat)
- [[_Notes - Hot Toddy Family]]: [[Hot Toddy]] (hot water instead of cold)
