# Dave Fernie's Old-Fashioned
## Root
[[Old Fashioned]]

## Ingredients
- 2 oz [[Evan Williams Black Label bourbon]] #core
- 1 tsp [[Demerara Gum Syrup]] #balance
- 2 dashes [[Angostura Bitters]] #seasoning
- 1 [[Orange]] twist #seasoning
- 1 [[Lemon]] twist #seasoning

## Your Bar
```dataviewjs
await dv.view("Views/recipe-status");
```

## Method
Stir all the ingredients over ice, then strain into an Old-Fashioned glass over 1 large ice cube. Express the orange twist over the drink, then gently rub it around the rim of the glass and place it into the drink. Express the lemon twist over the drink, then place it into the drink.

## Notes
Guest bartender Dave Fernie (Honeycut, The Walker Inn, The Normandie Club). His view: don't use top-shelf booze in an Old-Fashioned; the original was a way to make cheap booze taste better. The little nuances (which sweetener, whether you express the orange and discard it or drop it in) are what make it elegant.
