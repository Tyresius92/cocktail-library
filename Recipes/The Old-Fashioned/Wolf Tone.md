# Wolf Tone
## Root
[[Old Fashioned]]

## Ingredients
- 1 oz [[Campo de Encanto Grand and Noble pisco]] #core
- 3/4 oz [[Clear Creek Pinot Noir grappa]] #core
- 3/4 oz [[St-Germain]] #balance
- 1 dash [[House Orange Bitters]] (p295) #seasoning
- 1 drop [[Terra Spice eucalyptus extract]] #seasoning
- Garnish: 1 [[Lemon]] twist #seasoning

## Your Bar
```dataviewjs
await dv.view("Views/recipe-status");
```

## Method
Stir all the ingredients with ice, then strain into a double Old-Fashioned glass over 1 large ice cube. Express the lemon twist over the drink, then place it into the drink.
