# Experimenting with the Balance

Balance bridges the core flavor (whiskey) with the seasoning (bitters). The book prefers just enough sugar to round the rough edges, and uses Demerara Gum Syrup (2 parts demerara sugar to 1 part water, thickened with gum arabic) for body without dilution; plain simple syrup thins the drink out. Sugar isn't the only option: a sweet liqueur or amaro can balance a drink, and aged spirits seem sweeter on their own, so back off to about 1/2 tsp of syrup and go light on bitters.

- [[Chrysanthemum]]: no base spirit; Benedictine and absinthe sweeten and season vermouth
