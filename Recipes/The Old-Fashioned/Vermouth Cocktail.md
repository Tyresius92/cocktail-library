# Vermouth Cocktail
## Root
[[Old Fashioned]]

## Ingredients
- 2 oz [[Carpano Antica Formula vermouth]] #core
- 1/2 tsp [[Simple Syrup]] #balance
- 2 dashes [[Angostura Bitters]] #seasoning
- 1 dash [[Orange Bitters]] #seasoning
- 1 [[Lemon]] twist #seasoning

## Your Bar
```dataviewjs
await dv.view("Views/recipe-status");
```

## Method
Stir all the ingredients over ice, then strain into a chilled coupe. Express the lemon twist over the drink, then set it on the edge of the glass.

## Notes
Classic. Vermouth in the Old-Fashioned template gives a nuanced, low-ABV sipper, and shows that not every Old-Fashioned-style drink is served on ice in a rocks glass.
