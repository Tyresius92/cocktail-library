# Heat Miser
## Root
[[Hot Toddy]]

## Ingredients
- 1 oz [[Thai Chile-Infused Bourbon]] #core
- 1/2 oz [[Luxardo Amaro Abano]] #balance
- 1/2 oz [[Alexander Jules amontillado sherry]] #balance
- 3 oz fresh [[Fuji Apple Juice]] #balance
- 1 tsp [[Medlock Ames verjus]] #seasoning
- 1/2 oz dark, robust [[Maple Syrup]] #balance
- 1 drop [[Salt Solution]] #seasoning
- Garnish: 3 thin [[Apple]] slices on a skewer #seasoning

## Your Bar
```dataviewjs
await dv.view("Views/recipe-status");
```

## Method
Combine all the ingredients in a small saucepan over medium-low heat and cook, stirring occasionally, until steaming hot but not simmering. Pour into a toddy mug and garnish with the apple slices.

## Notes
Devon Tarby, Katie Emmerson and Alex Day, 2015. Much more at play than in a traditional toddy: chile-infused bourbon adds gentle heat without making the drink overtly spicy, and bright apple juice stands in for water, adding another layer of complexity.
