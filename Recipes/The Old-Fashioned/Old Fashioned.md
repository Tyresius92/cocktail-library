# The Classic Old-Fashioned
## Root
[[Old Fashioned]]

## Ingredients
- 1 [[Sugar cube]] #balance
- 2 dashes [[Angostura Bitters]] #seasoning
- 2 ounces [[Bourbon]] #core
- 1 [[Lemon]] twist #seasoning
- 1 [[Orange]] twist #seasoning

## Your Bar
```dataviewjs
await dv.view("Views/recipe-status");
```

## Method
Muddle the sugar cube and bitters in an Old-Fashioned glass. Add the bourbon and 1 large ice cube and stir until chilled. Garnish with the lemon and orange twists.
