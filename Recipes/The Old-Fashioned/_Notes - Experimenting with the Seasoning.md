# Experimenting with the Seasoning

Bitters are either flavoring (add a distinctive flavor, e.g. Angostura) or amplifying (heighten flavors already in the drink, e.g. orange bitters, Xocolatl mole), and sometimes both. Swap in different styles and see what happens. You don't need bitters in every cocktail, and more bitters won't necessarily make a better one: the moment they distract from the base spirit is when you've gone too far. To taste an unfamiliar bitters, try it in cold seltzer, then a drop on the back of your hand.
