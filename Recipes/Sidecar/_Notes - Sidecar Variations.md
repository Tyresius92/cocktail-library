# Sidecar Variations (pp. 182-, condensed)

The Sidecar underscores the importance of balance: too much liqueur overpowers, too much citrus hits like sandpaper. Manipulating any of the drink's three elements affects its balance; the variations get progressively more complex.

## Cocktails
- [[Why Not]]: whiskey + maple syrup for half the Cointreau, sage
- [[Cosmopolitan]]: a Sidecar with citrus vodka, lime and cranberry
- [[Clarified Cosmopolitan]]: the authors' ideal Cosmo
- [[Lily Pad]]: blanc vermouth + aloe liqueur
- [[Pegu Club Cocktail]]: gin Sidecar seasoned with two bitters
