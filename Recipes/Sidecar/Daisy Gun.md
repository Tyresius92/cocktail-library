# Daisy Gun
## Root
[[Sidecar]]

## Ingredients
- 2 oz [[Seltzer]] #balance
- 1 1/2 oz [[Campo de Encanto Grand and Noble pisco]] #core
- 1 oz [[Williams & Humbert Dry Sack sherry]] #core
- 3/4 oz fresh [[Lemon Juice]] #balance
- 3/4 oz [[Clarified Strawberry Syrup]] (p57) #balance
- 1/4 oz [[Cinnamon Syrup]] (p52) #balance
- Garnish: 1 [[Lemon]] wedge and [[Nutmeg]] #seasoning

## Your Bar
```dataviewjs
await dv.view("Views/recipe-status");
```

## Method
Pour the seltzer into a Collins glass. Short shake the remaining ingredients with ice for about 5 seconds, then strain into the glass. Fill the glass with ice cubes, then garnish with the lemon wedge and a few grates of nutmeg.

## Notes
Alex Day, 2013. A Daisy-style drink (split base, sour balance) lengthened with seltzer. Appendix cocktail; root assigned by me.
