# Sidecar: Experimenting with the Balance and Seasoning (Liqueurs)

From pp. 178-179. Swapping a liqueur other than curacao into the Sidecar template is more daunting than playing with a Daiquiri, because liqueurs vary so widely in proof, sugar and flavor. First learn how a liqueur compares to orange curacao: Cointreau is relatively dry and mild but still contains sugar and has a low proof. A liqueur with more proof and sweetness (green Chartreuse in the Last Word) or lower proof and higher sweetness (white creme de cacao in the 20th Century) means you must adjust the core up and the liqueur down to find balance.

## Cocktails
- [[20th Century]]: creme de cacao + Lillet blanc, more gin
- [[Last Word]]: green Chartreuse + maraschino, equal parts
- [[Crop Top]]: Last Word lightened with amaro and pamplemousse
