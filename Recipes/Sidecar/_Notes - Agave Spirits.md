# The Core: Agave-Based Spirits (pp. 163-165, condensed)

The chapter could have been based on the Margarita. Agave spirits are idolized for their honesty and rusticity. *Mezcal* means any agave distillate, so tequila is a type of mezcal. Tequila uses only 100% agave; mixto is inferior. See [[Tequila]].

## Tequila
- **Blanco**: lightest, bright and vegetal; good in citrusy drinks like the [[Margarita (Classic)]]. [[Cimarron Blanco tequila]], [[Pueblo Viejo Blanco tequila]], [[Siembra Azul blanco tequila]], [[Siete Leguas Blanco tequila]]
- **Reposado** (2 months to 1 year): clove, cinnamon, vanilla, slight sweetness; versatile. [[Cimarron Reposado tequila]], [[Don Julio Reposado tequila]], [[El Tesoro Reposado tequila]]
- **Anejo** (1-3 years): pricey, for the occasional Manhattan variation. [[Pueblo Viejo Anejo tequila]]
- **Extra anejo** (3+ years): too expensive for mixing.

## Mezcal
Mostly unaged (blanco/joven); the authors love the Los Amantes reposado. [[Del Maguey Vida mezcal]], [[Nuestra Soledad La Compania Ejutla mezcal]], [[Los Amantes Reposado mezcal]]

## Other agave spirits
- **Bacanora** (Sonora): earthen pit, mesquite; 40-50% ABV; earthy, savory, almost barbecued meat.
- **Raicilla** (Jalisco): above-ground ovens; de la costa vs de la sierra.
