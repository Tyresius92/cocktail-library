# La Valencia
## Root
[[Sidecar]]

## Ingredients
- 1 oz [[Chamomile-Infused Rye Whiskey]] (p288) #core
- 1 1/2 oz [[La Gitana manzanilla sherry]] #core
- 1/2 oz [[Chartreuse]] (yellow) #balance
- 3/4 oz fresh [[Lemon Juice]] #balance
- 1/2 oz [[Simple Syrup]] #balance
- 1 dash [[Angostura Bitters]] #seasoning

## Your Bar
```dataviewjs
await dv.view("Views/recipe-status");
```

## Method
Shake all the ingredients with ice, then strain into a chilled coupe. No garnish.

## Notes
Alex Day, 2008. A riff on the [[Champs-Elysees]] with a split base of dry, salty manzanilla and chamomile-infused rye. The honeyed, herbal yellow Chartreuse is perfect for drawing out the chamomile.
