# Lily Pad
## Root
[[Sidecar]]

## Ingredients
- 5 [[Basil]] leaves, preferably Thai basil #seasoning
- 1/2 oz [[Simple Syrup]] #balance
- 1 1/2 oz [[Bombay gin]] #core
- 1/2 oz blanc vermouth ([[Dolin Blanc]]) #core
- 1/2 oz [[Chareau California Aloe Liqueur]] #balance
- 3/4 oz fresh [[Lemon Juice]] #balance
- 1 dash [[St. George absinthe]] #seasoning
- 1 drop [[Salt Solution]] #seasoning
- Garnish: 1 [[Basil]] leaf, preferably Thai basil #seasoning

## Method
In a cocktail shaker, gently muddle the basil leaves and simple syrup. Shake all the ingredients with ice, then double strain into a chilled coupe. Garnish with the basil leaf.

## Notes
Devon Tarby, 2015. Blanc vermouth acts like a liqueur in shaken cocktails thanks to its sugar, here partnering with the aloe liqueur as the liqueur element. Chareau is so dry that the sweetener is increased to add body. Salt solution on p298.
