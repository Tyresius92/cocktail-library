# Sidecar
## Root
[[Sidecar]]

## Ingredients
- 1 1/2 oz [[Cognac]] #core
- 1 oz [[Cointreau]] #balance
- 3/4 oz fresh [[Lemon]] juice #balance
- Garnish: 1 orange twist #seasoning

## Method
Shake all the ingredients with ice, then strain into a chilled coupe. Express the orange twist over the drink, then gently rub it around the rim of the glass and place it into the drink.

## Notes
The classic recipe (p151). Rumored to have been created by an American army captain stationed in Paris during World War I who rode around the city in a motorcycle sidecar (the usual caveat: plenty of other origin stories). The likely predecessor is the Brandy Crusta, which first showed up in Jerry Thomas's 1862 *The Bar-Tender's Guide* with a large proportion of brandy and minuscule amounts of lemon juice and curacao (plus a couple of dashes of bitters). Some older French recipes call for equal parts Cognac and Cointreau, but few modern bartenders do that, since the Cointreau would overshadow the fruity qualities of the Cognac.
