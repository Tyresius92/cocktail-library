# Sidecar (Boozy)
## Root
[[Sidecar]]

## Ingredients
- 2 oz [[Cognac]] (preferably VSOP) #core
- 3/4 oz [[Cointreau]] #balance
- 3/4 oz fresh [[Lemon]] juice #balance

## Method
Shake all the ingredients with ice, then double strain into a chilled coupe. No garnish.

## Notes
Step two of the balance experiment (p155). The first thing you taste is booze: it packs a punch, and alcohol dominates, making a harsh, bitter drink. A widespread problem in cocktail making: ignoring the proof of higher-ABV modifying ingredients like Cointreau and simply substituting them for a sweetener without adjusting ratios. At 80 proof Cointreau is as boozy as most base spirits; its sweetness curbs some of the lemon but the result is far too dry. Hence the classic recipe uses less Cognac and slightly more Cointreau. Make the classic again for a side-by-side comparison.
