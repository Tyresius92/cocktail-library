# Exploring Technique: Rimming (pp. 180, condensed)

The authors rarely rim cocktails because a properly balanced drink doesn't need extra sugar or salt, but classics like the Paloma, Margarita, Bloody Mary and Sidecar are traditionally rimmed and they honor that heritage.

- **Sizes**: skinny and fat. Salt, sugar and standard rims cover about half the rim with a 1/2-inch swath; pungent ingredients (chile powder, spices) get a narrower rim. Only half the glass is rimmed so the guest can customize each sip or avoid the rim.
- **How**: wet the *outside* of the rim with a citrus wedge, ideally the same citrus as the drink (lemon if there's no citrus juice; its flavor is more neutral). Salt and/or sugar balance each other with citrus. Alternates: coconut oil, sugar syrup. Roll the glass in an even layer of the rimming ingredient on a flat plate, then hold upside down and tap off the excess. Anything on the inside falls into the drink.
- **Sidecar rim**: classically sugared, probably for the inherent dryness (and sugar once being a luxury). The book's spec is balanced enough to omit it. If you do, use fine-grain cane sugar; demerara and muscovado are too chunky and flavorful.

- **Salt rims** (p181): used more often than sugar rims, but often the same effect comes from a few drops of [[Salt Solution]] (p298). Salinity lifts bright fruit liqueurs, tames bitter ingredients, enhances savory/mineral flavors in sherry and gin, and melds dense amari and chocolate/caramel notes. Usually kosher salt; flaky sea salt for crunch.
- **Combined rims**: add aromatics such as black pepper and other pungent spices, or dehydrated citrus powder (dry citrus slices in a dehydrator, then pulverize). They should enhance what's in the glass, not overpower it.
- **Favorite rims**: specific salts (pink Himalayan, kosher, sea salt, sel gris); smoked salt; salt + pepper; salt + fennel pollen; salt + ground celery seed; cayenne or other ground chiles; cayenne + salt; sugar + ground cinnamon; dehydrated and pulverized fruit (strawberry, raspberry); dehydrated and pulverized citrus (lemon, lime, grapefruit); pink Himalayan salt + pulverized dehydrated pink grapefruit + citric acid + coconut oil.
