# Verjus (p188, condensed)

French for "green juice": the unfermented juice of unripe wine grapes. Fragile; once opened, use within a few days even if sealed and refrigerated. Red and white versions exist; the authors favor the more versatile white. Check the production date: over two years old lacks vibrancy. Adds acidity and flavor; occasionally used in a fairly large proportion for a bright acidic backbone, especially in stirred sour drinks, giving a clear, Martini-like cocktail with an unexpected, refreshing profile.

Bottles: [[Fusion Napa Valley verjus blanc]], [[Medlock Ames verjus]]. Used in: [[Four to the Floor]].
