# Corpse Reviver No. 2
## Root
[[Sidecar]]

## Ingredients
- 3/4 oz London dry gin ([[Gin]]) #core
- 3/4 oz [[Lillet Blanc]] #core
- 3/4 oz [[Cointreau]] #balance
- 3/4 oz fresh [[Lemon Juice]] #balance
- 2 dashes absinthe ([[Pernod absinthe]]) #seasoning

```dataviewjs
await dv.view("Views/recipe-status");
```

## Method
Shake all the ingredients with ice, then double strain into a chilled coupe. No garnish.

## Notes
Classic (pictured p173). A lower-booze member of the family: a split base of gin and Lillet blanc replaces the Cognac. The authors call it highly democratic: harmonious balance with each ingredient pulling its weight. Lillet mirrors the others in proof, sweetness and acidity, but is softer, so it turns down the volume of the louder ingredients and lets them coexist.
