# Glassware: The Coupe (p181, condensed)

The authors' favorite glass for the Sidecar and other shaken drinks served up, many stirred cocktails and the occasional Champagne Cocktail: a wide, shallow bowl with sides tapering toward the top.

- Standard all-purpose coupes hold only 5 1/2 oz, so a standard cocktail fills them completely and sloshes in transport, and some drink (often the white froth on a Sidecar or Daiquiri) is left in the shaker.
- They now use coupes holding about 7 oz, leaving room for bubbly ingredients and flourishes. Favorite: the Retro Coupe Glass by Urban Bar (7 oz, durable).
- Pre-chill coupes: keeps the drink colder longer, and sipping from an ice-cold rim improves the experience.
- No coupes? Old-Fashioned glasses or small wineglasses work. "We'd rather drink a Sidecar in any vessel than not one."
