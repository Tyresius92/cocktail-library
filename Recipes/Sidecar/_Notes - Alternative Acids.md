# Next-Level Technique: Using Alternative Acids (pp. 192-195, condensed)

Lemon and lime juice are the backbone of most cocktails, but acidity can come from other sources. Alternative acids also cut citrus waste (a fraction of the ecological impact), though flavor differs. For powdered acids, use a precision gram scale accurate to 0.01 g. Handle safely: acids degrade materials and maybe your skin; wear gloves; store in glass.

## Three uses
- **Replacement** (p192): omit lemon/lime and add enough acid for equivalent acidity. Treat these as a different expression of a cocktail, like the Clarified Daiquiri (p145); useful for stirred versions of normally shaken, frothy drinks. Experiment: [[Sidecar (Classic)]] vs [[Sidecar (with Citric Acid)]].
- **Seasoning** (p192-193): mix powdered acids into solutions and add by the drop. Phosphoric acid (the acid in soft drinks) adds acidity without flavor, great for Highball variations. A couple of drops of citric solution lift orange juice: [[Blood and Sand]] vs [[Blood and Sand (Ours)]].
- **Manipulation** (p193): a pinch of powdered acid in syrups draws out flavors (citric acid in Raspberry Syrup, p51); acids compensate for variable fruit like oranges.

## The acids (p194-195)
- **Phosphoric**: odorless, flavorless tartness. Dilution is tricky; use a ready-made product (Extinct Acid Phosphate, Darcy O'Neil). Use a couple of drops, or 1/2-1 tsp as solo backbone (never over 1 tsp). [[Phosphoric Acid Solution]]
- **Citric**: lemon/lime's acid. 100g water + 25g powder. 1 tsp solution is roughly 3/4 oz juice acidity; better used a few drops at a time to perk up flabby drinks (Blood and Sand) or boost acidity of syrups for carbonated cocktails. [[Citric Acid Solution]], [[Citric Acid]]
- **Lactic**: creamy texture without dairy. 90g water + 10g acid. Syrups: [[Vanilla Lactic Syrup]], [[Strawberry Cream Syrup]]. [[Lactic Acid Solution]], [[Lactic Acid]]
- **Malic**: green apple tartness, more potent than citric. 100g water + 10g acid. House Grenadine (p47), Apple Pop (p222). [[Malic Acid Solution]], [[Malic Acid]]
- **Tartaric**: grape acid with a drying effect; used with lactic as [[Champagne Acid Solution]] (3g + 3g + 94g water; Celebrate, p29). [[Tartaric Acid]]
- **Ascorbic** (vitamin C): antioxidant to slow browning of apple juice and garnishes; 1 tsp per quart of liquid. [[Ascorbic Acid]]
