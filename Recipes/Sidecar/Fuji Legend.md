# Fuji Legend
## Root
[[Sidecar]]

## Ingredients
- 2 oz [[Seltzer]] #balance
- 1 oz [[Chamomile-Infused Blanco Tequila]] (p288) #core
- 1 oz [[Manzanilla Sherry]] #core
- 1 oz fresh [[Fuji Apple Juice]] #balance
- 1/2 oz fresh [[Lemon Juice]] #balance
- 1/2 oz [[House Ginger Syrup]] (p48) #balance
- 1 tsp dark, robust [[Maple Syrup]] #balance
- Garnish: 3 thin apple slices on a skewer #seasoning

## Your Bar
```dataviewjs
await dv.view("Views/recipe-status");
```

## Method
Pour the seltzer into a Collins glass. Short shake the remaining ingredients with ice for about 5 seconds, then strain into the glass. Fill the glass with ice cubes, then garnish with the apple slices.
