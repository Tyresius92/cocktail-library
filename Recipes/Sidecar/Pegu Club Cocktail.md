# Pegu Club Cocktail
## Root
[[Sidecar]]

## Ingredients
- 2 oz London dry gin ([[Gin]]) #core
- 3/4 oz [[Cointreau]] #balance
- 3/4 oz fresh [[Lime Juice]] #balance
- 1 dash [[Angostura Bitters]] #seasoning
- 1 dash [[House Orange Bitters]] #seasoning
- Garnish: 1 [[Lime]] wedge #seasoning

## Your Bar
```dataviewjs
await dv.view("Views/recipe-status");
```

## Method
Shake all the ingredients with ice, then double strain into a chilled coupe. Garnish with the lime wedge.

## Notes
Classic. Highly potent bitters can also adjust the balance. Follows the same template as the book's [[Between the Sheets]]; gin plays a supporting role to Cointreau's juicy sweetness, so a dash each of Angostura and House Orange Bitters (p295) adds seasoning that dries the drink slightly and curbs the lime's sharpness.
