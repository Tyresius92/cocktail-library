# Cognac Sour
## Root
[[Sidecar]]

## Ingredients
- 2 oz [[Cognac]] (preferably VSOP) #core
- 3/4 oz fresh [[Lemon]] juice #balance
- 3/4 oz [[Simple Syrup]] #balance

## Your Bar
```dataviewjs
await dv.view("Views/recipe-status");
```

## Method
Shake all the ingredients with ice, then double strain into a chilled coupe. No garnish.

## Notes
Classic. The first step of the balance experiment (p155): a Basic Sour template ([[Basic Sour]]) built with Cognac. Tasty, but not special: the Cognac is clearly the star but its woodsier notes are muted by the refreshing combination of lemon and sugar. Set it aside to compare, rinse the shaking tin, then make the Sidecar (Boozy), which substitutes Cointreau for the simple syrup.
