# Long Island Iced Tea
## Root
[[Sidecar]]

## Ingredients
- 2 oz cold [[Coca-Cola]] #balance
- 3/4 oz [[Aylesbury Duck vodka]] #core
- 3/4 oz [[Plymouth gin]] #core
- 3/4 oz [[Cimarron Blanco tequila]] #core
- 3/4 oz [[Plantation 3 Stars White]] rum #core
- 3/4 oz [[Cointreau]] #balance
- 3/4 oz fresh [[Lemon Juice]] #balance
- Garnish: 1 [[Lemon]] wedge #seasoning

## Your Bar
```dataviewjs
await dv.view("Views/recipe-status");
```

## Method
Pour the cola into a pint glass. Short shake the remaining ingredients with ice for about 5 seconds, then strain into the glass. Fill the glass with ice cubes and garnish with the lemon wedge.

## Notes
Classic. The infamous member of the Sidecar family: it diverges in the amount of spirit, a whopping 3 oz split among vodka, gin, tequila and rum, but the Cointreau and lemon aren't increased, so the cola adds sugar and acidity to bring it back into balance.
