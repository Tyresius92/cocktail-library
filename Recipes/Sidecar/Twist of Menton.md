# Twist of Menton
## Root
[[Sidecar]]

## Ingredients
- 3/4 oz [[Grey Goose vodka]] #core
- 3/4 oz [[Aperol]] #balance
- 1/2 oz [[Amaro Nonino]] #balance
- 3/4 oz fresh [[Lemon Juice]] #balance
- 1/2 oz [[Strawberry Cream Syrup]] (p286) #balance
- Garnish: 1 [[Strawberry]] slice #seasoning

## Your Bar
```dataviewjs
await dv.view("Views/recipe-status");
```

## Method
Shake all the ingredients with ice, then double strain into a chilled coupe. Garnish with the strawberry slice.

## Notes
Devon Tarby, 2015. Another riff on the [[Last Word]]: two bitter liqueurs replace the Chartreuse and maraschino. A luscious strawberry syrup adds a sweet counterpoint and body.
