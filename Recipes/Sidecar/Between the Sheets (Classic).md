# Between the Sheets (Classic)
## Root
[[Sidecar]]

## Ingredients
- 3/4 oz [[Cognac]] #core
- 3/4 oz [[Rum]] #core
- 3/4 oz [[Cointreau]] #balance
- 3/4 oz fresh [[Lemon Juice]] #balance

## Your Bar
```dataviewjs
await dv.view("Views/recipe-status");
```

## Method
Shake all the ingredients with ice, then strain into a chilled coupe. No garnish.

## Notes
The classic recipe (p168). VS Cognac plus unaged Spanish-style rum is boozy and thin; funky Jamaican rum plus big XO is rich but out of balance. See [[Between the Sheets]] for the fix.
