# Sidecar: Experimenting with the Core

Condensed from pp. 166-168. Swapping blanco tequila and lime into the Sidecar root gives something dry, vegetal and overwhelmingly orangey, so adjust the balance.

## Cocktails
- [[Margarita (Classic)]]: 2 oz tequila, 3/4 oz Cointreau, 3/4 oz lime, 1/4 oz simple syrup
- [[Margarita (Dry)]]: no simple syrup
- [[Margarita (Sweet)]]: 1/2 oz simple syrup
- [[Between the Sheets (Classic)]]: equal parts Cognac, rum, Cointreau, lemon
- [[Between the Sheets]]: the authors' ideal, with gum syrup for body
- [[White Lady]]: gin for Cognac; more gin, less Cointreau, a bit of syrup, plus egg white
