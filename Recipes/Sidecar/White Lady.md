# White Lady
## Root
[[Sidecar]]

## Ingredients
- 2 oz [[Plymouth gin]] #core
- 1/2 oz [[Cointreau]] #balance
- 3/4 oz fresh [[Lemon Juice]] #balance
- 1/4 oz [[Simple Syrup]] #balance
- 1 [[Egg White]] #balance
- Garnish: 1 [[Lemon]] twist #seasoning

```dataviewjs
await dv.view("Views/recipe-status");
```

## Method
Dry shake all the ingredients, then shake again with ice. Double strain into a chilled coupe. Express the lemon twist over the drink, then set it on the edge of the glass.

## Notes
Classic riff on the Sidecar with gin for the Cognac (p169). As with the Margarita, true Sidecar proportions would let the orange liqueur overshadow the gin and give a punishing astringency, so increase the gin, back off the Cointreau and add a little simple syrup. Also shows how egg white affects balance: its froth expands the drink's volume and spreads the flavor through it (egg whites are covered on pp253 and 264).
