# Sidecar: Split Bases and Low-ABV

From pp. 170-171. Rather than a single spirit, split the core: a strong spirit plus pear brandy, or a larger portion of fortified wine with a small measure of spirit. The spirit then works with a flavorful liqueur to balance and season.
