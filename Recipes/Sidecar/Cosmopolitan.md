# Cosmopolitan
## Root
[[Sidecar]]

## Ingredients
- 2 oz citrus vodka ([[Vodka]]) #core
- 3/4 oz [[Cointreau]] #balance
- 1/2 oz fresh [[Lime Juice]] #balance
- 1/2 oz [[Pure unsweetened cranberry juice]] #balance
- 1/2 oz [[Simple Syrup]] #balance
- Garnish: [[Lime]] wheel #seasoning

## Method
Shake all the ingredients with ice, then double strain into a chilled coupe. Garnish with the lime wheel.

## Notes
Our version from Toby Cecchini's original, 1988. The Cosmo is a Sidecar with citrus vodka for the Cognac, lime for the lemon, and a little cranberry juice. It goes wrong with cranberry *cocktail* and too much of it: a sweet mess without complexity or balance. The authors use pure unsweetened cranberry juice (very tannic), balanced with an equal amount of simple syrup, with a little more vodka to punch through the tart sweetness.
