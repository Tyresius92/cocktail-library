# The Sidecar Extended Family (pp. 186-, condensed)

Many members contain a lot of booze, but there is precedent for going the other direction: split bases with fortified wine or aromatized wine.

## Cocktails
- [[Champs-Elysees]]: green Chartreuse for the Cointreau
- [[La Valencia]]: Champs-Elysees riff with manzanilla and chamomile rye
- [[Corpse Reviver No. 2]]: gin + Lillet blanc split base
- [[Four to the Floor]]: pisco + grapefruit liqueur, verjus instead of lemon, stirred
- [[Modern Display]]: Natasha David's amontillado and vanilla Sidecar
- [[Fish House Punch]]: Cognac + rum + peach liqueur, lengthened with seltzer
- [[Chatham Cocktail]]: fortified-wine core topped with Champagne
- [[Twist of Menton]]: Last Word riff with Aperol and Nonino
- [[Long Island Iced Tea]]: four spirits plus cola

## Family map (p187)
The book's map places the root Sidecar (1 1/2 oz Pierre Ferrand Ambre Cognac, 1 oz Pierre Ferrand dry curacao, 3/4 oz lemon, 1 tsp simple syrup) at the center, with four branches:
- **Experimenting with the Core**: [[White Lady]], [[Rational Thought]], [[Peaches and Smoke]], [[Beausoleil]], [[Between the Sheets]], [[Margarita (Classic)]]
- **Experimenting with the Balance and Seasoning**: [[20th Century]], [[Last Word]] ([[Twist of Menton]]), [[Crop Top]]
- **Sidecar Variations**: [[Cosmopolitan]] ([[Clarified Cosmopolitan]]), [[Pegu Club Cocktail]], [[Lily Pad]], [[Why Not]]
- **Sidecar Extended Family**: [[Corpse Reviver No. 2]] (La Valencia, [[Blood and Sand]] ([[Blood and Sand (Ours)]])), Four to the Floor, Champs-Elysees, [[Fish House Punch]], [[Chatham Cocktail]], [[Long Island Iced Tea]]

Note: the map labels Pegu Club Cocktail as "page 174"; the recipe is actually on p184.
