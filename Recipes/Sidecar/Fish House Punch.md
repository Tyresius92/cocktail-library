# Fish House Punch
## Root
[[Sidecar]]

## Ingredients
- 2 oz cold [[Seltzer]] #balance
- 3/4 oz [[Pierre Ferrand Ambre Cognac]] #core
- 3/4 oz [[Appleton Estate Reserve Blend]] rum #core
- 3/4 oz [[Giffard Creme de Peche de Vigne]] #balance
- 3/4 oz fresh [[Lemon Juice]] #balance
- 1/4 oz [[Cane Sugar Syrup]] #balance
- 1 strip [[Lemon]] peel #seasoning
- Garnish: 1 [[Lemon]] wheel and [[Nutmeg]] #seasoning

## Your Bar

## Method
Pour the seltzer into a Collins glass or goblet. Short shake the remaining ingredients with ice for about 5 seconds, then strain into the glass. Fill the glass with ice cubes, then garnish with the lemon wheel and grate some nutmeg over the top.

## Notes
Classic. Clearly related to the Sidecar: Cognac, liqueur and lemon juice in similar proportions. The key difference is seltzer, which increases the perception of acidity (as with the Collins, p138). The classic recipe already has ample richness in the core spirits and sweet peach liqueur, and the syrup bolsters them to increase body.
