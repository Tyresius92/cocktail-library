# Margarita (Dry)
## Root
[[Sidecar]]

## Ingredients
- 2 oz [[Siembra Azul blanco tequila]] #core
- 3/4 oz [[Cointreau]] #balance
- 3/4 oz fresh [[Lime Juice]] #balance
- Garnish: 1 [[Lime]] wedge #seasoning
- Rim: [[Kosher Salt]] #seasoning

## Your Bar
```dataviewjs
await dv.view("Views/recipe-status");
```

## Method
Rub the lime wedge along the upper 1/2 inch of a double Old-Fashioned glass, halfway around, and roll the rim in kosher salt. Add 1 large ice cube. Shake the remaining ingredients with ice and strain into the glass. No garnish.
