# Crop Top
## Root

## Ingredients
- 3/4 oz [[Beefeater 24 gin]] #core
- 3/4 oz [[Amaro Montenegro]] #core
- 3/4 oz [[Giffard Creme de Pamplemousse]] #balance
- 3/4 oz fresh [[Lemon Juice]] #balance

## Method
Shake all the ingredients with ice, then strain into a chilled Nick & Nora glass. No garnish.

## Notes
Devon Tarby, 2013. A modified [[Last Word]]: a serious punch made subtler and lighter by using an amaro as part of the base, significantly more refreshing than the dense original. The grapefruit-laden pamplemousse plus the cinnamon-spice of Montenegro was directly inspired by [[Donn's Mix No. 1]] (grapefruit juice and cinnamon syrup, a classic tiki syrup).
