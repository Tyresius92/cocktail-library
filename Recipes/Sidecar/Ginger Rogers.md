# Ginger Rogers
## Root
[[Sidecar]]

## Ingredients
- 1 oz [[Gosling's Black Seal rum]] #core
- 1 oz [[Hine H Cognac]] #core
- 3/4 oz fresh [[Lemon]] juice #balance
- 3/4 oz [[House Ginger Syrup]] (p48) #balance
- 1/2 oz [[Simple Syrup]] (p45) #balance
- 1 dash [[Peychaud's Bitters]] #seasoning


## Method
Shake all the ingredients with ice, then strain into a chilled coupe. No garnish.
