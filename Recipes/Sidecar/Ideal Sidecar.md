# Ideal Sidecar
## Root
[[Sidecar]]

## Ingredients
- 1 1/2 oz [[Pierre Ferrand Ambre Cognac]] #core
- 1 oz [[Pierre Ferrand dry curacao]] #balance
- 3/4 oz fresh [[Lemon Juice]] #balance
- 1 tsp [[Simple Syrup]] #balance
- Garnish: 1 [[Orange]] twist #seasoning

## Method
Shake all the ingredients with ice, then strain into a chilled coupe. Express the orange twist over the drink, then gently rub it around the rim of the glass and place it into the drink.

## Notes
"Our Ideal Sidecar", the book's root recipe (p152). Pierre Ferrand's Ambre Cognac spends enough time in oak to give a rich, flavorful foundation, beautifully complemented by that producer's extremely dry curacao. Because the dryness of the curacao is so bracing, a bit of simple syrup boosts the flavors and brings the cocktail into harmony.
