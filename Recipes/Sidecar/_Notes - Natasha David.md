# Friends and Family: Natasha David (p185, condensed)

Co-owner of the Manhattan bar Nitecap; runs the bar-consulting company You and Me Cocktails with her partner Jeremy Oertel. She started bartending with no concept of balance, dumping brandy, triple sec and sour mix into sugar-rimmed glasses, until her bosses at the speakeasy Woodson and Ford (Jim Kearns and Lynette Marrero) showed her a real Sidecar: good brandy, Cointreau, fresh lemon juice. Her Sidecar thinking: the template is versatile; vary it by splitting the modifiers (something sweet like a flavored syrup, something dry like sherry); skip sugar rims unless a guest asks; the Sidecar lets ingredients shine, so every ingredient's quality matters.
