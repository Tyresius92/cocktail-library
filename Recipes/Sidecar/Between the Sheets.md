# Between the Sheets
## Root
[[Sidecar]]

## Ingredients
- 1 oz [[Paul Beau VS]] Cognac #core
- 1 oz [[Appleton Estate Reserve Blend]] rum #core
- 3/4 oz [[Pierre Ferrand dry curacao]] #balance
- 3/4 oz fresh [[Lemon Juice]] #balance
- 1 tsp [[Demerara Gum Syrup]] #balance

## Your Bar
```dataviewjs
await dv.view("Views/recipe-status");
```

## Method
Shake all the ingredients with ice, then strain into a chilled coupe. No garnish.

## Notes
The authors' ideal recipe (p168). The gum syrup (p54) gives body because the curacao is so dry.
