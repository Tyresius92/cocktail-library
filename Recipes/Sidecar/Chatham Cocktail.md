# Chatham Cocktail
## Root
[[Sidecar]]

## Ingredients
- 3/4 oz [[Cocchi Americano Bianco]] #core
- 3/4 oz [[Lustau Puerto fino sherry]] #core
- 1/4 oz [[Grand Marnier]] #balance
- 1/2 oz fresh [[Lemon]] juice #balance
- 1/4 oz [[Simple Syrup]] #balance
- Pinch of salt #seasoning
- 1 1/2 oz [[Dry Champagne]] #balance
- Garnish: 1 [[Grapefruit]] twist #seasoning

## Your Bar
```dataviewjs
await dv.view("Views/recipe-status");
```

## Method
Short shake all the ingredients except the Champagne with ice for about 5 seconds, then strain into a chilled flute. Pour in the Champagne and quickly dip the barspoon into the glass to gently mix. Express the grapefruit twist over the drink and then place it into the drink.

## Notes
Devon Tarby, 2015. The core is a blend of fortified wines, which bring more sugar and acidity, and the drink is topped with Champagne, which adds even more acidity. So a smaller amount of both Grand Marnier and lemon juice is used and the simple syrup is boosted.
