## Notes
The book calls for dark, robust maple syrup.

Real maple syrup is expensive but there's no substitute. Favorite: "Grade A Dark Color and Robust Flavor"; lighter styles get washed out in cocktails. Pairs with whiskey, apple brandy and rum (especially aged molasses rum), herbs like rosemary and sage, and spices like nutmeg: anything fall/winter and warm. Used as is, no manipulation.
