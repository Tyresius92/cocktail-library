# Cocchi Americano Bianco

**Category:** Aromatized wine

## Notes
Similar to Lillet blanc (slightly bitter, orangey) but with a good deal more cinchona bitterness, so used when more structure is needed to balance other big flavors (Little Victory, p77, uses a root beer infusion of it).
