# Giffard Creme de Violette

**Category:** Violet liqueur

## Notes
Deeply purple with a vibrant violet aroma and just the right sweetness: sweet, but the sugar doesn't dumb down the floral violets (p175). Crème Yvette is higher in proof and dosed with other flavors, so the two aren't interchangeable without adjusting sweetness or base.
