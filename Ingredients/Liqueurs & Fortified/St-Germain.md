# St-Germain

**Category:** Elderflower liqueur

## Notes
The elderflower liqueur that launched in 2007 and started the trend; "bartender's ketchup". Sweet floral aroma, full-bodied like a dessert wine; extremely versatile. In tiny amounts adds juiciness without noticeable sweetness (Night Light, p282); in larger amounts can be one of the base spirits if balanced with acid (Normandie Club Spritz No. 3, p225) (p175).
