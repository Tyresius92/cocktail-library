# Giffard Menthe-Pastille

**Category:** Liqueur

## Notes
Try it the French way, over crushed ice, or in classic creme de menthe cocktails; add a bit to drinks with fresh mint (Salvation Julep, p283) or a tiny bit to herbal-liqueur drinks to amplify them (Greatest Dancer, p281) (p175).
