# Strega

**Category:** Herbal liqueur

## Notes
Yellow from saffron, one of over seventy flavorings: savory with light anise notes. Used like yellow Chartreuse but more challenging; typically 1 tsp in a stirred cocktail or alongside simple syrup in a sour (p176).
