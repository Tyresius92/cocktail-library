## Notes
Bittersweet orange aperitif made with the classic Amer Picon recipe in mind; used in place of modern, less bitter Amer Picon.

Book (p260, medium amaro): between an amaro and a curacao; balances citrus, spice and lingering bitterness with mandarin orange character. Used in small amounts (1 tsp to 1/4 oz) to add structure and lift dense spirits such as Cognac. [[_Notes - Amaro]]
