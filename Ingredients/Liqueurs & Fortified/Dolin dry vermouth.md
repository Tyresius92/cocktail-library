## Notes
*Tasting notes, substitution suggestions, where to buy.*

If the book could only stock one dry vermouth, this would be it: extremely dry and clean, a benchmark and the Martini's best buddy. Subtle alpine herbs and a slight bitterness; made in Chambery, France since 1821. To see it shine: a fifty-fifty Martini of Plymouth gin and Dolin dry, a dash of orange bitters and a lemon twist.
