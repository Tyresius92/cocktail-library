# Cinzano Rosso

**Category:** Sweet vermouth

## Notes
Shares its Torino heritage with other sweet vermouths but less aggressive than Carpano Antica Formula, so more versatile. Not the best Manhattan, but a great companion to gin in a classic Martinez, or gin and Campari in a Negroni.
