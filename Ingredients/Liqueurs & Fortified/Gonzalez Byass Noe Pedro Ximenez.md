# Gonzalez Byass Noe Pedro Ximenez

**Category:** Sherry

## Notes
Average age about 30 years; extremely concentrated and viscous (raisins, figs, cinnamon, anise). Too expensive to mix freely, but 1 tsp goes a long way, e.g. in place of the sweetener in a rum Old-Fashioned (p248). See [[_Notes - Sherry]].
