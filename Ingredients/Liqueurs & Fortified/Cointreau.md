# Cointreau

**Category:** Liqueur

## Notes
Orange liqueur, about 80 proof. Medium sweetness, between bone-dry Pierre Ferrand dry curacao and sweet Grand Marnier (book p154-155). Because it is as boozy as most base spirits, it must be accounted for in the proportions of a Sidecar.

The most iconic of the orange curacaos: clean and versatile, a favorite in shaken drinks like Sidecars and Margaritas. Relatively dry, so it also works well in stirred cocktails like the Nurse Hazel (p282) (p172).
