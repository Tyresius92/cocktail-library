# Dolin Rouge

**Category:** Sweet vermouth

## Notes
A sweet French vermouth, much lighter and softer than the Italian style: useful with spirits that need coddling (e.g. a Manhattan with wheated bourbon such as Maker's Mark, which a sturdy red vermouth would overwhelm), and with Cognac, Calvados and some aged rums.
