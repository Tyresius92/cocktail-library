# Gonzalez Byass Matusalem oloroso sherry

**Category:** Sherry

## Notes
Deep, complex oloroso sherry chosen as the core of the book's [[Ideal Flip]] (p242).

Average age about 30 years in a solera. Probably the priciest sherry the book recommends for cocktails, but worth it: technically a cream sherry (mostly Palomino with some Pedro Ximenez). Quite sweet yet intensely complex: dried fruit, coffee, cacao, soft acidity (p249). See [[_Notes - Sherry]].
