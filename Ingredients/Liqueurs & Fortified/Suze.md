# Suze

**Category:** Gentian liqueur

## Notes
*Tasting notes, substitution suggestions, where to buy.*

Book (p259): think of it as French Campari. Earthy, sweet, citrusy, light body, bright yellow, 20% ABV; mild enough for low-alcohol citrusy drinks and strong enough (gentian root) for Martini variations like the White Negroni (p89). [[_Notes - Amaro]]
