# Lustau East India solera sherry

**Category:** Fortified wine

## Notes
Raisiny sherry.

Book (p249): mostly oloroso with a little PX, aged 3 years in a warmer part of the bodega to mimic old long sea voyages; woodsy bite and structure. A great sweet-vermouth stand-in, especially against Aperol and Campari. [[_Notes - Sherry]]
