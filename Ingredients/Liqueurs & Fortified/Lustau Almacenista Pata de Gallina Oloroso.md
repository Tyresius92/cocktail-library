# Lustau Almacenista Pata de Gallina Oloroso

**Category:** Sherry

## Notes
Step up from Lustau's entry-level Don Nuno oloroso (around $25): bone-dry finish with a broad, chocolatey, spicy body, not sweet (p248). "Almacenista" = sherry from small family-owned soleras distributed by larger houses. See [[_Notes - Sherry]].
