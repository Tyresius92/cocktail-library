# Lustau San Emilio Pedro Ximenez

**Category:** Sherry

## Notes
Rich, sweet, full-bodied PX (raisins, figs, nuts, molasses, baking spices) with vibrant acidity. Acts like a fruit liqueur; pairs with lemon juice; used in about 1/2 oz doses with a syrup making up the difference (p249). See [[_Notes - Sherry]].
