# Chareau California Aloe Liqueur

**Category:** Herbal liqueur

## Notes
Introduced in 2014, on an unaged brandy backbone: layers of aloe, cucumber, lemon peel, muskmelon and spearmint. Aloe dominates at first but lingers into a refreshing herbal complexity; surprisingly dry. Used more like a spirit, adding a bit more sugar when substituting for another liqueur, as in the Lily Pad (p184) (p176).
