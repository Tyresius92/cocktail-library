## Notes
Made by Carthusian monks at the base of the French Alps. Yellow (80 proof) can be used in larger quantities, up to 1/2 oz; green (110 proof) quickly dominates, so use measured doses, with exceptions such as the Bijou (p89) that rely on a large dose balanced by Campari (p176).
