# Carpano Antica Formula vermouth

**Category:** 

## Notes
*Tasting notes, substitution suggestions, where to buy.*

Said to reproduce Antonio Benedetto Carpano's 1786 recipe, though the brand dates from the 1990s; a standard for Torino-style vermouth. Big, bitter, a huge vanilla kick: great with aged spirits (a luscious counterpoint to rye in a Manhattan), but a rambunctious guest with delicate spirits like gin, vodka and tequila.
