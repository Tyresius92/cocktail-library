# Grand Marnier

**Category:** Liqueur

## Notes
A foundation of aged Cognac gives this orange liqueur greater depth, though it can limit its usefulness in cocktails. The authors like mixing it with rum in classics like the Mai Tai (p136) and the Dark Horse (p280) (p172).
