# Martini & Rossi Bianco

**Category:** Bianco vermouth

## Notes
Favorite among the big-house biancos and easier to find than Dolin blanc or La Quintinye; exerts its personality a bit more than Dolin blanc, which makes it a good companion for blanco tequila, mezcal and gin.
