## Notes
Slightly bitter from cinchona bark and gentian root; tastes like a bitter amontillado sherry with raisin on the nose, though dry on the palate. Pairs well with aged rum (Manhattan-style or with citrus). Astringency can be problematic in sours, especially with lime.
