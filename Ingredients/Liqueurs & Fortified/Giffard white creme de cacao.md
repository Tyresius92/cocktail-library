# Giffard white creme de cacao

**Category:** Liqueur

## Notes
Made from cacao beans and a small amount of vanilla: a high-quality, reliable option. The authors are very careful with it: more than 3/4 oz and a drink tastes like powdered chocolate milk. White is preferred over dark, which is often colored with caramel (p177).
