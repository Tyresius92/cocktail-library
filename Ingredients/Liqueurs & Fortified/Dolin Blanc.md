# Dolin Blanc

**Category:** Blanc vermouth

## Notes
The bottle that made the book fall in love with the style. Versatile: Martini variations with every base spirit (tequila, mezcal, gin, rum, whiskey, brandy), an herbaceous split base in sours, spritzes and low-ABV drinks; especially good paired with eaux-de-vie.
