# Seltzer

**Category:** Other

## Notes
Simply filtered water that's been carbonated: no additives, so neutral and consistent. The authors prefer it in cocktails and highly recommend it for Highballs, since its clean flavor won't appreciably alter the core spirit (p211). Serve well chilled to preserve bubbles. See [[_Notes - Sparkling Water]].
