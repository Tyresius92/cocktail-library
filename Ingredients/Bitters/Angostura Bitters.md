# Angostura Bitters

**Category:** [[Aromatic Bitters]], [[Bitters]]

## Notes
The world's most widely available bitters and the most common aromatic type: a dense sweet base, a bitter backbone (gentian), and warming spice (clove, cinnamon). Made in Trinidad to a closely guarded recipe; on its own it tastes like a rum-fueled Christmas party. A flavoring bitters. Many people prefer an Old-Fashioned with both Angostura and orange bitters.
