# Gosling's Black Seal

**Category:** [[Rum]]

## Notes
Bermuda. A Dark and Stormy isn't one without its dark molasses flavor. Dense in color, surprisingly drinkable alone; an undeniable banana flavor and some spice that works well alongside another spirit (Ginger Rogers, p280).
