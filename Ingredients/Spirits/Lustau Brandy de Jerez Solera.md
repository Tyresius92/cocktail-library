## Notes
The bodega responsible for some of the authors' favorite sherries also makes some of the best-priced Brandy de Jerez. The reserva is aged around three years in former amontillado casks: dense aromatics but dry, with vanilla, toasted nuts and fresh nutmeg; full-bodied enough for shaken and stirred cocktails. The gran reserva (more than ten years, part of it in oloroso casks) is mature, with walnut and toffee flavors and a lingering raisin finish; the authors love Manhattan variations made with it (p158).
