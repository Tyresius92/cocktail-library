# Bushmills Original Irish whiskey

**Category:** [[Whiskey]]

## Notes
Aged five years in used American oak; a lighter style than other Bushmills, with clarity that suits citrusy cocktails. For Manhattans and Old-Fashioned-style drinks the authors recommend Black Bush (a high proportion of oloroso-aged whiskey) or the Bushmills single malts (p206).
