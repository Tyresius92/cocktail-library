# Cynar

**Category:** [[Amaro]]

## Notes
Medium amaro (p260): reputation for bitterness, but between CioCiaro and the dense amari. Vegetal, herbaceous, earthy nose (artichoke is just one of many botanicals), eucalyptus flavor, long bitter finish that stretches a cocktail's flavors. See [[_Notes - Amaro]].
