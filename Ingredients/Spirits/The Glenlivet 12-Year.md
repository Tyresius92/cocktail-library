# The Glenlivet 12-Year

**Category:** [[Whiskey]]

## Notes
Highlands (Speyside). Elegant and floral with a touch of honey; massively produced and consistently delicious if not terribly complex. That lack of complexity makes it a useful single malt for mixing, since it won't aggressively overpower other flavors (p204).
