# Zacapa 23

**Category:** [[Rum]]

## Notes
Guatemala. A blend of rums aged six to twenty-five years in a solera system (p245): deeply complex, great for sipping and as the base for spirituous cocktails (Old-Fashioned or Manhattan riffs). Aged in former whiskey barrels, so it has an affinity for American whiskey, especially rye.
