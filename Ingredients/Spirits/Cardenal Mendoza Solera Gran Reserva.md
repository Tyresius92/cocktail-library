## Notes
Aged fifteen to seventeen years in former oloroso and Pedro Ximenez sherry casks: onslaught of prunes and raisins, sweet nutty chocolate flavor that lasts. Dense, so typically used in a split base in a spirituous cocktail, such as alongside American whiskey in an Old-Fashioned (p158).
