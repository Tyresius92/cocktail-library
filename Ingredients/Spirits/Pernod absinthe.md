## Notes
Absinthe is much like bitters: a highly concentrated flavoring used in small amounts for a bright herbal punch, or as a glass rinse that perfumes the drink (the defining Sazerac technique). Rarely more than 1/4 oz in a drink or it bullies the core. The book prefers clean Swiss or French styles; favorite bottlings from Pernod, Emile Pernot and St. George.
