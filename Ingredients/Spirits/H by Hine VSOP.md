## Notes
Hine created the "H" bottling for demand for cocktail-friendly Cognacs: affordable and with a strong personality. Strong floral aroma, fresh stone fruit flavors, a little spice. Its elegance can get lost mixed with assertive spirits, but it's a delicious solo base and a great complement to light fortified wines (fino and amontillado sherries) and aromatized wines like Lillet blanc (p157).
