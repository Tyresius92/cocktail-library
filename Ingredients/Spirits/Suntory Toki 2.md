## Notes
Blend of whiskies from Suntory's three distilleries (Yamazaki, Hakushu, Chita): smooth and light, usually around $45, an affordable gentle introduction to Japanese whisky. Balances the refinement of a scotch-style single malt with some of the grit and spice of grain whisky (p207).
