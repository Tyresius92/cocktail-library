## Notes
Produced from West Virginia apples and distilled in Virginia; the benchmark American apple brandy. Highly potent and spicy, it dominates a cocktail if not kept in check. In sours pair it with strong flavors, as in the Jack Rose; elsewhere use it as a modifier to add an unexpected dimension, as in the Pink Lady (p161).
