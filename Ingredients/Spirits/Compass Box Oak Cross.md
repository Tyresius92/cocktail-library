# Compass Box Oak Cross

**Category:** [[Whiskey]]

## Notes
Blended scotch: three single malts combined and aged six months in both American and French casks. Dense, with malty vanilla and baking spices; strong enough to be known even among bold vermouth, as in bitter Manhattan-style drinks such as the Affinity (p278) (p205).
