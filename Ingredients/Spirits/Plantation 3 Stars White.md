# Plantation 3 Stars White

**Category:** [[Rum]]

## Notes
Jamaica, Barbados and Trinidad blend that performs very much like a Spanish-style rum. Shines in Daiquiris and streamlined sours; fairly mild, so too many strong ingredients (potent liqueurs, amari) can bury its character.
