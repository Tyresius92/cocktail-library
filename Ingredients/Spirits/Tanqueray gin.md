## Notes
On the market since the 1830s, the first of what is now called London dry. US version is 47.3% ABV. Highly juniper-dominant: the book's greatest asset in spirituous and bitter cocktails (the Negroni and its variations) but often too assertive for refreshing, citrus-heavy drinks.
