# Capurro Pisco Acholado

**Category:** Pisco

## Notes
All the iconic aromas and flavors of pisco (bright stone fruits and flowers) with something uniquely earthy that lets it shine in stirred cocktails, especially Martini riffs with blanc vermouth. Made from a blend of grapes (acholado); Capurro's single-grape bottlings are stunning too (p159).
