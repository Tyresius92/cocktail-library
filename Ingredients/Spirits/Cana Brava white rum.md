# Cana Brava white rum

**Category:** [[Rum]]

## Notes
Light Spanish-style white rum.

Panama. Created specifically for cocktail use by the 86 Co.; an homage to old-style Cuban rums like Havana Club (the US-market Havana Club is not the same thing). Straw-yellow, with subtle coconut, banana and a hint of vanilla. A must-have for Daiquiris and variations, most deliciously the Mojito.
