# Domaine d'Esperance Blanche Armagnac

**Category:** Armagnac

## Notes
Blanche (unaged) Armagnac is a great substitute for other unaged grape brandies. Whereas pisco is intensely floral, the high acidity of Armagnac's base wine gives a more savory aroma and flavor of almonds and white pepper, with lively freshness (p158).
