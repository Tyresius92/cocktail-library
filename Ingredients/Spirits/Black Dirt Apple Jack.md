## Notes
Made in New York State in limited quantities; inspired by Laird's but forges its own path. Made entirely from apples and aged at least four years (often five to six) in newly charred oak barrels: many of Laird's spicy aged characteristics, but more apple presence and a soft, not overly aggressive flavor (p161).
