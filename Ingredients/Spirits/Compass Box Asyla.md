# Compass Box Asyla

**Category:** [[Whiskey]]

## Notes
Blended scotch. All elegance: fruity core flavor topped with spice notes, but gentler and more reminiscent of fresh apple than cooked. Light-bodied alone, but shines in cocktails, especially Manhattan riffs such as the Bobby Burns (p279) (p205).
