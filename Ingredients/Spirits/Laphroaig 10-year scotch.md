# Laphroaig 10-year scotch

**Category:** Scotch

## Notes
Peaty Islay scotch.

Islay. Intensely smoky and briny; a few drops can flavor a cocktail. Also complex spice flavors of black pepper, cardamom and vanilla, and a lingering herbal note that makes it a great companion for mint, as in Smoke and Mirrors (p131) and Smokescreen (p132) (p205).
