# Pierre Ferrand 1840 Cognac

**Category:** Cognac

## Notes
Relatively new, created specifically for mixing. Pierre Ferrand doesn't put age statements on it, but the 1840 and the Ambre both mix like a VSOP. Powerful baking-spice aroma that extends into the flavor, and bottled at 45% ABV rather than the usual 40%, so it takes center stage and can collaborate with aggressive spirits like American rye without getting bullied (p157).
