# Tariquet VSOP Bas-Armagnac

**Category:** Armagnac

## Notes
More structure and complexity with more time in oak. Compared to the slightly wild younger bottle it is more rounded, with vanilla and cooked stone fruits; its fuller body complements the toasted spices, making it a tasty addition to stirred cocktails (p158).
