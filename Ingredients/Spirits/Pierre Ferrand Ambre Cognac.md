# Pierre Ferrand Ambre Cognac

**Category:** Cognac

## Notes
The authors' go-to Cognac for cocktails. Sweet vanilla and apricot nose, deep and complex flavor with clear oak influence and a fresh-fruit quality like biting into a sweet apple. Great in shaken and stirred cocktails, alone or with other spirits, particularly aged rum, apple brandy and bourbon (p157). Used in the [[Ideal Sidecar]].
