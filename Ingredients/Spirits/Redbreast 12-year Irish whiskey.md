## Notes
Epitomizes traditional pot still Irish whiskey: rich and almost oily (unmalted and malted barley mash in copper pot stills), an almost coconut-like quality and slight anise. Blended from distillates aged in American oak and sherry casks, with a balance between spice and the raisin-like finish of sherry. May get lost in a sour-style drink; shines as the base of spirituous cocktails such as the Cut and Paste (p35), where it mingles with aged apple brandy for a juicy Sazerac riff (p206).
