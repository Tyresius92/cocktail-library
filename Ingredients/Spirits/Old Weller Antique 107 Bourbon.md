## Notes
Pappy Van Winkle gets all the attention these days, but we love its cousin Old Weller (bother are made from the same mashbill at the Buffalo Trace Distillery). The Old Weller is a highly accessible, wheat-forward bourbon. Although wheat can sometimes make bourbon too soft for mixing in cocktails, allowing it to be overpowered by other ingredients, the high proof of this bottling means it will still shine through in boozy cocktails like an Old-Fashioned or a Manhattan. And if you use it in a sour-style cocktail, the wheat sings when balanced with citrus.
