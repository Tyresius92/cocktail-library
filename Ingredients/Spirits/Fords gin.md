# Fords gin

**Category:** Gin

## Notes
Named after Simon Ford (formerly of Plymouth and Beefeater). Falls between Plymouth and Beefeater in flavor and proof; created for use in all styles of cocktail: citrusy, boozy and bitter. Extremely versatile.
