# Absolut Elyx vodka

**Category:** Vodka

## Notes
Wheat-based; smooth, slightly sweet and soft, giving a Martini softness and rich body.

Absolut's single-estate vodka: wheat from a single Swedish estate and a specific type of copper pot still, exploring terroir in a category that lacks diversity. Rich and expensive, but the book can't swap it for another brand: its richness, creaminess and depth of flavor become a defining characteristic of any cocktail it touches.
