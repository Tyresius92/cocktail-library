## Notes
Our benchmark whiskey for Old-Fashioneds is this diplomat, which works equally well across the aisles of shaken and stirred cocktails. It balances the sweetness of corn with a healthy punch of rye and is aged long enough to stand up to a wide range of other ingredients. This is classic bourbon that tastes mature but is affordable enough for everyday cocktail making.
