## Notes
William & Humbert's brandy was for many years the only widely available Spanish brandy in the US, so it set expectations for the category. Rich and sweet on the nose, semidry but with the iconic raisiny flavor of oloroso-aged brandy. Delicious sipping brandy that also works in a split base for a stirred cocktail (p158).
