## Notes
A 12-year Japanese whisky that's soft and elegant with only a whisper of smokiness; the base of the book's [[Ideal Whisky Highball]] because it doesn't overpower the light tingle of sparkling water (p200).

Most similar to single-malt scotch, but far more than a Japanese rendition: a beautiful example of Japanese whisky's unique voice. The distillery sits 2,600 feet above sea level in a remote, forested location; the soft local water, mild peatiness from pot distillation and aging in bourbon and sherry barrels give a mildly fruity and floral whisky. The authors mix finer Japanese whiskies into Highballs too, but Hakushu's elegance makes an ideal benchmark Highball (p207).
