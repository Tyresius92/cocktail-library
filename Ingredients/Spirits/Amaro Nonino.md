# Amaro Nonino

**Category:** [[Amaro]]

## Notes
*Tasting notes, substitution suggestions, where to buy.*

Book (p259, light amaro): top-tier favorite; grappa base gives a deeper backbone; fresh orange oil, herbs and birch; 35% ABV. Great in a split base for Manhattan-style drinks (La Vina, p281) and as the base in Old-Fashioned riffs (Exit Strategy, p11). [[_Notes - Amaro]]
