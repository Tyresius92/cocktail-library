# Beefeater London Dry gin

**Category:** Gin

## Notes
The bottling exported to the US is 47% ABV: enough proof to cut through amari, citrus and fruit syrups, but delicate enough not to overpower the vermouth in a Martini. If you could stock only one gin, probably this.
