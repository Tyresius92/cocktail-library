# Highland Park 12-year scotch

**Category:** Scotch

## Notes
Orkney Islands. Possibly the authors' favorite go-to sipping single malt, for its elegance and affordable price. Sea influence lingers beneath a mildly smoky, slightly sweet flavor; aged primarily in former sherry barrels, lending a soft apricot flavor (p204).
