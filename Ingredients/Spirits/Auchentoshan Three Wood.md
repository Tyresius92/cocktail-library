## Notes
Lowlands. Triple distilled (rather than twice) and aged in three types of oak: former bourbon barrels for up to twelve years, then oloroso sherry barrels for one year, then Pedro Ximenez barrels for one year. Dynamic, multilayered scotch with flavors from each barrel in impressive balance (p204). Limited availability, so rarely mixed.
