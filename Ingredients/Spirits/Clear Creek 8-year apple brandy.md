# Clear Creek 8-Year Apple Brandy

**Category:** Brandy

## Notes
Made from Golden Delicious apples, distilled in a copper pot still, aged at least eight years in used and new French oak. Old soul of a French Calvados with the personality of the Pacific Northwest: bright, full-bodied apple aroma, oak-concentrated flavors with vanilla and slight woodsy notes, semisweet smooth finish. Adored for all cocktails, particularly as an accent to other aged spirits like pure pot still Irish whiskey, as in the Cut and Paste (p161).
