## Notes
Orange-flavored amaro.

Book (p260, medium amaro): 30% ABV; woody birch, cooked citrus and herbs; highly bitter, sweet and earthy yet drying. Great in Manhattan-style drinks (Beth's Going to Town, p87, where it replaces Angostura and some vermouth) and Old-Fashioneds (Pop Quiz, p17, replacing most of the sweetener). [[_Notes - Amaro]]
