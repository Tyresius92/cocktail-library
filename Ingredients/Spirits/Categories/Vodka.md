# Vodka

Distilled from any fermentable plant or fruit, almost always to about 95% ABV and then diluted with water to 40%, which strips away much of the potential flavor and leaves only the essence. Not flavorless: grain bases are subtle but predictable (wheat soft and sweet, rye spicy and peppery, corn rich and slightly sweet), potato is earthy and almost beet-like, grape is floral. In most classic templates it is overshadowed, but the subtle differences show up as softness and body, qualities more sensation than flavor. The Martini is the best way to study these subtleties, far better than citrusy cocktails; taste vodkas side by side.

The book's take: vodka's character, clarity and focus are useful for specific results, and it is liked for its honesty. The 1990s "-tinis" sullied the Martini's reputation but also popularized cocktails.
