# Cognac

Grape brandy from southwestern France: wine distilled in alembic stills and aged in oak. Blending matters most: each house keeps a consistent style year to year. Age grades, youngest to oldest: VS, VSOP, XO; *hors d'age* is officially XO-equivalent but used by producers for bottles beyond XO. The authors mostly use VS and VSOP because Cognac is pricey; VSOP is full-bodied and versatile, VS is woodsy and best in citrusy drinks like the Sidecar. See [[_Notes - Brandy]].
