# Tequila

Agave spirit made only from blue agave; the authors use only 100% agave bottles (mixto is inferior). Tequila is a type of mezcal. See [[_Notes - Agave Spirits]].

- **Blanco**: lightest, bright and vegetal; good in citrusy drinks like the Margarita.
- **Reposado**: 2 months to 1 year in barrels; clove, cinnamon, vanilla, slight sweetness; versatile.
- **Anejo**: 1-3 years; pricey, reserved for the occasional Manhattan variation.
- **Extra anejo**: 3+ years; too expensive for mixing.
