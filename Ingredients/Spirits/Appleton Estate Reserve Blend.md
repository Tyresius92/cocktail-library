## Notes
Jamaica. Less pungent than other Jamaican rums, almost elegant: rich molasses with a grassy finish; a blend of twenty aged rums, with a bright, fresh, citrusy character (tastes like fresh fruit, not stewed banana). The book's most-used Jamaican rum, as a base in shaken and stirred drinks or with other rums, brandy, whiskey or blended scotch.
