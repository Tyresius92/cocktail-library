# Aviation gin

**Category:** Gin

## Notes
Made from a malty rye spirit; pulls back on juniper and features sarsaparilla (root beer impression) and lavender. Full-bodied and flavorful at 42% ABV; also works for seasoning cocktails (Beth's Going to Town, p87).
