## Notes
Alsatian brandies have a clarity and sharpness like bone-dry Alsatian Rieslings; many connoisseurs regard Alsatian eaux-de-vie as the finest. Massenez makes a wide range of eaux-de-vie and liqueurs; their Poire Williams (the Bartlett pear in the US, a juicy variety) is one of the authors' favorites (p162).
