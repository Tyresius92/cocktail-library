# Busnel Pays d'Auge VSOP Calvados

**Category:** Apple brandy

## Notes
For the authors the aroma and flavor of Busnel's VSOP have become inseparable from Calvados in general. Sweet and spiced aroma, baked apples with cinnamon, clove and vanilla, with an off-dry flavor of butter, toffee and a bit of caramel. Works as the core in stirred and shaken drinks, or in small quantities for seasoning (p160).
