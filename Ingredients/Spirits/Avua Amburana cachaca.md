# Avua Amburana cachaca

**Category:** Cachaca

## Notes
Adds chocolate and cinnamon notes.

Aged two years in Latin American amburana wood. One of the book's secret weapons for Old-Fashioned and Manhattan variations: pronounced cinnamon spiciness from the wood and a vanilla-heavy base for a broad, sweet substructure. "Liquid French toast." Best in small quantity as a modifier; as the core it can overwhelm, so use a split base with at least one other spirit.
