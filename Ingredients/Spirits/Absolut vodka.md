# Absolut vodka

**Category:** Vodka

## Notes
Distilled from winter wheat grown in southern Sweden; a softness that suits many applications, especially citrus drinks, but also savory ones like a Bloody Mary. The book likes a Martini of equal parts Absolut and Dolin dry vermouth with a dash of orange bitters and a lemon twist.
